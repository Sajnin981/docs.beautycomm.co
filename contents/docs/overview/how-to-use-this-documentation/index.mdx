This documentation is organized by what you want to do, so you can find answers without reading everything.

## Purpose

Show you the fastest way to find the guide you need.

## Who can use it

Everyone. No account is required to read the documentation.

## Where to find it

You are already here. The sidebar on the left lists every section.

## Before you begin

- Know whether you use the web application, the Admin Panel or the mobile app.

## Steps

1. Use the **search box** on the home page or press the search shortcut in the header to search every guide.
2. Browse the **sidebar**. Select a section heading to open it, then pick a guide.
3. Use the **On this page** list on the right to jump to a part of a long guide.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom of a page to read guides in order.
5. Check **Changelog** for recent updates and **Roadmap** to see what is coming.

## Expected result

You land on the guide that answers your question.

## Notes

- Guides marked as draft are still being verified. They are safe to read but may be less detailed.
- Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| The sidebar is too long | Collapse sections you do not need by selecting their title. |
