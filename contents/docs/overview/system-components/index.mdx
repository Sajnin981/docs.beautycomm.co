## Landing Page

The BeautyComm landing page introduces the platform and its services. Visit [beautycomm.co](https://beautycomm.co) for BeautyComm company information.

## Customer Storefront

Each business has its own customer-facing storefront. Shoppers use the business-provided address to browse products, place orders, and manage their customer account.

## Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard is the business workspace for managing products, orders, customers, stock, marketing, and store settings. Business owners and authorized staff see features according to their assigned roles.

## Mobile App

Customers may be able to shop using an Android mobile app provided for their store. Use the official download link shared by the business and check that it is the correct store app before signing in.
