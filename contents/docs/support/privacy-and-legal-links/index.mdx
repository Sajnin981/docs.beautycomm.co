Use the privacy and legal links provided by your organization or BeautyComm team to understand data handling, account usage, and business compliance requirements.

## Common items to review

Depending on your business setup, these may include:

- privacy policy
- terms of use
- data handling and retention guidance
- security and compliance notices
- support or contact information related to legal matters

## When to review these links

Review these materials when you:

- need to understand how account data is handled
- are configuring account permissions or access settings
- need information about business policy or compliance requirements
- want to confirm the official support and legal contact information for your organization

## Important reminder

If your company has internal privacy or policy guidance, use that documentation as the primary source for local rules and obligations.
