Support depends on whether you are a **shopper** or a **BeautyComm business client**. Shoppers should contact the store where they placed an order. Business owners and staff should use the BeautyComm support channel assigned to their business. This section explains what information to provide and how to send documentation feedback.

## How to Contact BeautyComm Support

**Business clients:** use the official BeautyComm support channel assigned to your business when the admin workspace or a platform feature is blocked.

**Shoppers:** for an order, payment, delivery, return, product, or customer-account issue, contact the store using the support details on its website or order confirmation. The store handles its own customer orders and policies.

## What Information to Provide When Reporting an Issue

A useful support request includes the relevant order or record reference, the date and time, the page or workflow, what you expected, what happened, and any error message. Add a screenshot only after hiding passwords, verification codes, payment details, and personal information that is not needed.

## How to Submit Documentation Feedback

Use the documentation feedback process when something is unclear, outdated, or missing. Include the page name, the confusion or problem, and any context that makes the issue easier to fix.
