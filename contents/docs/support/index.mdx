## How to Contact BeautyComm Support

The right contact depends on who you are:

1. **Business owners and staff:** use the BeautyComm support channel assigned to your business.
2. **Shoppers:** contact the store where you placed the order. Use the contact details on that store's **Contact Us** page.

Support hours and response times are provided by the support channel assigned to your business.

For urgent problems that affect orders or payments, mark your message as **Urgent** and include the details below.

### Support & Contact

- **Email:** [mail@thetork.com](mailto:mail@thetork.com)
- **Phone:** +880 131 3941 166
- **Website:** [thetork.com](https://thetork.com)

## What Information to Provide When Reporting an Issue

A complete report helps the team fix the problem faster. Include:

1. **Your business or store name** and the email you sign in with.
2. **Where it happened:** Admin Panel, User Portal, or Mobile App, and the page or screen name.
3. **What you were trying to do,** and what happened instead. Copy any error message exactly.
4. **Steps to repeat the problem,** in order.
5. **When it happened,** with the date, time and time zone.
6. **Order, product or customer reference** involved, for example the order number. Do not send passwords.
7. **Device and browser or app version,** for example Chrome on Windows or Android on a mobile device.
8. **Any error logs or exact error text.** Do not include passwords, API keys or private customer details.

Never share your password or full payment details with anyone, including support.

## How to Submit Documentation Feedback

If a guide is unclear, outdated or missing, tell us. To send feedback:

1. Note the **page name or link** of the guide.
2. Describe what is wrong or missing, for example a step that does not match the screen.
3. Send it through the feedback form, support email or the “Was this helpful?” control at the bottom of the page.

We review all feedback and update the documentation regularly.

## Privacy and Legal Links

Account and store information is protected according to BeautyComm security practices. Never share passwords, verification codes, full payment details, or private customer data with support or other users.
