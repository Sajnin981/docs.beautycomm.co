## How to Contact BeautyComm Support

The right contact depends on who you are:

1. **Business owners and staff:** use the BeautyComm support channel assigned to your business: [support email, WhatsApp or ticket link].
2. **Shoppers:** contact the store where you placed the order. Use the contact details on that store's **Contact Us** page.

For urgent problems that affect orders or payments, mark your message as **Urgent** and include the details below.

## What Information to Provide When Reporting an Issue

A complete report helps the team fix the problem faster. Include:

1. **Your business or store name** and the email you log in with.
2. **Where it happened:** Admin Panel, Storefront or Mobile App, and the page or screen name.
3. **What you were trying to do,** and what happened instead. Copy any error message exactly.
4. **Steps to repeat the problem,** in order.
5. **When it happened,** with the date, time and time zone.
6. **Order, product or customer reference** involved, for example the order number. Do not send passwords.
7. **Device and browser or app version,** for example Chrome on Windows, or Android 14 with app version [x].
8. **Screenshots or a short screen recording.** Blur API keys, passwords and private customer details.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support What Information to Provide When Reporting an Issue screenshot" label="Capture for What Information to Provide When Reporting an Issue" description="Capture: Example of a good screenshot with the error highlighted and private data blurred. Never share your password or full payment details with anyone, including support.

## How to Submit Documentation Feedback

If a guide is unclear, outdated or missing, tell us. To send feedback:

1. Note the **page name or link** of the guide.
2. Describe what is wrong or missing, for example a step that does not match the screen.
3. Add a screenshot if it helps.
4. Send it through [feedback form, email or the "Was this helpful?" control at the bottom of each page].
