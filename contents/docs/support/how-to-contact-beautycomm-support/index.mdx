Reach out to BeautyComm support when a workflow is blocked, a feature is not behaving as expected, or you need help with a product issue. In most cases, the fastest path is to review the relevant troubleshooting page and then send a clear support request.

## Before you contact support

Review the guidance that matches your issue first:

- [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting)
- [Common Tasks](/docs/common-tasks)

## What to include in your message

Include the details that will help the team investigate quickly:

- the exact workflow or page that is affected
- your account role and business context
- the date and time the issue occurred
- the error message, if any
- a screenshot or screen recording when helpful
- the expected result versus the actual result

## How to contact support

Use the support channel assigned to your business. If your organization uses email, a help portal, or a ticketing form, provide the details above and keep the request concise and factual.

## Important reminder

Never share your password or security codes with anyone, including support teams. If you are not sure whether a message is legitimate, verify it through the official support channel your business uses.
