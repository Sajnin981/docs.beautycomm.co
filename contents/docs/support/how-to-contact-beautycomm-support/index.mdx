Contact the BeautyComm support team when you need help.

## Purpose

Explain how to get help.

## Who can use it

Any BeautyComm user.

## Where to find it

Use the support channel given to your business by BeautyComm.

## Before you begin

- Gather the details described in the linked guide.

## Steps

1. Check the relevant guide and troubleshooting page first.
2. Collect details about the problem.
3. Contact support through the channel given to your business.

## Expected result

The support team has what it needs to help you.

## Notes

- Never share your password with anyone, including support.
