Update stock for a product.

## Purpose

Show a quick way to adjust stock.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open **Inventory** or the product.

## Before you begin

- The correct quantity.

## Steps

1. Find the product.
2. Enter the quantity.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="Stock quantity field" label="Stock quantity field" />

3. Save.

## Expected result

Stock shows the new number.
