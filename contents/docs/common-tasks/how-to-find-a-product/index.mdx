Find a product quickly.

## Purpose

Show a quick way to locate a product.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open **Products**.

## Before you begin

- SKU or name.

## Steps

1. Open products.
2. Type the name or SKU in search.

3. Open the product.

## Expected result

The product opens.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Not found | Clear filters and try a shorter term. |
