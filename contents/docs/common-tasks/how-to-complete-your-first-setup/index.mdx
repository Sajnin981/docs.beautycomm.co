Set up your BeautyComm account for first use. This guide covers **How to Complete Your First Setup** in BeautyComm.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to how to complete your first setup.

## Who can use it

Authorized BeautyComm users.

## Where to find it

Open BeautyComm and look for the area related to how to complete your first setup. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.

## Steps

Detailed step-by-step instructions for this guide are being prepared and will be published once they are verified against the live product.

## Expected result

You can find the related area and understand what it offers.

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
