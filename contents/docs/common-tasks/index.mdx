These task guides are for **BeautyComm client businesses and their authorized staff**. Use them for store setup, product work, stock updates, order processing, customer lookup, reporting, and business-account access. Shoppers should use the [User Portal](/docs/user-portal) for help buying from a store.

## Set Up Your Business Account

Confirm your access and prepare your store before you start daily work. To do it:

1. Log in to the Admin Panel with the email and password from your welcome message.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Set Up Your Business Account screenshot" label="Capture for Set Up Your Business Account" description="Capture: Admin login page. Suggested filename: common-tasks/setup/01-login.png" caption="common-tasks/setup/01-login.png" />

2. Open your profile menu at the top right and check your name and role.
3. Go to **Settings > General** and confirm the store name, logo, currency, time zone and contact details.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Set Up Your Business Account screenshot" label="Capture for Set Up Your Business Account" description="Capture: General settings page. Suggested filename: common-tasks/setup/02-general-settings.png" caption="common-tasks/setup/02-general-settings.png" />

4. Go to **Staff** and add your team members with the right roles.
5. Follow the [First Steps](/docs/getting-started#first-steps) checklist on the Welcome page to finish payments, shipping and your first products.

**Related guides:** [General Settings & Configurations](/docs/admin-portal/general-settings-and-configurations) · [Staff & Role Permissions](/docs/admin-portal/staff-and-role-permissions) · [Payment Gateway Configuration](/docs/admin-portal/payment-gateway-configuration).

## Manage Products and Stock

Find a product, update its details and check inventory. To do it:

1. Go to **Products** and type the product name or SKU in the search box.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Manage Products and Stock screenshot" label="Capture for Manage Products and Stock" description="Capture: Products list with the search box. Suggested filename: common-tasks/products/01-search.png" caption="common-tasks/products/01-search.png" />

2. Click the product to open it.
3. Update the approved details, such as price, description, images or status, and click **Save**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Manage Products and Stock screenshot" label="Capture for Manage Products and Stock" description="Capture: Product edit page with the Save button. Suggested filename: common-tasks/products/02-edit.png" caption="common-tasks/products/02-edit.png" />

4. To change stock, go to **Inventory**, find the product or shade, click **Adjust Stock**, enter the quantity and a reason, and save.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Manage Products and Stock screenshot" label="Capture for Manage Products and Stock" description="Capture: Adjust stock dialog. Suggested filename: common-tasks/products/03-adjust-stock.png" caption="common-tasks/products/03-adjust-stock.png" />

5. Check the storefront to confirm the change is visible.

**Related guides:** [Products](/docs/admin-portal/products) · [Product Variations & Shades](/docs/admin-portal/product-variations-and-shades) · [Inventory & Stock Management](/docs/admin-portal/inventory-and-stock-management).

## Find and Process an Order

Open an order and move it through its workflow. To do it:

1. Go to **Orders** and search by order number, customer name or phone number. You can also use the status tabs.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Find and Process an Order screenshot" label="Capture for Find and Process an Order" description="Capture: Orders list with search and status tabs. Suggested filename: common-tasks/orders/01-find.png" caption="common-tasks/orders/01-find.png" />

2. Click the order to check the items, address, payment status and notes.
3. Update the status as you work: Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Delivered.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Find and Process an Order screenshot" label="Capture for Find and Process an Order" description="Capture: Order details with the status dropdown. Suggested filename: common-tasks/orders/02-status.png" caption="common-tasks/orders/02-status.png" />

4. Create a shipment with the courier and tracking number, and print the invoice or packing slip.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Find and Process an Order screenshot" label="Capture for Find and Process an Order" description="Capture: Create shipment and print actions. Suggested filename: common-tasks/orders/03-shipment.png" caption="common-tasks/orders/03-shipment.png" />

5. If the customer asks for a return, go to **Returns** and follow the return process.

**Related guides:** [Orders](/docs/admin-portal/orders) · [Shipping & Delivery Management](/docs/admin-portal/shipping-and-delivery-management) · [Returns & Refund Management](/docs/admin-portal/returns-and-refund-management).

## Find Customers and Reports

Look up a customer or review how your store is doing. To find a customer:

1. Go to **Customers** and search by name, email or phone number.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Find Customers and Reports screenshot" label="Capture for Find Customers and Reports" description="Capture: Customers list with search. Suggested filename: common-tasks/customers/01-search.png" caption="common-tasks/customers/01-search.png" />

2. Open the customer to see their orders, addresses and reward points.

To open a report:

1. Go to **Reports** and choose Sales, Products, Inventory or Customers.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Find Customers and Reports screenshot" label="Capture for Find Customers and Reports" description="Capture: Reports menu. Suggested filename: common-tasks/customers/02-reports-menu.png" caption="common-tasks/customers/02-reports-menu.png" />

2. Set the date range, review the results and click **Export** if you need the data.

**Related guides:** [Sales Reports & Analytics](/docs/admin-portal/sales-reports-and-analytics) · [Product & Inventory Reports](/docs/admin-portal/product-and-inventory-reports) · [Customer Insights & Analytics](/docs/admin-portal/customer-insights-and-analytics).

## Fix Account Access

Solve sign-in and permission problems. To do it:

1. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot Password** on the login page and follow the reset message.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Fix Account Access screenshot" label="Capture for Fix Account Access" description="Capture: Forgot password link on the login page. Suggested filename: common-tasks/access/01-forgot-password.png" caption="common-tasks/access/01-forgot-password.png" />

2. If you cannot see a menu item, ask your administrator to check your role under **Staff > Roles**.
3. If you left the company or lost a device, ask your administrator to deactivate or reset your account.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Fix Account Access screenshot" label="Capture for Fix Account Access" description="Capture: Staff list with the account status and role columns. Suggested filename: common-tasks/access/02-staff-status.png" caption="common-tasks/access/02-staff-status.png" />

If none of this works, see [Login and Access troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/login-and-access).

**Related guides:** [Staff & Role Permissions](/docs/admin-portal/staff-and-role-permissions) · [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
