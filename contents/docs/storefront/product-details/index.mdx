The product page gives the full information you need before you buy. To read it:

1. Click a product to open its page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Details screenshot" label="Capture for Product Details" description="Capture: Top of a product page with callouts on gallery, name, price, rating, stock status. Suggested filename: storefront/product/01-top-section.png" caption="storefront/product/01-top-section.png" />

2. Browse the image gallery and zoom into details.
3. Choose your options and quantity, then click **Add to Cart** or **Buy Now**.
4. Scroll down for the description, ingredients, how to use, delivery information and reviews.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Details screenshot" label="Capture for Product Details" description="Capture: Product tabs area. Suggested filename: storefront/product/02-tabs.png" caption="storefront/product/02-tabs.png" />

5. Check **Related Products** at the bottom for similar items.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Details screenshot" label="Capture for Product Details" description="Capture: Related products row. Suggested filename: storefront/product/03-related.png" caption="storefront/product/03-related.png" />
