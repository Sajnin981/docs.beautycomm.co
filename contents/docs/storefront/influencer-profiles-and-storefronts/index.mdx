1. Open **Influencers** from the menu.

2. Click an influencer to see their profile, content and picks.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Influencer Profiles &amp; Storefronts screenshot" label="Capture for Influencer Profiles &amp; Storefronts" description="Capture: Influencer storefront page with recommended products. 3. Click a product to buy it, or **Follow** to get updates.
