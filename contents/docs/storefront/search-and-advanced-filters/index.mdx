Find the right product with search and filters. To use them:

1. Type a product name, brand or concern in the search bar. Suggestions appear as you type.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Search &amp; Advanced Filters screenshot" label="Capture for Search &amp; Advanced Filters" description="Reuse the User Portal screenshot. Image path: search/03-filters.png" caption="search/03-filters.png" />

2. Press Enter to open the results page.
3. Use the filter panel to narrow by category, brand, price range, skin type, rating and availability.

4. Remove filters one by one with the tags above the results, or click **Clear All**.
