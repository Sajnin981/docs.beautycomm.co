Kirei Tube lets you watch beauty videos and shop the featured products. To use it:

1. Open **Kirei Tube** from the menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Kirei Tube &amp; Video Commerce screenshot" label="Capture for Kirei Tube &amp; Video Commerce" description="Reuse the User Portal screenshot. Image path: kirei-tube/02-product-tags.png" caption="kirei-tube/02-product-tags.png" />

2. Click a video to play it.
3. Click the product tags next to the video to open the featured items, or add them to your cart.
