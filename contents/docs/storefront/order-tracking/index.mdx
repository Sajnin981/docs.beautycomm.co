Follow your delivery from packing to arrival. To track an order:

1. Log in and open **My Orders**, or use **Track Order** in the footer or header.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Order Tracking screenshot" label="Capture for Order Tracking" description="Reuse the User Portal screenshot. Image path: tracking/02-timeline.png" caption="tracking/02-timeline.png" />

2. Select your order, or enter your order number and phone or email if you are tracking as a guest.
3. Check the timeline for the current status.
