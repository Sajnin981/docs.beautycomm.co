Connect with dermatologists for skin concerns. To use it:

1. Open **Dermatologist Support** from the menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Dermatologist Support screenshot" label="Capture for Dermatologist Support" description="Reuse the User Portal screenshot. Image path: dermatologist/02-request-form.png" caption="dermatologist/02-request-form.png" />

2. Choose a dermatologist and click **Request Consultation**.
3. Describe your concern and upload a photo if needed.

This service gives guidance and does not replace emergency care.
