Connect with dermatologists for skin concerns. To use it:

1. Open **Dermatologist Support** from the menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Dermatologist Support screenshot" label="Capture for Dermatologist Support" description="Capture: Dermatologist support page. Suggested filename: storefront/dermatologist/01-page.png" caption="storefront/dermatologist/01-page.png" />

2. Choose a dermatologist and click **Request Consultation**.
3. Describe your concern and upload a photo if needed.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Dermatologist Support screenshot" label="Capture for Dermatologist Support" description="Capture: Request form. Suggested filename: storefront/dermatologist/02-form.png" caption="storefront/dermatologist/02-form.png" />

This service gives guidance and does not replace emergency care.
