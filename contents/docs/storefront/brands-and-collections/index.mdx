Shop by brand or by curated collection. To browse them:

1. Open **Brands** from the main menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Brands &amp; Collections screenshot" label="Capture for Brands &amp; Collections" description="Capture: Brands page with logos and A-Z index. Suggested filename: storefront/brands/01-brands-page.png" caption="storefront/brands/01-brands-page.png" />

2. Click a brand logo to see all of its products.
3. Open **Collections** to browse themed sets such as "Summer Skincare" or "Gift Sets".

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Brands &amp; Collections screenshot" label="Capture for Brands &amp; Collections" description="Capture: Collections page. Suggested filename: storefront/brands/02-collections.png" caption="storefront/brands/02-collections.png" />

4. Use the filters on a brand or collection page to narrow the results.
