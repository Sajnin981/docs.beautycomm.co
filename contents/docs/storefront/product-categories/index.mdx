Categories organize the store so you can find products by type. To browse categories:

1. Open the **Categories** menu in the header.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Categories screenshot" label="Capture for Product Categories" description="Capture: Categories menu expanded with subcategories. Suggested filename: storefront/categories/01-menu.png" caption="storefront/categories/01-menu.png" />

2. Hover over or click a main category such as Skincare, Makeup or Haircare to see subcategories.
3. Click a subcategory to open its product list.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Categories screenshot" label="Capture for Product Categories" description="Capture: A category page with title and product grid. Suggested filename: storefront/categories/02-category-page.png" caption="storefront/categories/02-category-page.png" />

You can also click the category tiles on the home page.
