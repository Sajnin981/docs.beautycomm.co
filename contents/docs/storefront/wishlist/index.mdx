The wishlist saves products for later. To use it:

1. Click the **heart icon** on a product card or product page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Wishlist screenshot" label="Capture for Wishlist" description="Reuse the User Portal screenshot. Image path: wishlist/02-wishlist-page.png" caption="wishlist/02-wishlist-page.png" />

2. Click the **Wishlist** icon in the header to open your saved items.

3. Click **Add to Cart** to buy an item, or click the heart again to remove it.

You need to be logged in to keep your wishlist across devices.
