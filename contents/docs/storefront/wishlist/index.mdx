The wishlist saves products for later. To use it:

1. Click the **heart icon** on a product card or product page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Wishlist screenshot" label="Capture for Wishlist" description="Capture: Heart icon on a product card. Suggested filename: storefront/wishlist/01-heart.png" caption="storefront/wishlist/01-heart.png" />

2. Click the **Wishlist** icon in the header to open your saved items.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Wishlist screenshot" label="Capture for Wishlist" description="Capture: Wishlist page. Suggested filename: storefront/wishlist/02-wishlist-page.png" caption="storefront/wishlist/02-wishlist-page.png" />

3. Click **Add to Cart** to buy an item, or click the heart again to remove it.

You need to be logged in to keep your wishlist across devices.
