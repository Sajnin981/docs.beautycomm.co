After you place an order, BeautyComm confirms it. To read your confirmation:

1. After clicking **Place Order**, wait for the confirmation page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Order Confirmation screenshot" label="Capture for Order Confirmation" description="Capture: Order confirmation page. Suggested filename: storefront/confirmation/01-confirmation-page.png" caption="storefront/confirmation/01-confirmation-page.png" />

2. Note your order number, items, delivery address and payment method.
3. Check your email or phone for the confirmation message.

4. Click **Track Order** or **Continue Shopping**.
