When you are logged in, the store adapts to your interests and history. To benefit from it:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Browse and shop as usual. The home page then highlights products, offers and content based on your activity.

3. Complete your skin profile to improve beauty suggestions.
4. Check **Recently Viewed** to return to products you looked at.
