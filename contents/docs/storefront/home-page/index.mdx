The home page is the front door of the BeautyComm store. It brings together promotions, categories and recommended products so visitors can start shopping quickly.

1. Open the BeautyComm store at **shop.beautycomm.co**. The home page opens with the store header at the top, showing the logo, search bar and support phone number.

2. Use the search bar to find a product by name, brand or concern.

   ![Search bar on the BeautyComm home page](/images/storefront/home/home-search.jpg)

3. Click **Browse Categories** to open the product categories and choose the one you want.

   ![Browse Categories menu on the BeautyComm home page](/images/storefront/home/browse-categories.jpg)

4. Use the main menu to move around the store:
   - **Home:** return to the home page.
   - **Spin & Win:** select this menu item to open it.
   - **Shop:** browse all products.
   - **Blogs:** read beauty tips and articles.
   - **Contact:** reach the BeautyComm team.

   ![Main menu on the BeautyComm home page](/images/storefront/home/main-menu.jpg)

5. Use the icons at the top right to open your **wishlist**, **cart** and **account**.

   ![Wishlist, cart and account icons on the BeautyComm home page](/images/storefront/home/header-icons.jpg)

6. Look at the large banner below the menu to see current promotions and campaigns. Click a banner to open its offer.

   ![Promotional banner on the BeautyComm home page](/images/storefront/home/storefront-home.jpg)

7. Scroll down to see the store's delivery and discount offers, then keep scrolling to browse the categories and products shown on the home page.

Need help? Click the chat button at the bottom right of the page to ask questions about skincare, hair care or your order.
