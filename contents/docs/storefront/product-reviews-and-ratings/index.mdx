Reviews from other customers help you decide. To read reviews:

1. Scroll to the **Reviews** tab on a product page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Reviews &amp; Ratings screenshot" label="Capture for Product Reviews &amp; Ratings" description="Capture: Reviews tab with average rating and rating breakdown. Suggested filename: storefront/reviews/01-reviews-tab.png" caption="storefront/reviews/01-reviews-tab.png" />

2. Check the average rating and the breakdown by stars.
3. Sort or filter reviews, for example by most recent or with photos.
4. Click a review photo to view it larger.

Customers who bought the product can write their own review from their account.
