BeautyComm offers several ways to pay. To pay for an order:

1. At checkout, open the **Payment** section.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Payment Methods screenshot" label="Capture for Payment Methods" description="Capture: Payment method list. Suggested filename: storefront/payment/01-methods.png" caption="storefront/payment/01-methods.png" />

2. Choose a method, for example [card, mobile banking, cash on delivery].
3. For online payment, enter the required details or follow the redirect to the payment page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Payment Methods screenshot" label="Capture for Payment Methods" description="Capture: Card or mobile banking payment form. Suggested filename: storefront/payment/02-payment-form.png" caption="storefront/payment/02-payment-form.png" />

4. Click **Place Order**.

Available methods may vary by order amount or delivery area.
