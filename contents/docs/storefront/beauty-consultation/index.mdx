Beauty consultation gives you personal advice. To book it:

1. Open **Beauty Consultation** from the menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Beauty Consultation screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Consultation" description="Capture: Consultation page. Suggested filename: storefront/consultation/01-page.png" caption="storefront/consultation/01-page.png" />

2. Choose a topic and a preferred time.
3. Fill in the form and click **Book Consultation**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Beauty Consultation screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Consultation" description="Capture: Consultation booking form. Suggested filename: storefront/consultation/02-form.png" caption="storefront/consultation/02-form.png" />

See Beauty Consultation in the User Portal section for managing your bookings.
