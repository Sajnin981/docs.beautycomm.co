BeautyComm shows how it guarantees genuine products. To find this information:

1. Look for the **Authentic** badge or authenticity section on a product page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Authenticity Information screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity Information" description="Capture: Authenticity badge or section on a product page. Suggested filename: storefront/authenticity/01-badge.png" caption="storefront/authenticity/01-badge.png" />

2. Click it to read how the product is sourced and how you can verify it.
3. If the product has a verification code or QR label, follow the verification steps shown.

Contact Customer Support if you have doubts about a product you received.
