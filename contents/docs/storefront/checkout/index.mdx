Checkout is where you confirm delivery and payment details. To complete it:

1. Click **Proceed to Checkout** from the cart.
2. Enter or select your delivery address.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Checkout screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout" description="Capture: Checkout page, address section. Suggested filename: storefront/checkout/01-address.png" caption="storefront/checkout/01-address.png" />

3. Choose a delivery method and check the delivery charge.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Checkout screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout" description="Capture: Delivery method options. Suggested filename: storefront/checkout/02-delivery.png" caption="storefront/checkout/02-delivery.png" />

4. Review your items and order total, then continue to payment.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Checkout screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout" description="Capture: Order summary panel. Suggested filename: storefront/checkout/03-summary.png" caption="storefront/checkout/03-summary.png" />
