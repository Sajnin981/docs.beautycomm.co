Flash sales offer limited-time discounts, and group shopping gives better prices when buyers join together. To join:

1. Open **Flash Sale** from the home page or main menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Flash Sales &amp; Group Shopping screenshot" label="Capture for Flash Sales &amp; Group Shopping" description="Capture: Flash sale page with countdown timer. Suggested filename: storefront/flash-sale/01-flash-sale.png" caption="storefront/flash-sale/01-flash-sale.png" />

2. Check the countdown timer and remaining stock bar.
3. Click a product and add it to your cart before the sale ends.
4. For group deals, click **Join Group** or **Start Group**, then share the link with friends.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Flash Sales &amp; Group Shopping screenshot" label="Capture for Flash Sales &amp; Group Shopping" description="Capture: Group shopping deal with participant count and Share button. Suggested filename: storefront/flash-sale/02-group-deal.png" caption="storefront/flash-sale/02-group-deal.png" />

5. The group price applies when the required number of buyers is reached.

Sale items can sell out quickly, so check out soon after adding them.
