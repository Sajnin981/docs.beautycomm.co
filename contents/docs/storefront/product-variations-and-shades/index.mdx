Variations let you choose the exact shade, size or type of a product. To select one:

1. On the product page, find the **Shade** or **Size** options below the price.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Product Variations &amp; Shades screenshot" label="Capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Reuse the User Portal screenshot. Image path: variations/01-shade-selector.png" caption="variations/01-shade-selector.png" />

2. Click an option. The image, price and stock status update.
3. If an option is unavailable, it is greyed out or marked **Out of Stock**.

4. Click **Add to Cart** with your selection.
