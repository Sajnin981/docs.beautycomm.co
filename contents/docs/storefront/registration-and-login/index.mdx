Create an account or log in from any page of the storefront. To get started:

1. Click the **account icon** or **Login / Sign Up** in the header.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Header with the account icon highlighted. Suggested filename: storefront/auth/01-header-login.png" caption="storefront/auth/01-header-login.png" />

2. To register, choose **Sign Up**, fill in your details and verify with the code sent to you.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Sign-up form. Suggested filename: storefront/auth/02-signup.png" caption="storefront/auth/02-signup.png" />

3. To log in, enter your email or phone number and password, or use a social login button.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Login form with social login options. Suggested filename: storefront/auth/03-login.png" caption="storefront/auth/03-login.png" />

4. After logging in, you return to the page you were on.

For full account steps, see Registration & Login in the User Portal section.
