Create an account or log in from any page of the storefront. To get started:

1. Click the **account icon** or **Login / Sign Up** in the header.

2. To register, choose **Sign Up**, fill in your details and verify with the code sent to you.

3. To log in, enter your email or phone number and password, or use a social login button.

4. After logging in, you return to the page you were on.

For full account steps, see Registration & Login in the User Portal section.
