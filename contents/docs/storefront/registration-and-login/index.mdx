Create your BeautyComm account or log in with your mobile phone number to shop, save products to your wishlist, track orders and earn reward points. You can open your account from the top of the storefront.

## Open the account page

Click the account icon at the top right of the page. The Login and Register page opens.

![Account icon at the top right of the BeautyComm store](/images/user-portal/registration-login/dashboard.jpg)

## Register

If you are new to BeautyComm, create an account with your phone number:

1. Select the **Register** tab.

![Register tab on the BeautyComm account page](/images/user-portal/registration-login/register.jpg)

2. Enter your mobile phone number in the **Phone** field.
3. Click **Send OTP**. A one-time password (OTP) is sent to your phone.
4. Enter the OTP to verify your number and follow any additional prompts shown.

Use a phone number that can receive messages, since the OTP is sent to it. If you already have an account, click **Log in** instead.

## Login

If you already have an account, log in with your phone number:

1. Select the **Login** tab.

![Login tab on the BeautyComm account page](/images/user-portal/registration-login/login.jpg)

2. Choose how you want to log in:
   - **With an OTP:** enter your phone number, click **Send OTP**, then enter the code you receive.
   - **With a password:** click **Login With Password**, then enter your phone number and password.
3. After you log in, continue shopping, use your wishlist and cart, and open your dashboard from the account icon.

If you do not remember your password, log in with an OTP instead. If you do not have an account yet, click **Register**.
