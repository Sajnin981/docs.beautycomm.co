Use coupons and see discounts across the store. To apply a coupon:

1. Look for the discount tag on product cards, and coupon banners on the home page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Coupons &amp; Discounts screenshot" label="Capture for Coupons &amp; Discounts" description="Capture: Product card with discount tag and a coupon banner. Suggested filename: storefront/coupons/01-discount-tags.png" caption="storefront/coupons/01-discount-tags.png" />

2. In the cart or at checkout, type your code in the **Coupon** field and click **Apply**.

3. The discount appears in the order summary.

Check the minimum spend and expiry date of each coupon.
