Banners on the home page highlight sales, new launches and campaigns. To use them:

1. Look at the large banner slider at the top of the home page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Home &amp; Promotional Banners screenshot" label="Capture for Home &amp; Promotional Banners" description="Capture: Main banner slider with arrows and dots visible. Suggested filename: storefront/banners/01-main-slider.png" caption="storefront/banners/01-main-slider.png" />

2. Use the arrows or dots to move between banners, or wait for it to rotate automatically.
3. Click a banner to open its offer, category or campaign page.
4. Scroll down to find smaller promotional banners placed between sections.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Home &amp; Promotional Banners screenshot" label="Capture for Home &amp; Promotional Banners" description="Capture: Smaller promotional banners mid-page. Suggested filename: storefront/banners/02-promo-banners.png" caption="storefront/banners/02-promo-banners.png" />

Check the banner's dates or conditions on the offer page before buying.
