BeautyComm suggests products you may like. To find them:

1. Look for sections such as **Recommended for You**, **You May Also Like** and **Frequently Bought Together**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront AI Product Recommendations screenshot" label="Capture for AI Product Recommendations" description="Capture: Recommendation section on the home page. Suggested filename: storefront/recommendations/01-home.png" caption="storefront/recommendations/01-home.png" />

2. Click a suggested product to open it, or add it directly to your cart or wishlist.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront AI Product Recommendations screenshot" label="Capture for AI Product Recommendations" description="Capture: Recommendation row on a product page. Suggested filename: storefront/recommendations/02-product-page.png" caption="storefront/recommendations/02-product-page.png" />

Suggestions become more relevant the more you browse and shop while logged in.
