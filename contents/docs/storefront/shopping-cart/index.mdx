The cart lists everything you plan to buy. To use it:

1. Click **Add to Cart** on a product, then click the **cart icon** in the header.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Shopping Cart screenshot" label="Capture for Shopping Cart" description="Capture: Mini-cart dropdown. Suggested filename: storefront/cart/01-mini-cart.png" caption="storefront/cart/01-mini-cart.png" />

2. Click **View Cart** to open the full cart page.
3. Change quantities, remove items or apply a coupon.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Storefront Shopping Cart screenshot" label="Capture for Shopping Cart" description="Capture: Cart page with quantity controls, coupon field and order summary. Suggested filename: storefront/cart/02-cart-page.png" caption="storefront/cart/02-cart-page.png" />

4. Check the totals, then click **Proceed to Checkout**.
