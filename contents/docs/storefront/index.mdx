The Storefront is the public-facing shopping experience for visitors and customers. These guides cover the shopping journey, including finding products, learning about items, using the cart, and proceeding through checkout.

Use the [User Portal](/docs/user-portal) for personal account activity and the [Admin Panel](/docs/admin-portal) for staff operations and store configuration.

Store-specific payment methods, policies, promotions, and features may differ. Confirm what is available on the relevant storefront before following a workflow.
