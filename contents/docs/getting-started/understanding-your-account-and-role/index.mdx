Your account determines how you sign in and which business context you are working in. Your role determines what you can view, edit, and manage inside that context.

## Why roles matter

Not every user needs access to the same parts of BeautyComm. A person managing inventory may need stock visibility, while an administrator may need permission to manage users, configuration, or operational settings.

## Typical role-based concepts

- Access to product and catalog features
- Permissions for customer and order information
- Reporting access for operational or administrative views
- The ability to manage account settings or business configuration

The exact role names and permission boundaries can vary by business and product configuration, so use this guidance as a general framework rather than a fixed list of role names.

## What to do if a feature is missing

If a page, workflow, or option is not available to you:

- check whether your account is active
- confirm that you are in the correct business environment
- verify that your role includes the workflow
- speak with your administrator if the missing access is expected to be available
