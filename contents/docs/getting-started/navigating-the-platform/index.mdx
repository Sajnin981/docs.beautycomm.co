BeautyComm is typically organized around a main workspace, navigation menu, and task-specific sections. Once you are signed in, the fastest way to work effectively is to understand the primary areas you use most often.

## The main structure

Most users will move between these kinds of areas:

- Catalog and product work
- Inventory and stock visibility
- Orders and customer support
- Reports and operational monitoring
- Account settings and profile information
- Administrative setup for authorized roles

The exact menu labels and available sections can vary by business configuration and user role.

## How to move around efficiently

1. Open the main navigation menu after signing in.
2. Select the area that matches your task, such as catalog, orders, or reports.
3. Use list search, filters, or record lookup features to narrow the content you need.
4. Open the relevant record to review details or update information.
5. Return to the main navigation when moving to a different area of the platform.

## Practical guidance

- If a section is missing, it may not be available to your role.
- If a list is empty or incomplete, check whether the data is visible for your current business context.
- If a workflow feels unclear, use the task guides in [Common Tasks](/docs/common-tasks) for step-by-step support.
