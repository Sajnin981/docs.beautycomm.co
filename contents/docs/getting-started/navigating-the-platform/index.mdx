BeautyComm is organized around a main navigation menu and a working area.

## Purpose

Help you move between dashboard, catalog, orders, customers and reports.

## Who can use it

All signed-in users.

## Where to find it

After signing in, look at the main navigation menu.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to BeautyComm.

## Steps

1. Find the main navigation menu. It lists the areas available to your role.

<Screenshot step={1} alt="Main navigation menu" label="Main navigation menu" />

2. Select an area, for example products or orders, to open it.
3. Use search or filters at the top of a list to narrow what you see.
4. Select a row to open its details.
5. Use your profile or account menu to manage your account or sign out.

## Expected result

You can reach any area you have access to in a few clicks.

## Notes

- Menu items you cannot see are not part of your role.
- Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| A menu item is missing | Your role may not include it. Ask your administrator. |
