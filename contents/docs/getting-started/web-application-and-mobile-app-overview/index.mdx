BeautyComm is designed to support core work through the web experience and, where relevant, the mobile experience. The goal is not to duplicate every action, but to ensure the same core workflows remain accessible in the way your team needs to work.

## Web experience

The web application is typically where teams complete broader operational work, review records, manage dashboards, and work through the more detailed product and workflow views.

## Mobile experience

The mobile experience is usually used for quick checks, limited tasks, or access while away from the desk. The exact features available on mobile depend on your business setup and role.

## How they fit together

Use the web experience for deeper operational work and the mobile experience for lighter or time-sensitive tasks. Your business configuration and permissions determine which features are available in either context.
