Always sign out of BeautyComm when you finish work, especially on shared devices or public networks. Closing the session helps protect your account and prevents unauthorized access to the business workspace.

## When to sign out

Sign out whenever you:

- finish your working session
- leave a shared or public device
- switch to a different account or environment
- complete work on a device that is not personally managed

## Sign out safely

1. Open the account menu or the session controls from the main workspace.
2. Select the sign-out action.
3. Confirm that you are returned to the sign-in screen or a logged-out state.

## Expected result

Your account is no longer active in the current browser session and the workspace is protected from unauthorized use.

## Security reminders

- Never leave a signed-in session open on a shared device.
- Do not share session access or saved login details.
- If you are unable to sign out, close the browser window and contact your administrator if the session remains active unexpectedly.
