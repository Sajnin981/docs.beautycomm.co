This documentation is most useful to people who need to understand the BeautyComm platform, complete everyday operational work, or troubleshoot access or workflow issues.

## Typical audiences

- business owners and operators who need an overview of the platform
- store or team leads who coordinate product, order, and customer workflows
- support or operations staff who handle day-to-day tasks in the workspace
- administrators who manage access, settings, and account permissions

## What this guide is meant to help with

- getting started with the product quickly
- finding the right task guide for a workflow
- understanding role-based access and permissions
- resolving common account and operational issues

## Start with the right section

If you are new to the platform, begin with [Welcome to BeautyComm](/docs/getting-started). If you need a specific workflow, browse the task guides in [Common Tasks](/docs/common-tasks). If you need help with access or a broken workflow, start with [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
