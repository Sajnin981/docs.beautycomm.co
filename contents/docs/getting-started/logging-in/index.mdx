Signing in is the first step to accessing your product catalog, inventory, orders, customers, and reports. Always use the BeautyComm sign-in page assigned to your organization.

## Before you begin

- Your business-specific BeautyComm URL
- Your username or email address
- Your password or the verification method your business requires
- A supported browser and an active network connection

## Sign in

1. Open the BeautyComm sign-in page provided by your business.
2. Enter your account credentials.
3. Complete any additional verification if your organization requires it.
4. Select the sign-in action to open your workspace.

## After you sign in

After signing in, you should land in the main workspace and see only the areas your role allows. If you cannot see the menu items you expect, review your role and account access before continuing.

## Security reminders

- Never share your password or one-time verification codes.
- Sign out when using a shared or public device.
- If your organization uses a password reset or account recovery flow, use the official process provided to your business.
