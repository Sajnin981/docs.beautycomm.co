Sign in to see your products, orders and reports.

## Purpose

Explain how to sign in to your BeautyComm account.

## Who can use it

Anyone with a BeautyComm account. Your role decides what you see after signing in.

## Where to find it

Open the BeautyComm sign-in page provided to your business.

## Before you begin

- Your sign-in email or username and password.
- A modern, up-to-date web browser.

## Steps

1. Open the BeautyComm sign-in page in your browser.
2. Enter your sign-in details.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="Sign-in page with the sign-in form" label="Sign-in page with the sign-in form" />

3. Select the sign-in button.

## Expected result

You arrive at your dashboard and see the menu items your role allows.

## Notes

- Never share your password.
- If your business uses extra verification, follow the on-screen prompt.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong password message | Check caps lock, then reset the password if available or ask your administrator. |
| The page keeps reloading | Clear browser cookies for the site and try again. |
