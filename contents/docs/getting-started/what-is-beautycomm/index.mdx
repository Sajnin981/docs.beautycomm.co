BeautyComm is a beauty commerce platform designed to help businesses manage products, stock, orders, customers, and store operations in one place. It brings together the operational work of selling beauty products with the visibility needed to keep day-to-day store functions accurate and efficient.

## What it is designed to help with

BeautyComm supports the core work of a beauty business, including:

- Product and catalog management
- Inventory tracking and stock visibility
- Order review and fulfillment coordination
- Customer record access and support workflows
- Sales and storefront visibility
- Reporting and operational oversight
- Role-based access for different teams and administrators

## Why the platform matters

Beauty businesses often work across several moving parts at once: product information, stock levels, customer requests, promotions, and order handling. BeautyComm is designed to give operational teams a single place to keep those workflows aligned and easier to manage.

## Where the platform is typically used

BeautyComm is structured around a few distinct working areas:

- The web application for day-to-day operational work
- The Admin Panel for configuration and operational oversight
- The mobile experience for general access and supported workflows

These areas are designed to serve different needs, but they all work toward the same goal: keeping the business running smoothly and consistently.
