BeautyComm is your beauty shop with AI: a commerce platform that helps beauty businesses manage products, stock, orders, customers and sales in one place.

## Purpose

Give you a clear picture of what BeautyComm does before you start using it.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized staff.

## Where to find it

This guide is available in Getting Started in the documentation sidebar.

## Before you begin

- Nothing is required. This is an introduction.

## Steps

1. Read the overview of the platform below.
2. Continue to the platform guide to see how the web application, Admin Panel and mobile app fit together.

## Expected result

You understand the main areas of BeautyComm and where to learn more.

## Notes

- **Catalog and inventory:** keep products, shades and stock up to date.
- **Orders and customers:** follow purchases from placement to delivery.
- **Sales channels and promotions:** sell in more places and run offers.
- **Reports and AI assistance:** understand performance and get suggestions you can review.
- Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.
