Use this guide as a practical starting checklist for a new BeautyComm account. The exact setup flow depends on your role, business configuration, and specific product version, but these are the common first activities to review.

## Before you begin

- Confirm that you can sign in to the correct BeautyComm environment.
- Review your role and the features available to it.
- Identify which workflows matter most to your job: catalog, inventory, orders, customers, or reporting.

## Recommended first checks

1. Confirm your account access and sign-in details.
2. Review the main navigation and the areas available to your role.
3. Check whether product, stock, order, and customer data are visible and complete.
4. Identify the most important product categories, reports, or workflows for your role.
5. Review the relevant task guides in [Common Tasks](/docs/common-tasks).

## Typical onboarding priorities

- Make sure the account is linked to the correct business or store context.
- Confirm that you can access the areas needed for your daily workflow.
- Review the product catalog and key stock information if you work with merchandising or operations.
- Review order and customer workflows if you support fulfillment or service requests.
- Review reporting and access settings if you are responsible for monitoring or administration.

## When to ask for help

If you cannot see the workflow you expect, first check your account access and role. If the issue continues, speak with the account administrator or support team and include the exact workflow you expected to access.
