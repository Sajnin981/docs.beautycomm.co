Use these steps as a practical starting path after your BeautyComm account is ready. The exact order may vary by role and business setup, but this sequence helps new users get oriented quickly.

## Recommended order

1. Confirm access and sign in with the correct account.
2. Review [Navigating the Platform](/docs/getting-started/navigating-the-platform) to understand the main areas of the product.
3. Check whether your role includes the product, inventory, order, or reporting workflows you need.
4. Review the most relevant task guides under [Common Tasks](/docs/common-tasks).
5. If you are setting up the platform for the first time, read [First-Time Setup](/docs/getting-started/first-time-setup).
6. If a workflow is not behaving as expected, use [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) before escalating.

## Focus areas for new teams

- Product setup and catalog basics
- Inventory and stock accuracy
- Order review and customer support workflows
- Reporting and operational visibility
- Account access and role review

## Typical first-week priorities

Depending on your role, common early priorities include:

- learning where orders, products, and customers are managed
- confirming your access level and permissions
- reviewing product information for accuracy and completeness
- checking inventory visibility for key items
- identifying the reports or operational views you rely on most
