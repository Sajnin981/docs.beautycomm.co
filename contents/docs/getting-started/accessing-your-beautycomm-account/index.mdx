Access to BeautyComm is typically provided through the business-specific sign-in environment assigned to your organization. Before you begin work, make sure you are using the correct URL, account, and role context for your company or store.

## What you need before you start

- The correct BeautyComm sign-in address for your business
- Your username or email address
- Your password or the verification method your organization requires
- Confirmation that your account is active and assigned to the right business context

## Typical access checks

1. Confirm that you are using the correct BeautyComm environment.
2. Check whether your sign-in details are current and valid.
3. Check that your account is active and not locked or suspended.
4. Verify that your role includes the features you need for your work.
5. If you cannot access the area you expect, contact your administrator or support team.

## If something is missing

If a menu item, product area, or admin feature is not visible, the most common explanation is that the account has limited access for the current role. In that case, check your role configuration or ask your administrator.
