Share a product with friends and family. To share a product:

1. Open the product page.
2. Click the **Share** icon.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Social Product Sharing screenshot" label="Capture for Social Product Sharing" description="Capture: Product page with the Share icon highlighted. Suggested filename: sharing/01-share-icon.png" caption="sharing/01-share-icon.png" />

3. Choose an app such as WhatsApp, Facebook or Messenger, or click **Copy Link**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Social Product Sharing screenshot" label="Capture for Social Product Sharing" description="Capture: Share options. Suggested filename: sharing/02-share-options.png" caption="sharing/02-share-options.png" />

4. Send the link. Your friend can open the product directly.
