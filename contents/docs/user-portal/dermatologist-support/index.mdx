Speak with a dermatologist about skin concerns. To get support:

1. Open **Dermatologist Support** from the menu.
2. Browse the list of available dermatologists and select one.

3. Click **Request Consultation**, describe your concern and upload a photo if needed.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Dermatologist Support screenshot" label="Capture for Dermatologist Support" description="Capture: Request form with photo upload. Suggested filename: dermatologist/02-request-form.png" caption="dermatologist/02-request-form.png" />

4. Submit and follow the reply in your account or notifications.

This service offers guidance and does not replace emergency medical care.
