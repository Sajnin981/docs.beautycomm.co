Explore the full BeautyComm range of skincare, makeup, haircare and more. To browse products:

1. Open the BeautyComm store homepage.
2. Use the top menu to open **Shop** or choose a category.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Shop &amp; Browse Products screenshot" label="Capture for Shop &amp; Browse Products" description="Capture: Homepage with the main menu and Shop link highlighted. Suggested filename: shop/01-homepage-menu.png" caption="shop/01-homepage-menu.png" />

3. Scroll through the product grid. Each card shows the product image, name, price and rating.
4. Use **Sort By** to order products by popularity, price or newest.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Shop &amp; Browse Products screenshot" label="Capture for Shop &amp; Browse Products" description="Capture: Product listing page with the Sort By dropdown open. Suggested filename: shop/02-product-listing.png" caption="shop/02-product-listing.png" />

5. Click a product to open its full details.

Browse by category, brand or promotion to find products that suit you.
