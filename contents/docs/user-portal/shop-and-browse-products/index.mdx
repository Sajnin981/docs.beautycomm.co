## Home

The Home page is the front door of the store. It highlights featured banners, new arrivals and the main ways to start shopping.

![Home page with header, menu bar and New Arrivals](/images/user-portal/home.jpg)

- The header holds the logo, the product search bar, the support phone number, and icons for wishlist, cart and your account.
- The menu bar gives you **Browse Categories**, **Home**, **Spin & Win**, **Shop**, **Blogs** and **Contact**.
- Scroll down from the hero banner to find sections such as **New Arrivals**, each with product cards.
- Select the chat button at the bottom right any time to ask about skincare, hair care or an order.

## Browse Products

Use the Shop page to look through the full catalogue at your own pace.

1. Select **Shop** in the menu bar.
2. Scroll through the product grid. Each card shows the photo, name, rating, price and an **Add to Cart** button.
   ![Browse products](/images/user-portal/browse-products.jpg)
3. Select a product name or photo to open its details.
4. Use the heart icon on a card to save the product for later.
5. Load more products by scrolling or using the page controls at the bottom.

## Search & Filters

Find a specific product quickly, or narrow a long list down to what suits you.

1. Select **Search for product** and type a product or brand name, then press Enter or select the magnifier.
2. Open **Browse Categories** in the menu to jump straight to a category such as K Beauty, J-Beauty or Makeup.
3. On the results page, use the filters to narrow the results, such as category, brand, price range or skin concern, where available.
4. Clear the filters to return to the full list.
5. Open any result to continue to its product page.

## Product Details

The product page gives you everything needed to decide before you buy.

1. Swipe or scroll through the product photos.
2. Read the name, brand, rating and price.
3. Select **view More** to read the full description, size, ingredients and usage notes.
4. Set the quantity with the **+** and **−** buttons. Check that it says **In Stock**.
5. Select **Add to Cart**, or select **Buy now** to go straight to checkout.
6. Select the heart to save the product, or the share icon to send it to a friend.
7. Customer reviews appear on the same page once other shoppers have rated the item.

## Shades & Variations

Some products come in several shades, sizes or variants, and the price or stock can differ between them.

1. Open the product and look for the shade or variation selector near the price.
2. Select the option you want. The photo, price and availability update to match it.
3. An unavailable option is shown as disabled, so pick another or save the product to your wishlist.
4. Add the chosen variation to your cart and confirm its name appears in the cart line.

## Recommendations

BeautyComm can suggest products based on what you browse, buy and save, so you see more of what fits you.

- Suggested products appear on the Home page and below product details.
- The more you browse and shop while logged in, the more relevant the suggestions become.
- Your skin type and concerns from the Consultation pages sharpen the picks further.
- Add a suggestion to your cart or wishlist exactly like any other product.

## Wishlist

The Wishlist keeps products you like in one place until you are ready to buy.

1. Select the heart icon on any product card or product page to save it. You may need to Sign in first.
2. Select the heart icon in the header, or **My WishList** in the Dashboard sidebar, to open your Wishlist.
3. Review the saved products and their current prices.
4. Select **Add to Cart** to buy one, or the remove option to take it off the list.

## Verify Product

Verify Product lets you check that an item you bought is genuine.

1. Find the authenticity code or QR mark on the packaging.
2. Select **Verify Product** in the Dashboard sidebar and enter the code, or scan the mark if scanning is offered.
3. Select **Verify** and wait for the result.
4. A genuine result shows the product details. If the code is not recognized, contact Support before using the product.

## Reviews

Reviews help other shoppers choose products and help BeautyComm improve its catalogue.

1. Open a delivered order, or a product you purchased previously.
2. Select a star rating and write a short description of your experience.
3. Submit the review.
4. It may appear after the store team performs a quick check.

## Sharing

Share a product you like with friends or family in one step.

1. Open the product page.
2. Select the share icon.
3. Choose an app or copy the product link.
4. Send the link. The recipient opens directly to the same product.
