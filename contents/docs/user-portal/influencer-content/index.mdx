Follow influencers and shop their picks. To see influencer content:

1. Open **Influencers** from the main menu.
2. Click an influencer to open their profile.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Influencer Content screenshot" label="Capture for Influencer Content" description="Capture: Influencer profile page. Suggested filename: influencers/01-profile.png" caption="influencers/01-profile.png" />

3. Browse their videos, posts and recommended products.
4. Click **Follow** to stay updated, and click a product to buy it.
