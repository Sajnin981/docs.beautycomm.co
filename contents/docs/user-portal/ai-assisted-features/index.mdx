## Check Available Features

Use only features visible in your BeautyComm workspace. The available tools and inputs may vary by business. Ask your administrator if you expect a feature but cannot find it.

<Screenshot alt="BeautyComm AI-assisted feature" label="Add a screenshot of an enabled AI-assisted workflow" />

## Review a Suggestion

Check the suggestion against current product, order, or business information. AI output may be incomplete or wrong; do not treat it as verified product, health, financial, or customer information.

## Use or Discard Output

Correct or discard unsuitable output. Before saving a change, review each affected field and follow the usual business approval process.

## Use AI Responsibly

Do not enter sensitive or identifying information unless the feature and your business policy explicitly allow it. Report unexpected output through your normal support channel.
