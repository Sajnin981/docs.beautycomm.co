AI assistance helps with tasks like drafting or insights. You stay in control.

## Purpose

Explain what AI features are and how to use them responsibly.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Look for AI or assistant options in the areas where they are available.

## Before you begin

- Check that the feature is available in your account.

## Steps

1. Open an area that offers AI assistance.
2. Choose the AI option.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="AI suggestion" label="AI suggestion" />

3. Review the suggestion.
4. Edit it, then accept or discard it.

## Expected result

You get a suggestion that you can edit before using it.

## Notes

- Always review AI output for accuracy before publishing.
- Do not enter confidential information you would not share in a business setting.
