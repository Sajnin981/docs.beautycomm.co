Your cart collects everything you plan to buy before checkout. To manage your cart:

1. Click **Add to Cart** on a product, then open the cart icon at the top right.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Shopping Cart screenshot" label="Capture for Shopping Cart" description="Capture: Cart icon with item count and the mini-cart preview. Suggested filename: cart/01-cart-icon.png" caption="cart/01-cart-icon.png" />

2. Review each item's shade or size, price and quantity.
3. Use the **+** and **-** buttons to change quantity, or click **Remove** to delete an item.
4. Enter a coupon code in the **Coupon** field and click **Apply**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Shopping Cart screenshot" label="Capture for Shopping Cart" description="Capture: Full cart page with quantity controls, coupon field and order summary. Suggested filename: cart/02-cart-page.png" caption="cart/02-cart-page.png" />

5. Click **Proceed to Checkout** when ready.

The order summary updates automatically as you change items or apply a coupon.
