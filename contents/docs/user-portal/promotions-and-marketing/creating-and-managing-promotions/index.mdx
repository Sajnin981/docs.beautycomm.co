Learn about creating and managing promotions. Promotions help you attract customers with discounts, offers and campaigns. This guide covers **Creating and Managing Promotions** in the User Portal.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to creating and managing promotions.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open the User Portal and look for the area related to creating and managing promotions. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.

## Steps

Detailed step-by-step instructions for this guide are being prepared and will be published once they are verified against the live product.

## Expected result

You can find the related area and understand what it offers.
