Learn about understanding available promotional features. Promotions help you attract customers with discounts, offers and campaigns. This guide covers **Understanding Available Promotional Features** in the User Portal.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to understanding available promotional features.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open the User Portal and look for the area related to understanding available promotional features. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.
