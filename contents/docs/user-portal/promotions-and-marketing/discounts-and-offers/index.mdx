Discounts reduce a price; offers combine discounts with conditions.

## Purpose

Explain how discounts work.

## Who can use it

Users whose role allows promotions.

## Where to find it

Open the promotions area, if available in your account.

## Before you begin

- The products or orders to discount.
- Start and end dates.

## Steps

1. Open promotions.
2. Choose to create a discount or offer.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="Create discount" label="Create discount" />

3. Choose the discount type, such as percentage or fixed amount.
4. Choose what it applies to and when.
5. Save and activate it.

## Expected result

The discount applies for the dates and items you chose.

## Notes

- Percentage discounts and fixed-amount discounts differ. Check examples before activating.
- Promotion options differ by account and version.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Discount not applying | Check dates, eligibility and whether it is active. |
