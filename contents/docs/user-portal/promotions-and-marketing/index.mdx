## Available Offers

Your store may support discounts, coupon codes, special event offers, or featured products. Available promotion types and rules depend on configuration. Use the offer details in BeautyComm as the source of truth.

## Set Up a Promotion

Before publishing an approved offer, confirm its code, discount, eligible items, start and end dates, and any usage limits. Preview the customer-facing message and verify the discount in the checkout summary where possible.

## Campaign Content

Keep campaign messages, product links, offer terms, and dates consistent. Update or remove time-sensitive banners when an offer ends. Use reports, where available, to review campaign activity.

## Fix an Offer Problem

Check that the offer is active, within its dates, and applies to the selected product and order. Confirm the code and any limits. Do not promise a discount until it appears in the checkout summary.
