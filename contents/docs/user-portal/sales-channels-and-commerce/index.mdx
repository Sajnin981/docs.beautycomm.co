This guide is for businesses managing a BeautyComm store. A channel is a place where shoppers discover or buy products. The online store is the primary customer-facing channel; additional channels depend on your setup.

## Online Store

The storefront displays products, categories, and approved store content. Before publishing, check the product title, image, price, selected variant, and availability. Preview the store where possible.

## Mobile and Social Channels

The storefront may be viewed on mobile, and social connections may help shoppers find products. Check mobile presentation and configured social links. Do not assume social posts or an external channel update BeautyComm records automatically.

## Sales and Channel Records

Use the configured process to record sales from each channel. Check the order record before creating another entry, and confirm stock and reporting use the intended store context.

## Troubleshoot Visibility

If a product is missing, check its active state, store or channel, category, variant, and stock. If information from another channel is delayed, review its sync status and compare with the underlying order before escalating.
