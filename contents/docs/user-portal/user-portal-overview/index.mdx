## Understanding the User Portal

The User Portal brings together the business workflows available to your organization, such as product, inventory, order, customer, and reporting tasks. Not every account or role has the same features.

## Navigating the Main Dashboard

Use the dashboard as a starting point to review available summaries, notifications, or shortcuts. Treat displayed figures as an overview and open the related area when you need record-level detail.

## Understanding the Main Navigation

The main navigation groups related work into sections. Select the area that matches your task, then use available search, filters, and record details to continue. Menu names may vary with configuration.

## Account Access and Permissions

Your role determines which records and actions are available. If a section or action is missing, confirm the correct account and business context, then ask your administrator to review access.
