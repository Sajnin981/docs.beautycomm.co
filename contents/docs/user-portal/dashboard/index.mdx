## Dashboard

After you Sign in, you stay on the Dashboard. It greets you and shows options such as My Orders, Account Details, Verify Product, My Coupons, My WishList and My Rewards. While you are logged in, selecting the profile icon always brings you back here.

![User portal dashboard](/images/user-portal/dashboard.jpg)

- A greeting card at the top welcomes you by name.

![Dashboard cards](/images/user-portal/dashboard-cards.jpg)

- Tiles below it link to **My Orders**, **Account Details**, **Verify Product**, **My Coupons**, **My WishList** and **My Rewards**.
- The same links sit in the left sidebar, with **Logout** at the bottom, so you can switch pages without returning here.
- Select any summary card to open the full page behind it.

## Profile & Settings

Open Account Details to keep your personal information current.

1. Update your name, phone number, email or profile photo.
2. To change the password, enter the current password first and the new password twice.
3. Select Save changes and verify the updated information appears afterwards.

## Notifications

Notifications keep you informed about orders, offers and support replies.

- Open Notifications to see alerts newest first.
- Select an order, coupon or support entry to open its related page.
- Mark alerts as read after you have reviewed them.

## Help & Support

Help & Support is the quickest way to get an answer about an order, product or account.

- Call the support number shown in the header.
- Use the chat button to ask about skincare, hair care or an order.
- Open Contact from the menu bar to send a message.
- Have the order number ready so the team can find the purchase faster.
