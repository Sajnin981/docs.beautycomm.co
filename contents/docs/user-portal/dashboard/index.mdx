This dashboard is for the **business owner, administrator, or staff member** operating a BeautyComm-powered store. Depending on your role, it may summarize order activity, recent orders, top-selling products, stock value, category performance, notifications, or work that needs attention. It is not the shopper’s account dashboard; shoppers should use [Manage Your Customer Account](/docs/user-portal/customer-account). The business dashboard is a summary, so open the underlying record or report before making an operational decision.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard brings selected information together so teams can see what may need attention. Common examples in the BeautyComm store include order trends, recent orders, products performing well, stock information, and pending tasks such as low stock or unprocessed orders. Not every account displays every tile.

Start with the date range and business context shown on the dashboard. If information looks incomplete, check whether a filter or permission affects the view before concluding that a record is missing.

## Metrics and Shortcuts

Metrics summarize activity over a specific time range or scope. For example, order totals and trends can help teams understand volume; product and category summaries can help identify items attracting attention; stock figures can support replenishment planning.

To read a metric responsibly:

1. Check the period and any active filters.
2. Confirm whether the value is a count, amount, rate, or summary.
3. Compare like periods and the same business scope.
4. Open the relevant report or record to investigate an unexpected change.

Do not treat a dashboard total as a complete financial or inventory reconciliation unless the corresponding report is designed for that purpose.

Shortcuts provide a faster route to orders, products, or other work available to your role. If a shortcut is missing, use the main navigation.

## Notifications and Actions

Notifications may flag order activity, stock concerns, or another event configured for the account. Read the message, note its time, and open the related record to confirm current details. A notification is an alert, not a replacement for the source record.

1. Identify the alert and its related record, if provided.
2. Open the underlying order, product, or report.
3. Confirm that the information is current and relevant to your role.
4. Complete only actions you are authorized to perform.
5. Recheck the record after the action to confirm the result.
