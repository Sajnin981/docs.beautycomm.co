Your dashboard is the home of your account. It gives you a quick view of your orders, reward points, wishlist and shortcuts. To use it:

1. Log in and click your name or profile icon, then select **Dashboard**.
2. Check the summary cards at the top for recent orders, reward points and saved items.
3. Use the left menu to move to **My Orders**, **Wishlist**, **Addresses**, **Coupons** and other sections.
4. Click any recent order to open its details.

Use the dashboard whenever you want to jump quickly to the part of your account you need.
