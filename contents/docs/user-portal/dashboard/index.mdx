Your dashboard is your personal space in the BeautyComm store. It gives you a quick overview of your account, so you can check your orders, coupons, rewards and saved products in one place.

To open it, log in and click the account icon at the top right of the page. See [Registration & Login](/docs/user-portal/registration-and-login) for the steps.

## Dashboard menu

The menu on the left lets you move between the sections of your account:

- **Dashboard:** your account home page.
- **My Orders:** your recent and pending orders and their status.
- **Account Details:** your name, phone number and other profile information.
- **Verify Product:** check that a BeautyComm product is genuine.
- **My Coupons:** the discount coupons available to you.
- **My WishList:** the products you have saved for later.
- **My Rewards:** your reward points.
- **Logout:** sign out of your account.

![Dashboard menu on the left side of the page](/images/user-portal/dashboard/dashboard.jpg)

## Dashboard overview

The main area of the dashboard greets you by name. Below the greeting, shortcut cards take you straight to **My Orders**, **Account Details**, **Verify Product**, **My Coupons**, **My WishList** and **My Rewards**. Click a card to open that section.

![Dashboard overview with account shortcut cards](/images/user-portal/dashboard/dashboard-cards.jpg)

You can come back to your dashboard at any time by clicking your profile picture at the top right.
