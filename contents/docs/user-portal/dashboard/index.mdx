## Overview

This page is for the signed-in customer dashboard, not the business administration dashboard. Its exact contents and availability require product-owner confirmation.

## Purpose and intended audience

The proposed scope is a personal summary of account information and relevant shopping activity. Administrative metrics, staff shortcuts, sales reporting, and stock alerts belong in the [Admin Portal](/docs/admin-portal/dashboard-and-best-practices).

## Access, interface, and workflows

Confirm the account navigation path, displayed activity, and available actions before adding instructions.

## Related documentation

See [My Orders](/docs/user-portal/my-orders) for personal order history and [My Profile & Account Settings](/docs/user-portal/my-profile-and-account-settings) for account settings once verified.
