The dashboard is your starting point. It gives a quick view of how your shop is doing.

## Purpose

Show key numbers and shortcuts so you can act quickly.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Select **Dashboard** in the main navigation, or sign in. It is usually the first page you see.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to BeautyComm.

## Steps

1. Open the dashboard.

2. Review the summary cards, such as sales, orders or stock indicators, if shown.
3. Select a card or shortcut to open the related list.
4. Check notifications for items that need attention.

## Expected result

You know the current state of your shop and can open the area that needs attention.

## Notes

- Available cards depend on your role and enabled features.
- Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Numbers look different from reports | Dashboard and reports may use different date ranges. Check the date range shown. |
