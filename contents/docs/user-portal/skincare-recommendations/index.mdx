Get a routine suited to your skin. To receive recommendations:

1. Open **Skincare Recommendations**.
2. Select your skin type, concerns and preferences.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Skincare Recommendations screenshot" label="Capture for Skincare Recommendations" description="Capture: Skin profile questionnaire. Suggested filename: skincare/01-questionnaire.png" caption="skincare/01-questionnaire.png" />

3. Click **Get Recommendations**.
4. Review the suggested products and routine, then add items to your cart or wishlist.

You can update your answers any time to refresh your suggestions.
