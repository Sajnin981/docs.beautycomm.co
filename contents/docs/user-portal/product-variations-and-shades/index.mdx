Many products come in different shades, sizes or types. To choose a variation:

1. Open the product page.
2. Find the **Shade** or **Size** selector below the price.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Product Variations &amp; Shades screenshot" label="Capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Capture: Shade swatch selector. Suggested filename: variations/01-shade-selector.png" caption="variations/01-shade-selector.png" />

3. Click the shade or size you want. The image, price and stock status update automatically.

4. If a variation shows **Out of Stock**, choose another or use **Notify Me** if available.
5. Click **Add to Cart**.

The cart shows exactly the shade or size you selected.
