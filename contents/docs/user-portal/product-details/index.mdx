Every product page shows what you need to decide before buying. To read a product page:

1. Click a product from the listing or search results.
2. Check the gallery at the top. Click a thumbnail to change the main image or zoom in.
3. Read the price, discount, rating and stock status next to the images.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Product Details screenshot" label="Capture for Product Details" description="Capture: Product page with callouts on gallery, price, rating and stock status. Suggested filename: product-details/01-top-section.png" caption="product-details/01-top-section.png" />

4. Scroll down for the **Description**, **Ingredients**, **How to Use** and **Reviews** tabs.

5. Choose a quantity, then click **Add to Cart** or **Buy Now**.

Check the ingredients and reviews before buying, especially for skincare products.
