The User Portal is for shoppers using an online store powered by BeautyComm. It brings personal shopping activity and account guidance together, from sign-in and profile details to orders, saved products, rewards, and support.

Use the [Storefront](/docs/storefront) guides for public browsing and checkout. Business owners and staff should use the [Admin Panel](/docs/admin-portal) for store operations.

## Who Should Use This Section

Use these guides if you are buying from a store’s website or mobile shopping experience. You do not need a BeautyComm staff or business-admin account to follow shopper instructions.

The store controls its own products, prices, delivery areas, payment methods, promotions, and return policies. Available options may differ from one BeautyComm-powered store to another, so confirm the details with the store before relying on a particular workflow.
