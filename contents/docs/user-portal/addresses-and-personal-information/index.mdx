Save your delivery addresses so checkout is faster. To manage addresses:

1. Go to **Dashboard** and select **Addresses**.

2. Click **Add New Address**.
3. Enter your name, phone number, district or city, area and full address, then click **Save**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Addresses &amp; Personal Information screenshot" label="Capture for Addresses &amp; Personal Information" description="Capture: Add address form. Suggested filename: addresses/02-add-address.png" caption="addresses/02-add-address.png" />

4. To edit or remove an address, click **Edit** or **Delete** next to it.
5. To use an address by default, click **Set as Default**.

Your default address is selected automatically at checkout, and you can change it any time.
