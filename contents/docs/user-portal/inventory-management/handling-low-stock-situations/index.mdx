Learn about handling low-stock situations. Inventory is the count of how many units of each product you have available to sell. This guide covers **Handling Low-Stock Situations** in the User Portal.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to handling low-stock situations.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open the User Portal and look for the area related to handling low-stock situations. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.
