Keep stock counts accurate so you never oversell.

## Purpose

Show how to change the quantity available for a product or variant.

## Who can use it

Users whose role allows inventory updates.

## Where to find it

Open **Inventory**, or open a product and look at its stock field.

## Before you begin

- The SKU or product name.
- The correct quantity, from a stock count or delivery.

## Steps

1. Open inventory or the product.
2. Find the product or variant.
3. Enter the new quantity, or the amount to add or remove, as the screen allows.

<Screenshot step={3} alt="Stock quantity field" label="Stock quantity field" />

4. Save the change.
5. Check that the stock figure updated.

## Expected result

The product shows the new stock level and its availability updates.

## Notes

- Count physical stock before updating.
- Orders reduce stock automatically when placed.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Stock goes back to the old number | Refresh and check for pending orders or an unsaved form. |
