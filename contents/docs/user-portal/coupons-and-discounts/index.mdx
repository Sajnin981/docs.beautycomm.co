Save money with coupons and discounts. To use a coupon:

1. Open **Dashboard** and select **Coupons** to see available coupons.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Coupons &amp; Discounts screenshot" label="Capture for Coupons &amp; Discounts" description="Capture: Coupons page with available coupons. Suggested filename: coupons/01-coupon-list.png" caption="coupons/01-coupon-list.png" />

2. Click **Collect** on a coupon to save it, and check its minimum spend and expiry date.
3. In your cart or at checkout, enter the code in the **Coupon** field, or select it from the list, and click **Apply**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Coupons &amp; Discounts screenshot" label="Capture for Coupons &amp; Discounts" description="Capture: Coupon field in the cart with a successful discount. Suggested filename: coupons/02-apply-coupon.png" caption="coupons/02-apply-coupon.png" />

4. The discount appears in your order summary.

Only one coupon may be usable per order, depending on the offer terms.
