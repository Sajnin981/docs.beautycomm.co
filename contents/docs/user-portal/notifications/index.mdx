Stay updated on orders, offers and account activity. To manage notifications:

1. Click the **bell icon** at the top of the page.

2. Click a notification to open the related order or offer.
3. To change what you receive, open **Account Settings**, select **Notifications**, and turn email, SMS or push notifications on or off.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Notifications screenshot" label="Capture for Notifications" description="Capture: Notification settings toggles. Suggested filename: notifications/02-settings.png" caption="notifications/02-settings.png" />

You can mute promotions while still receiving order updates.
