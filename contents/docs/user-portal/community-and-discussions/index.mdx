## Discussions

Discussions are where customers ask questions and share beauty experiences.

1. Open Discussions and browse the topics, or search for one.
2. Select a topic to read the conversation.
3. Sign in, then write a reply or start a new topic.
4. Keep posts respectful. The store team can remove inappropriate content.

Please follow the community guidelines and be respectful.
