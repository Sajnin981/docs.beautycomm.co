Ask questions and share experiences with other beauty lovers. To join a discussion:

1. Open **Community** from the main menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Community &amp; Discussions screenshot" label="Capture for Community &amp; Discussions" description="Capture: Community feed. Suggested filename: community/01-feed.png" caption="community/01-feed.png" />

2. Click a topic to read it, then use **Reply** or **Like**.
3. To start a new topic, click **New Post**, add a title and message, and click **Post**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Community &amp; Discussions screenshot" label="Capture for Community &amp; Discussions" description="Capture: New post form. Suggested filename: community/02-new-post.png" caption="community/02-new-post.png" />

4. To report inappropriate content, click **Report** on the post.

Please follow the community guidelines and be respectful.
