Keep your personal details and account security up to date from your profile. To edit your profile:

1. Open your **Dashboard** and click **My Profile**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Profile &amp; Account Settings screenshot" label="Capture for My Profile &amp; Account Settings" description="Capture: Profile page with the menu item highlighted. Suggested filename: profile/01-profile-page.png" caption="profile/01-profile-page.png" />

2. Click **Edit Profile** and update your name, email, phone number or profile photo.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Profile &amp; Account Settings screenshot" label="Capture for My Profile &amp; Account Settings" description="Capture: Edit profile form including the photo upload button. Suggested filename: profile/02-edit-profile.png" caption="profile/02-edit-profile.png" />

3. Click **Save Changes**.
4. To change your password, open **Account Settings**, click **Change Password**, enter your current password and your new password, then click **Update Password**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Profile &amp; Account Settings screenshot" label="Capture for My Profile &amp; Account Settings" description="Capture: Change password form. Suggested filename: profile/03-change-password.png" caption="profile/03-change-password.png" />

5. To delete your account, open **Account Settings** and click **Delete Account**, then confirm. This cannot be undone.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Profile &amp; Account Settings screenshot" label="Capture for My Profile &amp; Account Settings" description="Capture: Delete account confirmation dialog. Suggested filename: profile/04-delete-account.png" caption="profile/04-delete-account.png" />

Your changes apply immediately across the store and the mobile app.
