Keep your personal details and password up to date from your account page.

## Update your profile

1. Log in and open your dashboard, then click **Account Details** in the left menu. The **My Account** page opens with the **Update Profile** form.

   ![Account Details in the dashboard menu](/images/user-portal/profile/account-details-menu.jpg)

2. To change your profile picture, click the edit icon on the picture and choose a photo from your device.

   ![Edit icon on the profile picture](/images/user-portal/profile/edit-photo.jpg)

3. To change your name, edit the **Full Name** field. Your name appears in the greeting on your dashboard.

   ![Full Name field on the Update Profile form](/images/user-portal/profile/full-name.jpg)

4. Check your phone number or email shown below the name. It is shown for reference and cannot be edited on this page.
5. Click **Update Profile** to save your changes.

   ![Update Profile button](/images/user-portal/profile/update-profile.jpg)

## Update your password

Scroll down to the **Update Password** section on the same page.

1. Enter your new password in the **New Password** field. It must be at least 8 characters long.

   ![New Password field](/images/user-portal/profile/new-password.jpg)

2. Re-enter the same password in the **Confirm New Password** field.

   ![Confirm New Password field](/images/user-portal/profile/confirm-password.jpg)

3. Click **Update Password** to save it.

   ![Update Password button](/images/user-portal/profile/update-password.jpg)

Use your new password the next time you choose **Login With Password**. Choose a password you do not use on other websites.
