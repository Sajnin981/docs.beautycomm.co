## Payment Details and Status

An order may show payment method, amount, reference, and transaction status. Payment status is different from order status. Confirm the current transaction before retrying a payment or promising a refund.

## Shipping and Fulfillment

Check the delivery address, method, and order items before dispatch. Follow store policy for address changes, cancellations, and returns. Update fulfillment information only when the relevant step has occurred.

## Tracking an Order

Use the order record and configured delivery integration to review tracking. Providers such as Pathao or RedX are available only when configured for your store. Check the latest status before updating the customer.

<Screenshot alt="BeautyComm shipping and tracking information" label="Add a screenshot of delivery tracking with personal details hidden" />

## Resolve a Payment or Delivery Question

Confirm the order reference, date, payment state, and delivery state. Compare the order with available transaction or tracking information. If details do not match, record the error and use your business’s support process.
