See every order you have placed and its current status. To view your orders:

1. Open **Dashboard** and select **My Orders**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Orders screenshot" label="Capture for My Orders" description="Capture: My Orders list with status badges. Suggested filename: orders/01-order-list.png" caption="orders/01-order-list.png" />

2. Use the tabs or filters to view All, Pending, Shipped, Delivered or Cancelled orders.
3. Click an order to see its items, delivery address, payment details and total.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Orders screenshot" label="Capture for My Orders" description="Capture: Order details page. Suggested filename: orders/02-order-details.png" caption="orders/02-order-details.png" />

4. To cancel an order that has not shipped yet, click **Cancel Order** and choose a reason.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm My Orders screenshot" label="Capture for My Orders" description="Capture: Cancel order dialog. Suggested filename: orders/03-cancel-order.png" caption="orders/03-cancel-order.png" />

Your order list is always available in your account, so you can check it any time.
