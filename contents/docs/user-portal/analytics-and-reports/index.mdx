## Choose a Report

Reports may cover sales, orders, products, suppliers, categories, customers, or other activity. Select a report that matches your business question and confirm the store and period it covers.

## Read Sales and Product Results

Check whether a value is a count, amount, average, or percentage. Compare equivalent periods with the same filters. Consider promotions and stock availability when reviewing product performance. Open a source record if a number looks unexpected.

## Customer Reports and Exports

Use aggregated customer information where possible. Export or download only when available and approved. Store files in an approved location and share them only with authorized people; remove personal fields that are not needed.

## Fix a Report Problem

Check the date range, filters, and store context. Refresh the report and compare a sample with the underlying order or product. If the issue remains, record the report name, filters, time, and error before contacting support.
