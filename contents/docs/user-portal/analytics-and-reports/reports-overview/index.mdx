Reports summarize your sales, products and customers.

## Purpose

Help you open and read reports.

## Who can use it

Users whose role includes reports.

## Where to find it

Open **Reports** or **Analytics**, if available to you.

## Before you begin

- Decide the date range you want to review.

## Steps

1. Open reports.
2. Choose a report.

3. Set the date range.
4. Review numbers and charts.

## Expected result

You see figures for the range you chose.

## Notes

- Compare equal periods, such as one month with the previous month.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Numbers differ from dashboard | Check that date ranges match. |
