Save products you like and come back to them later. To use your wishlist:

1. Click the **heart icon** on any product card or product page to save it.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Wishlist &amp; Saved Products screenshot" label="Capture for Wishlist &amp; Saved Products" description="Capture: Product card with the heart icon highlighted. Suggested filename: wishlist/01-heart-icon.png" caption="wishlist/01-heart-icon.png" />

2. To view saved items, open **Dashboard** and select **Wishlist**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Wishlist &amp; Saved Products screenshot" label="Capture for Wishlist &amp; Saved Products" description="Capture: Wishlist page with several products. Suggested filename: wishlist/02-wishlist-page.png" caption="wishlist/02-wishlist-page.png" />

3. Click **Add to Cart** next to a product to buy it, or click the heart again to remove it.

Check your wishlist during sales to catch discounts on products you saved.
