Share your experience to help other shoppers. To write a review:

1. Open **My Orders** and select a delivered order.
2. Click **Write a Review** next to the product.

3. Choose a star rating, write your comments and upload photos if you like.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Product Reviews &amp; Ratings screenshot" label="Capture for Product Reviews &amp; Ratings" description="Capture: Review form with rating, text box and photo upload. Suggested filename: reviews/02-review-form.png" caption="reviews/02-review-form.png" />

4. Click **Submit**. Your review appears on the product page after approval.

Honest, detailed reviews help others choose the right product.
