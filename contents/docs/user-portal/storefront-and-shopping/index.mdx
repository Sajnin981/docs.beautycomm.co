## Find Products

The storefront home page helps customers discover the store and its products. Depending on the current configuration, it may display promotional banners, featured products, categories, popular items, editorial content, or links to store information.

Start by choosing a category or using the store search. Promotional content can change over time, so review the offer details and validity before relying on a discount.

<Screenshot alt="BeautyComm storefront home page" label="Add a screenshot of the BeautyComm storefront home page" />

You can find products by browsing the available categories, subcategories, or brands, or by using search. Where the store provides filters, narrow results by options such as price, rating, availability, or new arrivals. Sorting controls may organize results by popularity, price, or recency.

To find an item:

1. Open the relevant category or enter a product name in search.
2. Apply available filters to narrow the results.
3. Sort the list if you want a particular order.
4. Open a product card to review the full product details.

If you do not see an expected product, clear restrictive filters and check another category or search term. Availability can change, and some products may not be listed for every store or customer.

## Review Product Details

Before choosing a product, review its available images, description, specifications, price, discount information, and stock or availability indication. Some products have variants such as shade, size, color, or weight; choose the required option before adding the item to your cart.

Product pages may also show ratings, reviews, delivery information, and related products. Use the product details as the primary reference for what is being offered. If a variation is unclear or the information appears inconsistent, contact the store before placing the order.

## Cart and Checkout

The cart gives you a chance to review your selection before checkout. Check each item, variant, quantity, and displayed price. If the store supports promotional codes, apply the code in the cart or checkout and confirm that the discount appears in the order summary.

You can remove an item or change its quantity before continuing. Availability and price may change before an order is submitted, so review the latest summary rather than relying on an earlier product page.

Checkout collects the information needed to submit the order. Review the delivery address, item summary, applicable charges, promotion, and final total before confirming. The payment methods shown are the methods currently available to the store and your order; they can vary.

Typical checkout sequence:

1. Sign in or provide the customer details requested by the store.
2. Select or enter a delivery address.
3. Review shipping or delivery charges shown for that address.
4. Apply an eligible promotion or reward if the checkout offers it.
5. Choose one of the payment methods presented.
6. Review the complete order summary and submit the order.
7. Save the order reference shown after successful submission.

Do not submit payment more than once if the page appears to pause. Check for an order confirmation first, then contact the store’s support channel if the result is unclear.

## Offers and Store Information

The storefront may show campaigns, discount banners, customer reviews, recommendations, or trust information. The exact content depends on the store. Check promotion terms, dates, eligible products, and any minimum conditions before applying an offer.

Store pages may also provide contact details, shipping or refund information, privacy terms, and other policies. Review these details before purchasing if you need to understand delivery, returns, or how the store handles customer information.

## Fix a Shopping Problem

If search returns no results, clear filters and try a broader term. If a cart total changes, review quantities, promotion eligibility, and delivery charges. If checkout does not complete, check for a confirmation or order reference before retrying. For a payment or order issue, contact the store through its published support channel and include the time and any order reference. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or full payment-card details in a support message.
