Find the right product quickly using search, categories and filters. To search and filter:

1. Type a product, brand or concern (for example, "sunscreen") in the search bar and press Enter.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Search, Categories &amp; Filters screenshot" label="Capture for Search, Categories &amp; Filters" description="Capture: Search bar with search suggestions. Suggested filename: search/01-search-bar.png" caption="search/01-search-bar.png" />

2. To browse by category, open the **Categories** menu and choose one, such as Skincare or Makeup.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Search, Categories &amp; Filters screenshot" label="Capture for Search, Categories &amp; Filters" description="Capture: Categories menu expanded. Suggested filename: search/02-categories-menu.png" caption="search/02-categories-menu.png" />

3. On the results page, use the filter panel to narrow results by brand, price, skin type, rating and more.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Search, Categories &amp; Filters screenshot" label="Capture for Search, Categories &amp; Filters" description="Capture: Filter panel with a few filters selected. Suggested filename: search/03-filters.png" caption="search/03-filters.png" />

4. To remove filters, click the **x** on a filter tag or click **Clear All**.

Combine filters to reach the products that match your needs faster.
