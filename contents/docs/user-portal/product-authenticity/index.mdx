BeautyComm helps you confirm that your product is genuine. To verify a product:

1. Look for the authenticity badge on the product page, or the code or QR label on your product packaging.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Product Authenticity screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity" description="Capture: Authenticity badge on a product page. Suggested filename: authenticity/01-badge.png" caption="authenticity/01-badge.png" />

2. Open **Verify Product** in the store or app and scan the QR code or enter the code.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Product Authenticity screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity" description="Capture: Verification screen. Suggested filename: authenticity/02-verify-screen.png" caption="authenticity/02-verify-screen.png" />

3. The result shows whether the product is verified.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Product Authenticity screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity" description="Capture: Verification result screen. Suggested filename: authenticity/03-result.png" caption="authenticity/03-result.png" />

If a product cannot be verified, contact Customer Support.
