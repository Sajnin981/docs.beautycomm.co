Learn about viewing your profile. Your profile and account settings control how you sign in and how BeautyComm shows information to you. This guide covers **Viewing Your Profile** in the User Portal.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to viewing your profile.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open the User Portal and look for the area related to viewing your profile. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.
