## Profile and Contact Details

Confirm the correct business account and update only the fields that your role allows. Review contact details before saving. Ask your administrator to correct locked or business-managed information.

## Password and Sign-In

Use your business’s approved password-change or recovery process. Choose a unique password and never share it, a reset link, or a verification code. Sign out when using a shared device.

## Preferences and Notifications

Account preferences depend on role and configuration. Check the available options and see [Notifications and Communication](/docs/user-portal/notifications-and-communication) for notification settings.

## Access Problems

Confirm the right account, business context, and network. If a feature is missing, ask an administrator to check that the account is active and has the correct role. Do not solve access problems by sharing credentials or granting unnecessary permissions.
