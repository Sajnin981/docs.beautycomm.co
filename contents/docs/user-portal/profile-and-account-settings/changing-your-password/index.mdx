Change your password regularly and whenever you think it may be exposed.

## Purpose

Show how to change your password.

## Who can use it

Any signed-in user.

## Where to find it

Open your profile or account settings.

## Before you begin

- Your current password.

## Steps

1. Open your account settings.
2. Choose the password option.

3. Enter your current and new password.
4. Save.

## Expected result

The new password works at your next sign-in.

## Notes

- Use a long, unique password.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Password rejected | Make it longer and add variety. |
