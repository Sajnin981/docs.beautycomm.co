Learn about managing account preferences. Your profile and account settings control how you sign in and how BeautyComm shows information to you. This guide covers **Managing Account Preferences** in the User Portal.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to managing account preferences.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open the User Portal and look for the area related to managing account preferences. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.

## Steps

Detailed step-by-step instructions for this guide are being prepared and will be published once they are verified against the live product.

## Expected result

You can find the related area and understand what it offers.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot see this area | Your role may not include it. Ask your account administrator. |
| The page does not load | Refresh the page, then sign out and back in. If it continues, contact support. |
