Variants let one product come in several versions, such as shades or sizes.

## Purpose

Explain variants, attributes and SKUs.

## Who can use it

Users whose role allows editing products.

## Where to find it

Open a product, then look for its variants or attributes section.

## Before you begin

- An existing product or a product you are creating.

## Steps

1. Open the product.
2. Choose the attribute that differs, such as shade or size.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="Variant options for a product" label="Variant options for a product" />

3. Add each value, for example "Ivory" and "Sand".
4. Give each variant its own SKU, price and stock if needed.
5. Save the product.

## Expected result

Shoppers can choose a version, and stock is tracked for each variant.

## Notes

- **Attribute:** a property such as shade. **Variant:** one specific combination of values.
- A **SKU** should be unique to each variant.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Stock is shared across shades | Check that each shade is its own variant with its own stock. |
