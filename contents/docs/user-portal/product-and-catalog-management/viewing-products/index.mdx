View your product list to check what is in your catalog.

## Purpose

Let you find and review products and their basic details.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Open **Products** in the main navigation.

## Before you begin

- Your role must include access to products.

## Steps

1. Open the products area.

<Screenshot step={1} alt="Product list" label="Product list" />

2. Scroll the list or use search to find a product by name or SKU.
3. Use filters, such as category or status, to narrow the list.
4. Select a product to open its details.

## Expected result

You see the products you want and can open their details.

## Notes

- Products that are not published may still appear in the list for you but not to shoppers.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| A product is missing | Clear filters, then check whether it is unpublished or in another category. |
