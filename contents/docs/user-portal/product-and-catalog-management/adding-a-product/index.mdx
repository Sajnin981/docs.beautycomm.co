Add a product so shoppers can see and buy it.

## Purpose

Walk you through creating a new product with the information shoppers need.

## Who can use it

Users whose role allows creating products.

## Where to find it

Open **Products**, then look for the option to add a product.

## Before you begin

- Product name and description.
- Price and, if you track stock, the starting quantity.
- Product images.
- The category the product belongs in.

## Steps

1. Open the products area and choose to add a new product.

2. Enter the product name and description.
3. Set the price and, if used, the SKU.
4. Choose a category.
5. Add images.
6. Add variants such as shades or sizes if the product has them.
7. Save the product. Publish it when you are ready for shoppers to see it.

## Expected result

The product appears in your product list and, once published, in your shop.

## Notes

- Save as a draft if you are not ready to publish.
- Use clear, accurate names so shoppers can find the product.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot save | Check required fields and look for error messages next to them. |
| Product not visible in shop | Confirm it is published and in stock. |
