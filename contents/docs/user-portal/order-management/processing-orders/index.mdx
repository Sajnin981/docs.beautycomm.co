Processing an order means checking it, preparing it and moving it forward.

## Purpose

Show a typical order workflow.

## Who can use it

Users whose role allows order management.

## Where to find it

Open **Orders** in the main navigation.

## Before you begin

- Order is paid or approved according to your process.
- Stock is available.

## Steps

1. Open the order list and select the order.

<Screenshot step={1} alt="Order details screen" label="Order details screen" />

2. Check customer, items and payment status.
3. Prepare the items.
4. Update the order status as your process requires.
5. Add shipping or tracking information if available.

<Screenshot step={5} alt="Shipping information" label="Shipping information" />

6. Save changes.

## Expected result

The order moves to the next status and the customer can be informed.

## Notes

- Only change statuses you are authorized to change.
- Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot update status | Your role may be read-only for orders. |
