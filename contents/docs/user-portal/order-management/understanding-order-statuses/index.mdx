An order status tells you what stage an order has reached.

## Purpose

Help you read order and payment statuses correctly.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Statuses appear in the order list and on each order.

## Before you begin

- Open the order list.

## Steps

1. Open the order list.
2. Look at the status column.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="Status column in the order list" label="Status column in the order list" />

3. Open an order to see its full status.

## Expected result

You understand what each status means and what to do next. The statuses below are typical examples; your account may use different names.

| Example status | Typical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Order placed, waiting for payment or confirmation. |
| Paid | Payment received. |
| Processing | Being prepared. |
| Shipped | Handed to a carrier. |
| Delivered | Received by the customer. |
| Cancelled | Stopped before completion. |
| Refunded | Payment returned. |

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Status did not change | Refresh. Some statuses update when payment or shipping updates arrive. |
