Quickly find a customer record when helping with an order or question.

## Purpose

Show how to search the customer list.

## Who can use it

Users whose role includes customers.

## Where to find it

Open **Customers** in the main navigation.

## Before you begin

- Customer name, email or phone number.

## Steps

1. Open the customers area.
2. Type into the search box.

3. Select the matching customer.

## Expected result

The customer record opens with their details and purchases.

## Notes

- Handle customer data carefully and only for work purposes.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No results | Try a shorter search or another detail such as email. |
