## Find a Customer

Use the customer list or search with an available identifier such as a name, email, or phone number. Compare the result with the order or support context to make sure it is the right person. Clear filters if a record is missing.

## Review a Customer Record

A record may show contact details, account status, order history, rewards, or communication preferences. Use the order record to confirm transaction-specific information before responding to a question.

## Update Customer Information

Change only fields that are editable, verified, and permitted for your role. Confirm the selected customer and review the result after saving. For a bulk import, check the approved source, required format, and result for errors or duplicates.

## Customer Privacy and Imports

Access only the information needed for your task. Do not copy or export customer details unless your role and business policy allow it. Hide unrelated personal information in screenshots and use approved storage for any permitted exports.
