## Register

Create a BeautyComm account to track orders, save your wishlist and earn reward points.

1. Select the profile icon at the top right of the store. The login page opens.
2. Choose the option to create a new account.
3. Enter your name, phone number, email and a password.
4. Agree to the terms and submit.
5. You stay on your Dashboard when it is done.

## Login

1. Select the profile icon at the top right of the store. The login page opens.
2. Enter your phone number or email, and your password.
3. Select Login. You stay on your Dashboard, where you see your greeting and options such as My Orders and Account Details.
4. Select Home in the menu to go back to the shop.
5. To return to your Dashboard, select the same profile icon. You are already logged in, so you don’t need to log in again.
