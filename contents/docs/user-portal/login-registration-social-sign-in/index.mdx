## Register

Create a BeautyComm account to track orders, save your wishlist and earn reward points.

1. Open the store and select the profile (account) icon at the top right.
   ![Home icon](/images/user-portal/home-icon.jpg)
2. The Login and Register page opens. Select the **Register** tab.
   ![Registration page](/images/user-portal/register.jpg)
3. Enter your phone number and select **Send OTP**.
4. Enter the verification code (OTP) you receive.
5. When it is verified, your account is ready and you stay on your Dashboard.

## Sign In

1. Open the store and select the profile (account) icon at the top right.
   ![Sign In page](/images/user-portal/login.jpg)
2. The Login and Register page opens with the **Sign In** tab selected.
3. Enter your phone number and select **Send OTP**, then enter the code you receive.
4. Or select **Login with Password** to sign in with a password.
5. If you don’t have an account, select **Register**.
6. When you are logged in, you stay on your Dashboard, where you see your greeting and options such as My Orders and Account Details. Log out on any shared device.
7. Select Home in the menu to go back to the shop.
8. To return to your Dashboard, select the same profile icon. You are already logged in, so you don’t need to sign in again.
