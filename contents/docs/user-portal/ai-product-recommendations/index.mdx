BeautyComm suggests products based on your browsing, wishlist and purchases. To see your recommendations:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Look for **Recommended for You** on the homepage and on product pages.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm AI Product Recommendations screenshot" label="Capture for AI Product Recommendations" description="Capture: Recommended for You section on the homepage. Suggested filename: recommendations/01-homepage.png" caption="recommendations/01-homepage.png" />

3. Click a recommended product to view it, or add it to your cart or wishlist.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm AI Product Recommendations screenshot" label="Capture for AI Product Recommendations" description="Capture: Recommendations carousel on a product page. Suggested filename: recommendations/02-product-page.png" caption="recommendations/02-product-page.png" />

The more you browse and shop, the better your recommendations become.
