Help & Support is the quickest way to get an answer about an order, product or account.

- Call the support number shown at the top of the page.
- Select the chat button at the bottom right to ask about skincare, hair care or an order.
- Use the Contact page in the menu to send a message.
- Check the FAQs for a quick answer before submitting a ticket.
- Have your order number ready so the team can find your purchase faster.

Include your order number to get help faster.
