Get help when you need it. To contact support:

1. Open **Support** from the main menu or the footer.

2. Check the FAQs for a quick answer.
3. If you need more help, click **Contact Us** or **Submit a Ticket**, choose a topic, describe the issue and attach screenshots if useful.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Customer Support screenshot" label="Capture for Customer Support" description="Capture: Support ticket form. Suggested filename: support/02-ticket-form.png" caption="support/02-ticket-form.png" />

4. Track replies from your account or by email.

Include your order number to get help faster.
