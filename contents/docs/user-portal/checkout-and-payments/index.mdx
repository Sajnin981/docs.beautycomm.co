Complete your purchase securely in a few steps. To place an order:

1. From the cart, click **Proceed to Checkout**.
2. Select your delivery address or add a new one.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Checkout &amp; Payments screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout &amp; Payments" description="Capture: Checkout step with address selection. Suggested filename: checkout/01-address.png" caption="checkout/01-address.png" />

3. Choose a delivery method.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Checkout &amp; Payments screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout &amp; Payments" description="Capture: Delivery method selection. Suggested filename: checkout/02-delivery.png" caption="checkout/02-delivery.png" />

4. Choose a payment method, such as card, mobile banking or cash on delivery.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Checkout &amp; Payments screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout &amp; Payments" description="Capture: Payment method list. Suggested filename: checkout/03-payment.png" caption="checkout/03-payment.png" />

5. Review your order summary, then click **Place Order**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Checkout &amp; Payments screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout &amp; Payments" description="Capture: Final review page with Place Order button. Suggested filename: checkout/04-review.png" caption="checkout/04-review.png" />

6. Your order confirmation page appears, and you also receive a confirmation by email or SMS.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Checkout &amp; Payments screenshot" label="Capture for Checkout &amp; Payments" description="Capture: Order confirmation screen. Suggested filename: checkout/05-confirmation.png" caption="checkout/05-confirmation.png" />

Keep your order number to track your order later.
