## Cart

Your cart collects everything you plan to buy and shows the running total before you pay.

1. Select **Add To Cart** on any product. A number badge appears on the cart icon in the header.
2. Select the cart icon to open the Cart page.
3. Use the minus and plus buttons beside each item to change the quantity.
4. Select the red **X** to remove an item you no longer want.
5. Check the Cart Total at the bottom, then select **Proceed To Checkout**.
   ![Cart page with quantity buttons and Cart Total](/images/user-portal/cart.jpg)

## Checkout

Checkout is where you confirm delivery details, apply a coupon and choose how to pay.

1. Under Billing Details, fill in your Name and Phone. Email address is optional.
   ![Checkout page with delivery form, order summary and payment options](/images/user-portal/checkout.jpg)
2. Choose your City, then the Zone and Area that appear after it.
3. Type your full Address and add a note under **Notes about your order** if needed. Or select **Change Delivery Address** to pick a saved address. Fields marked with * are required.
4. Review the order summary: Subtotal, Shipping Cost, Discount and Total.
5. Enter a coupon code and select **Apply Coupon** if you have one, or select **Redeem Points** under Your Balance to use reward points. Check that the Discount line updates.
6. Pick a payment method: **Cash on Delivery**, **bKash** or **Pay Online**.
7. Tick the box to agree to the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy.
8. Select **Place My Order** and wait for the confirmation message.
