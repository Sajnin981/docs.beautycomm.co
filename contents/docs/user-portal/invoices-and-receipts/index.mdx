Download your invoice for any completed order. To get an invoice:

1. Open **My Orders** and click the order.
2. Click **Download Invoice** (or **Print Invoice**).

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Invoices &amp; Receipts screenshot" label="Capture for Invoices &amp; Receipts" description="Capture: Order details with the invoice button highlighted. Suggested filename: invoices/01-invoice-button.png" caption="invoices/01-invoice-button.png" />

3. The invoice opens as a PDF. Save or print it.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Invoices &amp; Receipts screenshot" label="Capture for Invoices &amp; Receipts" description="Capture: Sample invoice (hide personal details). Suggested filename: invoices/02-sample-invoice.png" caption="invoices/02-sample-invoice.png" />

Keep your invoice for warranty, returns or personal records.
