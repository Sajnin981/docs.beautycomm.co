Watch beauty videos and shop the products shown. To watch videos:

1. Open **Kirei Tube** from the menu.

2. Click a video to play it.
3. Use the product tags beside or below the video to open featured products.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Kirei Tube &amp; Beauty Videos screenshot" label="Capture for Kirei Tube &amp; Beauty Videos" description="Capture: Video player with the product tags highlighted. Suggested filename: kirei-tube/02-product-tags.png" caption="kirei-tube/02-product-tags.png" />

4. Click **Like** or **Save** to keep videos for later.

Look for new videos regularly to discover tutorials and launches.
