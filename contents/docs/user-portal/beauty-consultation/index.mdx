## Consultation

Ask a beauty expert for personal advice instead of guessing which product suits you.

1. Open **Consultation** from your account or the chat button at the bottom right.
   ![Chat button](/images/user-portal/chat.jpg)
2. Describe your concern, such as dry skin, acne marks or hair fall.
3. Add your skin type, age range and any products you already use.
4. Submit the request and watch for the reply in **Notifications**.
5. Reply in the same conversation if you want to ask a follow-up.

## Dermatologist

For skin problems that need medical knowledge, you can request support from a dermatologist.

- Open **Dermatologist** and browse the available specialists.
- Pick one and describe your skin issue, with a clear photo if that helps.
- Wait for the specialist's reply, which arrives in your account.
- Treat the advice as guidance and see a clinic in person for anything serious.

## Skincare Routine

Get a routine built around your skin rather than a generic one.

1. Open **Skincare Routine** and choose your skin type and main concerns.
2. Review the suggested morning and evening steps.
3. Select a suggested product to read its details.
4. Add the whole routine, or only the items you need, to your cart.
