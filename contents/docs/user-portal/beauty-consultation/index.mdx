Get personal guidance on products and routines. To request a consultation:

1. Open **Beauty Consultation** from the main menu.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Beauty Consultation screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Consultation" description="Capture: Beauty Consultation landing page. Suggested filename: consultation/01-landing.png" caption="consultation/01-landing.png" />

2. Choose a topic such as skincare, makeup or haircare.
3. Fill in your concern and preferred time, then click **Book Consultation**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Beauty Consultation screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Consultation" description="Capture: Booking form. Suggested filename: consultation/02-booking-form.png" caption="consultation/02-booking-form.png" />

4. You receive a confirmation, and your consultation appears in your account.

Prepare your questions and current routine to get the most from the session.
