## Coupons

Coupons reduce the price of an order, either through a code you type or an offer the store has assigned to you.

- Find your available coupons under **My Coupons** in the Dashboard sidebar.
- Copy the code, then paste it into the Coupon field at checkout and select **Apply**.
- The Discount line in the order summary changes when the coupon is accepted.
- If it is rejected, check the expiry date and minimum order amount.

## Reward Points

Shopping with BeautyComm earns reward points that you can use on later orders.

1. Select **My Rewards** in the Dashboard sidebar to see your balance.
2. Select **Points History** to see which orders and actions earned or used points.
   ![My Rewards page with balance, Points History and earn and redeem panels](/images/user-portal/my-rewards.jpg)
3. At checkout, check Your Balance and select **Redeem Point** to use your points.
4. Confirm that your balance and the order total update after the purchase.

The My Rewards page also explains the rules in two panels, How To Earn Points and How To Redeem Points.

- Earning: you get points for every 100 BDT spent on product purchases, 5 points for each product review and 3 points for each community post.
- Redeeming: you need at least 100 points, and after that points can be redeemed in multiples of 50.
- Six months without shopping may adjust your membership level.
