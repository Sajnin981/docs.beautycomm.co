Earn points on every purchase and use them for discounts. To use reward points:

1. Open **Dashboard** and select **Membership** or **Reward Points**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Membership &amp; Reward Points screenshot" label="Capture for Membership &amp; Reward Points" description="Capture: Reward points page showing balance, tier and history. Suggested filename: rewards/01-points-page.png" caption="rewards/01-points-page.png" />

2. Check your points balance, membership level and earning history.
3. At checkout, choose **Use Points** to apply them to your order.

4. Complete your order. Your balance updates, and you earn new points on the purchase.

Keep an eye on expiry dates and tier benefits to get the most from your points.
