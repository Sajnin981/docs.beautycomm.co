---
title: "Logout"
description: "Log out to keep your account safe, especially on shared devices. To log out:"
keywords: ["BeautyComm","User Portal","Logout"]
---

1. Select the profile icon to open your Dashboard.
2. Select Logout at the bottom of the left sidebar.
3. The next time you select the profile icon, you will be asked to Sign in again.
4. Your cart and wishlist stay saved in your account for your next Sign In.
