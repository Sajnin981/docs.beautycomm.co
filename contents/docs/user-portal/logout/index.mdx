Log out to keep your account safe, especially on shared devices. To log out:

1. Click your name or profile icon at the top right.
2. Select **Logout**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Logout screenshot" label="Capture for Logout" description="Capture: Profile menu with Logout highlighted. Suggested filename: logout/01-logout.png" caption="logout/01-logout.png" />

3. You return to the homepage and are signed out.

Your cart and wishlist stay saved in your account for your next login.
