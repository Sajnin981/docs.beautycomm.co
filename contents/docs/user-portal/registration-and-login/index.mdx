Create your BeautyComm account to shop, save products, track orders and earn reward points. To get started:

1. Open the BeautyComm store and click **Sign Up** at the top right of the page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Header of the store with the Sign Up / Login button highlighted. Suggested filename: registration-login/01-header-signup.png" caption="registration-login/01-header-signup.png" />

2. Enter your name, email address or phone number, and a password, then click **Create Account**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Registration form with all fields visible (use dummy data). Suggested filename: registration-login/02-signup-form.png" caption="registration-login/02-signup-form.png" />

3. Enter the verification code (OTP) sent to your email or phone and click **Verify**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: OTP verification screen. Suggested filename: registration-login/03-otp.png" caption="registration-login/03-otp.png" />

4. To log in later, click **Login**, enter your email or phone number and password, then click **Login**. You can also choose a social login option such as Google or Facebook.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Login form with the social login buttons visible. Suggested filename: registration-login/04-login-form.png" caption="registration-login/04-login-form.png" />

5. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot Password**, enter your email or phone number, and follow the reset link or code.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Registration &amp; Login screenshot" label="Capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Forgot Password screen. Suggested filename: registration-login/05-forgot-password.png" caption="registration-login/05-forgot-password.png" />

After logging in, you can start browsing products, placing orders and managing your account.
