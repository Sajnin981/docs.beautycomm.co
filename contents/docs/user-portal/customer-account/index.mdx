## Account Home

Your account home may show recent orders, delivery updates, offers, reward information, or links to your profile and wishlist. Open the relevant page to confirm the full details; a summary may not show the latest status.

## Orders and Delivery

Select an order to review its reference, items, payment state, and delivery status. A tracking link, invoice, cancellation, or return option appears only when provided by the store and available for that order.

If an order is missing or unclear, contact the store using its published support information and include the order reference. Check for an order confirmation before retrying a checkout that appeared to pause.

## Profile and Addresses

Keep your name, email, phone number, and delivery address accurate. Review every change before saving. If a detail cannot be edited, contact the store; a change to your profile may not update an order already submitted.

## Wishlist and Rewards

If enabled, save products to your wishlist to revisit them later. A saved item is not reserved; its price and availability can change.

Some stores offer loyalty points, memberships, or referrals. Check the store’s current terms for earning and redeeming rewards. Contact the store if an order or points balance does not appear as expected.

## Help and Account Safety

Use the store’s official support channel for shopping, payment, delivery, or return questions. Provide the order reference and a brief description, but never send your password, one-time code, or full payment-card details.

Sign out on a shared device. Use the official password-recovery process if you cannot access your account.
