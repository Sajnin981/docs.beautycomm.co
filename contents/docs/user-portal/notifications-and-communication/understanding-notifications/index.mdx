Notifications tell you about things that need attention, such as new orders.

## Purpose

Explain where notifications appear.

## Who can use it

Business owners, store managers and authorized team members signed in to the BeautyComm web application. What you see depends on your role.

## Where to find it

Look for the notification icon in the header.

## Before you begin

- Notifications must be enabled for your account.

## Steps

1. Select the notification icon.

2. Read the notification.
3. Select it to open the related item.

## Expected result

You can see and act on new items.

## Notes

- You can change preferences in your notification settings.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No notifications | Check your notification preferences. |
