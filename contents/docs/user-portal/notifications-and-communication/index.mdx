## Read a Notification

Notifications may flag activity or a status change. Open the related order or record to confirm the latest information before acting. Do not repeat an action based only on an old alert.

## Manage Preferences

If preferences are available, choose the settings that support your work. Some alerts may be controlled by an administrator or business policy.

## Email and Mobile Alerts

For email, check the address on your account and review spam or inbox filters. For mobile alerts, check both BeautyComm preferences and device permissions. The exact delivery methods vary by store and account.

## Fix a Missing Notification

Confirm that you are signed into the correct account and that the relevant feature is enabled. Check email or device settings, then open BeautyComm to see whether the activity is present. If needed, report the notification type, approximate time, and record reference.
