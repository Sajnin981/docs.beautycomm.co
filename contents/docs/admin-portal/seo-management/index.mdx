Help customers find your store on search engines. To set it up:

1. Go to **SEO** and open **Global SEO Settings**.

2. Enter the default site title and description.
3. Open a product, category or blog post and fill in its **SEO Title**, **Meta Description** and **URL slug**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel SEO Management screenshot" label="Capture for SEO Management" description="Capture: SEO fields on a product page. Suggested filename: admin/seo/02-product-seo.png" caption="admin/seo/02-product-seo.png" />

4. Check the **Sitemap** and robots settings, and submit the sitemap to search engines [Google Search Console].

Write unique titles and descriptions for each important page.
