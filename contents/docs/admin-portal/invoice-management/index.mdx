Control how invoices look and how to find them. To manage invoices:

1. Go to **Settings > Invoices** to set your business name, logo, address, tax number and invoice prefix.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Invoice Management screenshot" label="Capture for Invoice Management" description="Capture: Invoice settings. Suggested filename: admin/invoices/01-settings.png" caption="admin/invoices/01-settings.png" />

2. Preview the invoice layout.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Invoice Management screenshot" label="Capture for Invoice Management" description="Capture: Invoice preview. Suggested filename: admin/invoices/02-preview.png" caption="admin/invoices/02-preview.png" />

3. To find a specific invoice, go to **Orders**, open the order and click **Print Invoice**, or search under **Invoices**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Invoice Management screenshot" label="Capture for Invoice Management" description="Capture: Invoice list. Suggested filename: admin/invoices/03-list.png" caption="admin/invoices/03-list.png" />
