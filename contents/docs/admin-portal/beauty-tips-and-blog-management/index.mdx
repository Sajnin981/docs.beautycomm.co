## Blog

The Blogs section shares beauty tips, ingredient guides and how-to articles written by the BeautyComm team.

1. Add a title, summary, category, cover image and article content.
2. Link relevant products inside the post where supported.
3. Save as a draft while preparing the article.
4. Publish it, then open the storefront post to confirm the layout and links work.

## Community

Community content lets customers ask questions and share beauty experiences.

- Review new discussions and replies regularly.
- Remove spam, inappropriate language or unsafe advice.
- Respond to questions where an official store answer is useful.
- Keep discussion guidance consistent with the store's community policy.

## Kirei Tube

Kirei Tube manages the tutorials, product demos and beauty routine videos shown to customers.

1. Add a video title, thumbnail, description and source.
2. Link related products when relevant.
3. Choose whether the video appears in the list or is featured.
4. Preview it on the storefront and confirm it plays on mobile.

## Influencers

Manage the beauty creators featured by BeautyComm.

1. Add the creator's profile, photo and linked content.
2. Select the products, posts and routines associated with the creator.
3. Publish the profile and preview it on the storefront.
4. Remove or update expired creator promotions.

## Reviews

Product reviews build trust and should receive regular moderation.

- Open Reviews to see recent ratings and comments.
- Approve helpful reviews and hide content that breaks store rules.
- Reply when an answer would help other shoppers.
- Escalate repeated complaints or authenticity concerns to the appropriate team.
