Publish helpful content. To write a post:

1. Go to **Blog** and click **Add Post**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Beauty Tips &amp; Blog Management screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Tips &amp; Blog Management" description="Capture: Blog posts list. Suggested filename: admin/blog/01-list.png" caption="admin/blog/01-list.png" />

2. Enter the title, add content and images, and choose a category.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Beauty Tips &amp; Blog Management screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Tips &amp; Blog Management" description="Capture: Post editor. Suggested filename: admin/blog/02-editor.png" caption="admin/blog/02-editor.png" />

3. Link products inside the article so readers can shop.
4. Fill in the SEO title and description, and set the status to **Published** or **Draft**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Beauty Tips &amp; Blog Management screenshot" label="Capture for Beauty Tips &amp; Blog Management" description="Capture: SEO fields and publish option. Suggested filename: admin/blog/03-seo-publish.png" caption="admin/blog/03-seo-publish.png" />
