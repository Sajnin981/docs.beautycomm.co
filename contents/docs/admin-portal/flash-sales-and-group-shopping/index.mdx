Run limited-time sales and group deals. To create a flash sale:

1. Go to **Marketing > Flash Sales** and click **Add Flash Sale**.

2. Set the title, start and end time and banner.
3. Add products with their sale price and sale quantity.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Flash Sales &amp; Group Shopping screenshot" label="Capture for Flash Sales &amp; Group Shopping" description="Capture: Flash sale product selection and prices. Suggested filename: admin/flash-sales/02-products.png" caption="admin/flash-sales/02-products.png" />

4. Save and activate. A countdown timer shows on the storefront.
5. For group shopping, go to **Group Deals**, choose a product, set the group price and the number of buyers required, and save.

Make sure sale stock is available before the sale starts.
