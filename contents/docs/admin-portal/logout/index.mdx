Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer. To log out:

1. Click your name or profile icon at the top right.
2. Select **Logout**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Logout screenshot" label="Capture for Logout" description="Capture: Profile menu with Logout highlighted. Suggested filename: admin/logout/01-logout.png" caption="admin/logout/01-logout.png" />

3. You return to the login page.

If you are inactive for a long time, you may be logged out automatically for security.
