description: "See who did what in the admin panel. To review activity:"
keywords: ["BeautyComm","Admin Panel","Activity Logs"]
---

Activity Logs record who did what in the panel, making mistakes and unusual changes easy to trace.

- Each entry shows the person, action, item affected and time.
- Filter by staff member, date or type of action.
- Open an entry to see what changed.
- Review the log regularly and whenever something changes unexpectedly.

Review logs when something unexpected changes, such as prices or permissions.
