## Consult Doctor

The Consult Doctor menu contains: **All Appointments**, **All Doctors**, **Add New Doctor** and **Doctor Payments**.

- **All Appointments** has tabs for All, Scheduled and Processing. Filter by search (ID or patient), Doctor ID, Status, Payment status, created date or appointment date. **Add New Appointment** books one manually, and **Export CSV** downloads the list.
- **Add New Doctor** creates a doctor profile, and **All Doctors** lists them.
- **Doctor Payments** tracks what is owed to and paid to each doctor.
