## Consultations

Manage requests for personal beauty advice from your account and storefront.

1. Review the customer's concern, skin type, age range and current products.
2. Assign the consultation to an available specialist when assignment is supported.
3. Reply in the linked conversation and add follow-up questions if needed.
4. Mark the consultation resolved after the customer receives an answer.

## Dermatologists

Manage dermatologist profiles and the appointments customers book.

- Add or edit a specialist's name, profile, expertise and availability.
- Review appointments customers have booked.
- Keep appointment and payment records up to date.
- Encourage customers to seek in-person care for serious medical concerns.

## Skin Types

Skin types help the consultation and recommendation tools match customers with suitable routines.

1. Add the options customers can choose, such as oily, dry, combination or sensitive.
2. Keep names clear and avoid duplicate entries.
3. Link relevant concerns and products where your store supports those mappings.
4. Test the consultation flow as a customer after making changes.

## AI Suggestions

AI Suggestions uses customer information and catalogue data to recommend products and routines.

- Review suggestion rules and product mappings available to your store.
- Keep product categories, skin concerns and ingredient data accurate.
- Test suggestions for different skin types and concerns before publishing.
- Treat generated guidance as recommendations, not medical advice.
