Use barcodes to find products and update stock quickly. To use it:

1. Go to **Inventory** and click **Scan Barcode** [or use a connected barcode scanner].

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Barcode Scanning screenshot" label="Capture for Barcode Scanning" description="Capture: Scan barcode button or field. Suggested filename: admin/barcode/01-scan.png" caption="admin/barcode/01-scan.png" />

2. Scan the product barcode. The product opens, or its stock field is selected.
3. Enter the quantity and save.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Barcode Scanning screenshot" label="Capture for Barcode Scanning" description="Capture: Product found by barcode with quantity field. Suggested filename: admin/barcode/02-result.png" caption="admin/barcode/02-result.png" />

4. To add barcodes to products, enter them in the **Barcode** field on the product page.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Barcode Scanning screenshot" label="Capture for Barcode Scanning" description="Capture: Barcode field on the product form. Suggested filename: admin/barcode/03-field.png" caption="admin/barcode/03-field.png" />
