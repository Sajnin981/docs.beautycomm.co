Use barcodes to find products and update stock quickly. To use it:

1. Go to **Inventory** and click **Scan Barcode** [or use a connected barcode scanner].

2. Scan the product barcode. The product opens, or its stock field is selected.
3. Enter the quantity and save.

4. To add barcodes to products, enter them in the **Barcode** field on the product page.
