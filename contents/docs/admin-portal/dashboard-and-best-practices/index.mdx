## Admin Panel

The left sidebar is grouped into **Dashboard**, **Product Management**, **Order Management**, **Customer Management**, **Supplier & Partner Management**, **Marketing & Promotions**, **Content & Engagement** and **Admin Controls**. The menu button beside the header collapses or opens the sidebar, the bell icon shows notifications, and **Website** opens the storefront.

## Sales

The store’s daily overview, opened from Dashboard > Sales.

- Choose the period with the **Today** menu or **Select Date Range**, and download the figures with **Export**.
- **Sales Overview** shows Total Sales, Total Discounts, Net Revenue, Total Orders, Sales Growth Rate and Conversion Rate.
- **Sales Trend** compares This Year with Last Year.
- **Sales by Channel** splits revenue between Website, Mobile App, Influencer Links and Others.
- **Top Selling Products** lists units sold, sales in Tk, discount % and stock left. You can search it and change the entries per page.
- Other charts show sales by category, **Sales by Customer Type**, the **Sales Funnel** (Visitor, View, Add to Cart, Checkout, Purchase), courier performance for Pathao and Redx, and **Sales by Region**.

![Sales Overview cards](/images/admin/ss02-sales-overview-cards.png)

## Coupon

Shows how your discount codes perform: how many times each code was used, the total discount given and the revenue from orders that used a coupon. Use the date menu to change the period.

## Best Practices

- Check new and pending orders at least twice a day and process them quickly.
- Review low-stock alerts every day so popular products do not sell out.
- Give each staff member their own account with only the permissions they need.
- Review the dashboard weekly and compare it with your reports.
