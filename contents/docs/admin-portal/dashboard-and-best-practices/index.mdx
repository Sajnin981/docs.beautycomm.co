The Dashboard is the store owner's daily overview. It opens on the Sales page, with a Coupon page beneath it, and shows how the business is doing without opening every report.


## Sales

1. Log in to the Admin Panel. The **Dashboard** opens first on the Sales page.
2. Pick a period from the date menu. Today is selected by default, or use **Select Date Range**.
3. Review the Sales Overview cards: Total Sales, Total Discounts, Net Revenue, Total Orders, Sales Growth Rate and Conversion Rate.
4. Use Sales Trend to compare this year with last year and spot busy or slow periods.
5. Select **Export** to download the figures, or use the bell icon to check new notifications.
6. Use the menu button beside the logo to open or collapse the left sidebar.

## Coupon

Coupon under Dashboard shows how discount codes are performing.

1. Select **Coupon** under Dashboard in the left sidebar.
2. Choose the reporting period.
3. Review each code's usage and discount value.
4. Open **Coupons** under Marketing & Promotions to create or edit a code.

## Best Practices

- Check new and pending orders at least twice a day and process them quickly.
- Review low-stock alerts every day so popular products do not sell out.
- Give each staff member their own account with only the permissions they need.
- Review the dashboard weekly and compare it with your reports.
