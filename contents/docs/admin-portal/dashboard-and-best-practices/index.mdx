The admin dashboard gives you a quick view of how your store is doing. To use it:

1. Log in to the Admin Panel. The **Dashboard** opens first.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Dashboard &amp; Best Practices screenshot" label="Capture for Dashboard &amp; Best Practices" description="Capture: Admin login and the dashboard after login. Suggested filename: admin/dashboard/01-dashboard.png" caption="admin/dashboard/01-dashboard.png" />

2. Check the summary cards at the top: today's sales, new orders, pending orders, low-stock products and new customers.
3. Review the charts for sales trends and top-selling products.

4. Use the left menu to move to the area you need, and the date filter to change the period.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Dashboard &amp; Best Practices screenshot" label="Capture for Dashboard &amp; Best Practices" description="Capture: Left menu fully expanded with section names visible. Suggested filename: admin/dashboard/03-left-menu.png" caption="admin/dashboard/03-left-menu.png" />

Best practices:

- Check new and pending orders at least [twice a day] and process them quickly.
- Review low-stock alerts every day so popular products do not sell out.
- Give each staff member their own account with only the permissions they need.
- Review the dashboard weekly and compare it with your reports.
