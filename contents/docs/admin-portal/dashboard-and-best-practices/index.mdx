## Overview

This page is for authorized business staff using the administrative dashboard. Dashboard metrics, alerts, shortcuts, and available actions vary by release and account and must be confirmed before publication.

## Dashboard information

The current documentation proposes possible summaries such as order activity, recent orders, product performance, stock information, and pending work. Treat these as examples to verify, not as a confirmed list of dashboard tiles.

Before interpreting a metric, confirm its time period, filters, scope, and definition. Use the relevant source record or report to investigate unexpected values; do not treat a summary as a complete financial or inventory reconciliation unless its documented purpose confirms that.

## Recommended operational practices

- Confirm the correct store and date range before reviewing records.
- Check the source record before acting on a summary or alert.
- Perform only actions allowed by your assigned role.
- Verify the result after a change and follow the approved escalation process if information appears inconsistent.
- Do not include passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary customer details in support requests.
