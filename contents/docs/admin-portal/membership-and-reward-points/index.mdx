---
title: "Membership & Reward Points"
description: "Reward loyal customers. To set it up:"
keywords: ["BeautyComm","Admin Panel","Membership & Reward Points"]
---

Rewards configure the membership and points program customers see in their accounts.

1. Open Memberships in the left sidebar and select All Membership.
2. Create levels such as Silver or Gold, with the spend needed and benefits.
3. Set how many points a purchase earns and how many points are worth one unit of currency.
4. Add review and community post rewards if these actions are enabled.
5. To adjust a customer's points manually, open the customer and use Adjust Points with a reason.
6. Place a test order and review Points History to confirm the customer's balance updates.
