Learn who your customers are and how they behave. To use it:

1. Go to **Reports > Customers**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Customer Insights &amp; Analytics screenshot" label="Capture for Customer Insights &amp; Analytics" description="Capture: Customer insights page. Suggested filename: admin/reports-customers/01-insights.png" caption="admin/reports-customers/01-insights.png" />

2. Review new versus returning customers, top customers and locations.
3. Check **Customer Segments** such as high spenders or inactive customers.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Customer Insights &amp; Analytics screenshot" label="Capture for Customer Insights &amp; Analytics" description="Capture: Segments view. Suggested filename: admin/reports-customers/02-segments.png" caption="admin/reports-customers/02-segments.png" />

4. Use a segment to send targeted notifications or coupons.

Handle customer data carefully and only for legitimate store purposes.
