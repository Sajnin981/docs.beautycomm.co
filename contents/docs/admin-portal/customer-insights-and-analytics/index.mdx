## Customer Insights

Customer insights show who is buying and what brings them back. There is no separate page. Use these places together:

1. **New and repeat buyers:** open Dashboard > Sales and look at **Sales by Customer Type** to compare customer groups over the period you choose. In Orders > All Orders, the **Recurring** column shows whether an order came from a returning customer (Yes or No).
2. **Customer list:** open Customer > All Customers to see registered customers, their contact details and their orders. Search for a customer to check their history.
3. **Loyalty activity:** open Customer > User Point History to see reward points earned and used. Customers who earn and redeem often are your most engaged.
4. **Membership levels:** open Memberships > All Membership to see the levels customers can reach. Use the minimum reward points to understand how customers move up.
5. **Where customers come from:** on Dashboard > Sales, **Sales by Channel** shows Website, Mobile App, Influencer Links and Others, and **Sales by Region** shows which regions buy the most.
6. **Interest in your offers:** open Customer > Surprise Subscribers to see who joined the surprise offer.
7. **Use what you learn:** plan coupons for slow regions, reward your repeat buyers with a membership level, and restock the products your top regions order.

The Customer menu also includes **User Lists**, which shows saved customer groups for campaigns, and **User Lists Import**, which adds customers in bulk from a file.
