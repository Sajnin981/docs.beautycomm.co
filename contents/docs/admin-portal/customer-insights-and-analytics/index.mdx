Customer records help your team support shoppers and understand how the store is being used.

- **All Customers** lists registered shoppers. Open a customer to see their details, orders and point history.
- **User Point History** shows the reward points each customer earned and used.
- **Surprise Subscribers** shows subscribed phone numbers and their total count.
- **User Lists** and **User Lists Import** help manage lists of customers in bulk.
- Use customer segments and reports only for legitimate store purposes.
- Handle personal information carefully and limit access to team members who need it.

Handle customer data carefully and only for legitimate store purposes.
