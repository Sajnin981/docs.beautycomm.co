Track and review your sales. To use it:

1. Go to **Sales**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Sales Management screenshot" label="Capture for Sales Management" description="Capture: Sales overview. Suggested filename: admin/sales/01-overview.png" caption="admin/sales/01-overview.png" />

2. Filter by date, channel, product or payment status.
3. Open a sale to see its details and payment information.
4. Export the list as a file when you need it for accounting.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Sales Management screenshot" label="Capture for Sales Management" description="Capture: Export button and filters. Suggested filename: admin/sales/02-export.png" caption="admin/sales/02-export.png" />
