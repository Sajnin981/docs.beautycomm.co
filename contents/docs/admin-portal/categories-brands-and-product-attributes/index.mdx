Categories, brands and product attributes shape how customers browse and filter the catalogue.

## Categories

Categories organize the catalogue and fill the Browse Categories menu in the store.

1. Open Category in the left sidebar.
2. Add a category with a name and image, placing it under a parent category if needed.
3. Edit or reorder existing categories.
4. Check Browse Categories in the store to confirm the change.

## Brands

Brands group products by manufacturer and let customers shop by trusted names.

- Add Brand: enter the brand name and logo, then save.
- Brands: see every brand with its ID, name, slug, category, Is Top and Is Active status.
- Verify Brands: review brands that need approval and confirm genuine entries.
- Use Filter or Clear to search the brand list.

## Product Attributes

1. Open the attributes area and add properties such as skin type, concern, size or ingredient.
2. Enter clear values for each attribute.
3. Assign products to the correct category, brand and attributes.
4. Check the storefront filters to confirm the new values appear and are easy to understand.
