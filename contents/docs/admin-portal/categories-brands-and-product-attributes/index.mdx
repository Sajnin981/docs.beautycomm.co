These organize your catalog and power customer filters. To manage them:

1. Go to **Catalog > Categories** and click **Add Category**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes screenshot" label="Capture for Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes" description="Capture: Categories list with the Add button. Suggested filename: admin/catalog/01-categories.png" caption="admin/catalog/01-categories.png" />

2. Enter the name, choose a parent category if it is a subcategory, add an image and click **Save**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes screenshot" label="Capture for Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes" description="Capture: Add category form. Suggested filename: admin/catalog/02-add-category.png" caption="admin/catalog/02-add-category.png" />

3. Go to **Brands** and click **Add Brand**. Enter the name and logo and save.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes screenshot" label="Capture for Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes" description="Capture: Add brand form with logo upload. Suggested filename: admin/catalog/03-add-brand.png" caption="admin/catalog/03-add-brand.png" />

4. Go to **Attributes** to add properties such as skin type, concern or ingredient, with their values.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes screenshot" label="Capture for Categories, Brands &amp; Product Attributes" description="Capture: Attribute list with values. Suggested filename: admin/catalog/04-attributes.png" caption="admin/catalog/04-attributes.png" />

Attributes appear as filters in the storefront, so keep their names simple.
