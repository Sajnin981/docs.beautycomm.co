## Manage Orders

Every store order arrives here for processing, whether it is paid online, by bKash or as Cash on Delivery.

1. Open Orders and select All Orders.
2. Use the status filter or search to find a specific order by number, name or phone.
3. Open the order to review its items, customer details, address and payment method.
4. Change the status as it moves forward: pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered or completed.
5. Confirm that the customer can see the updated status in Tracking.

## Sales

The Sales page shows how orders turn into revenue over a period you choose.

- Pick a date range to see total sales, the number of orders and average order value.
- Break the numbers down by payment method or order status when available.
- Open a sales line to go directly to the underlying orders.

## Preorders

Preorders let customers reserve products that are not yet in stock.

1. Mark a product as available for preorder and set its expected arrival date.
2. Customers order it normally, and the order is flagged as a preorder.
3. Open Preorders to see everything waiting for stock.
4. When goods arrive, update the stock and move the orders into normal processing.

## Invoices

Invoice management lets you find and reprint the invoice for an order.

- Search by order number or customer name.
- Open the invoice to check its items, totals and customer details.
- Print it or save it as a file to send to the customer.
