## Orders

The Orders menu contains: **All Orders**, **Scan Order**, **Add New Order**, **Add New Preorder** and **Add Whole Sale Order**.

- The tabs at the top filter by status: All, Processing, Packaging, Shipped, Completed, Preordered, On Hold, Cancelled, Return and Refunded.
- Filter by Bulk Action, product, date range or search.
- Each row shows Order ID, Date, Customer Name, Recurring (Yes/No), Amount, Payment (for example COD) and Status.
- The **eye** icon opens the order. The action icons let you **edit**, **print** and **download** the invoice.
- To move an order forward, open it and change its status, for example Processing, Packaging, Shipped, then Completed. For many orders at once, tick them and use **Bulk Action**.
- **Add New Order** creates a normal order, **Add New Preorder** reserves out-of-stock items, and **Add Whole Sale Order** is for bulk buyers.

## Pathao Orders

The Pathao Orders menu contains: **Scan Pathao Orders** and **Pathao Orders**.

Works like Redx Orders. Scan an order ID to book it with Pathao, and use **Pathao Orders** to see every parcel booked with this courier and its delivery status.

## Redx Orders

The Redx Orders menu contains: **Scan Redx Orders** and **Redx Orders**.

1. Open **Scan Redx Orders**.
2. Click the **Order ID** field and scan or type the order ID.
3. The order is booked with Redx. Open **Redx Orders** to see all booked parcels.

![Scan Redx Orders page](/images/admin/ss09-scan-redx-orders.png)

## Scan Orders

Opens an order directly when you scan its ID, which speeds up packing and dispatch. Click the scan field, scan the barcode on the parcel, and the order opens or updates.
