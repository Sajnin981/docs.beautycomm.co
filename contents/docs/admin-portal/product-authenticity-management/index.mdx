Give customers a way to confirm that products are genuine. To set it up:

1. Go to **Authenticity** and open the product you want to protect.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Authenticity Management screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity Management" description="Capture: Authenticity management page. Suggested filename: admin/authenticity/01-page.png" caption="admin/authenticity/01-page.png" />

2. Generate or import verification codes or QR codes for the product batch.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Authenticity Management screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity Management" description="Capture: Generate codes screen. Suggested filename: admin/authenticity/02-generate.png" caption="admin/authenticity/02-generate.png" />

3. Turn on the **Authentic** badge for the product.
4. Review verification activity to spot repeated or suspicious checks.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Authenticity Management screenshot" label="Capture for Product Authenticity Management" description="Capture: Verification log. Suggested filename: admin/authenticity/03-log.png" caption="admin/authenticity/03-log.png" />

Print codes on packaging only after confirming that they match the right batch.
