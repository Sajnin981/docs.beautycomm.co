Moderate customer reviews. To manage them:

1. Go to **Reviews**.

2. Filter by status (Pending, Approved, Rejected), product or rating.
3. Open a review to read it and see any photos.
4. Click **Approve**, **Reject** or **Delete**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Reviews &amp; Ratings screenshot" label="Capture for Product Reviews &amp; Ratings" description="Capture: Review detail with moderation buttons. Suggested filename: admin/reviews/02-moderate.png" caption="admin/reviews/02-moderate.png" />

5. Use **Reply** to answer a review publicly [if supported].

Approve genuine reviews, including critical ones, so customers trust your ratings.
