Decide where you deliver and what it costs. To set it up:

1. Go to **Settings > Shipping Zones** and click **Add Zone**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Shipping Zones &amp; Rates screenshot" label="Capture for Shipping Zones &amp; Rates" description="Capture: Shipping zones list. Suggested filename: admin/shipping-zones/01-list.png" caption="admin/shipping-zones/01-list.png" />

2. Name the zone and choose the areas it covers, for example cities or districts.
3. Add shipping methods and rates: flat rate, free shipping above an amount, or weight-based.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Shipping Zones &amp; Rates screenshot" label="Capture for Shipping Zones &amp; Rates" description="Capture: Zone with shipping rates. Suggested filename: admin/shipping-zones/02-rates.png" caption="admin/shipping-zones/02-rates.png" />

4. Save. Customers see the matching rates at checkout.

Check checkout with an address in each zone.
