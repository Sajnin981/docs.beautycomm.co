## Returns & Refunds

Returns and refunds are managed from Orders > All Orders. An order moves through the **Return** and **Refunded** statuses, and each has its own tab with a count.

1. Open **Orders** in the left sidebar and select **All Orders**.
2. Select the **Return** tab to see orders where the customer has asked to send items back.
3. Open the order with the **eye** icon to check the items, the customer and the payment method.
4. When the item arrives and is in good condition, put it back into stock. You can check the quantity under Stock Management > All Stocks.
5. Pay the customer back. For a Cash on Delivery (COD) order, this is usually done by hand outside the panel, such as a mobile wallet or bank transfer.
6. Change the order status to **Refunded**. It then moves to the **Refunded** tab, where you can find it later.
