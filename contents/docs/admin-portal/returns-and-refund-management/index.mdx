This page handles the return requests customers submit from their accounts.

1. Open Returns & Refunds and review the new requests.
2. Read the reason and look at any photos the customer attached.
3. Approve or reject the request, adding a short internal or customer note.
4. For an approved return, confirm the item has arrived back in good condition.
5. Process the refund through the original payment method and mark the request as completed.
6. Restock the item if it can be sold again.

Follow your published Returns & Refund Policy so decisions stay fair and consistent.
