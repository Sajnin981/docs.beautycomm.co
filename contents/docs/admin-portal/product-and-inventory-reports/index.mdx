Understand which products perform and what needs restocking. To use these reports:

1. Go to **Reports > Products** to see best sellers, slow movers and top-rated products.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product &amp; Inventory Reports screenshot" label="Capture for Product &amp; Inventory Reports" description="Capture: Product performance report. Suggested filename: admin/reports-products/01-report.png" caption="admin/reports-products/01-report.png" />

2. Go to **Reports > Inventory** to see current stock, low stock and out-of-stock items.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product &amp; Inventory Reports screenshot" label="Capture for Product &amp; Inventory Reports" description="Capture: Inventory report. Suggested filename: admin/reports-products/02-inventory.png" caption="admin/reports-products/02-inventory.png" />

3. Use filters by category, brand or date, and export if needed.

Use these reports to plan your next restock.
