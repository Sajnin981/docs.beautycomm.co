## Shipments

The Shipments page follows each parcel from your shelf to the customer's door.

1. Open Shipping and select **Shipments**.
2. Pick confirmed orders that are ready to dispatch.
3. Assign a courier and enter or generate the tracking number.
4. Mark the shipment as dispatched, then update it again when it is delivered.
5. Watch for delayed shipments and follow up with the courier.

## Couriers

Connect the courier companies you use so parcels can be booked and tracked from the panel.

- Add a courier and enter the credentials or API details it provides.
- Switch each courier on or off depending on whether you currently use it.

## Zones & Rates

Shipping zones and rates decide what a customer pays for delivery, based on where they live.

1. Create a zone, for example inside the city or outside it.
2. Add the cities and areas that belong to the zone.
3. Set the delivery charge and a free-shipping threshold if you offer one.
4. Place a test order from an address in each zone and check the Shipping line at checkout.
