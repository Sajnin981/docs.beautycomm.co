Handle shipments from packing to delivery. To manage them:

1. Go to **Shipping** or open an order.

2. Click **Create Shipment** and choose the items, courier and tracking number.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Shipping &amp; Delivery Management screenshot" label="Capture for Shipping &amp; Delivery Management" description="Capture: Create shipment form. Suggested filename: admin/shipping/02-create.png" caption="admin/shipping/02-create.png" />

3. Mark the shipment as **Shipped**, and later **Delivered**.
4. The customer sees the tracking updates in their account.
