Control how product suggestions are shown. To manage them:

1. Go to **Personalization** [or AI Recommendations].

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel AI Recommendations &amp; Personalization screenshot" label="Capture for AI Recommendations &amp; Personalization" description="Capture: Settings page. Suggested filename: admin/ai/01-settings.png" caption="admin/ai/01-settings.png" />

2. Turn on the recommendation areas you want: home page, product page, cart.
3. Set options such as how many products to show and whether to exclude out-of-stock items.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel AI Recommendations &amp; Personalization screenshot" label="Capture for AI Recommendations &amp; Personalization" description="Capture: Recommendation options. Suggested filename: admin/ai/02-options.png" caption="admin/ai/02-options.png" />

4. Save and check the storefront to see the results.

Recommendations improve as more customers browse and buy.
