Keep track of who supplies your products. To manage suppliers:

1. Go to **Suppliers** and click **Add Supplier**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Suppliers &amp; Product Sourcing screenshot" label="Capture for Suppliers &amp; Product Sourcing" description="Capture: Suppliers list. Suggested filename: admin/suppliers/01-list.png" caption="admin/suppliers/01-list.png" />

2. Enter the supplier name, contact details, address and notes.
3. Link products to a supplier, and record purchase prices [if supported].

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Suppliers &amp; Product Sourcing screenshot" label="Capture for Suppliers &amp; Product Sourcing" description="Capture: Supplier form and linked products. Suggested filename: admin/suppliers/02-form.png" caption="admin/suppliers/02-form.png" />

4. Create a purchase or restock record when ordering new stock [if supported].

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Suppliers &amp; Product Sourcing screenshot" label="Capture for Suppliers &amp; Product Sourcing" description="Capture: Purchase order or restock record. Suggested filename: admin/suppliers/03-purchase.png" caption="admin/suppliers/03-purchase.png" />
