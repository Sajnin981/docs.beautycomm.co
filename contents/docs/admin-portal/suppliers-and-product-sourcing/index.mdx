Keep track of who supplies your products. To manage suppliers:

1. Go to **Suppliers** and click **Add Supplier**.

2. Enter the supplier name, contact details, address and notes.
3. Link products to a supplier, and record purchase prices [if supported].

4. Create a purchase or restock record when ordering new stock [if supported].
