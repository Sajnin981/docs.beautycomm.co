Set the basics of your store. To update them:

1. Go to **Settings > General**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel General Settings &amp; Configurations screenshot" label="Capture for General Settings &amp; Configurations" description="Capture: General settings. Suggested filename: admin/settings/01-general.png" caption="admin/settings/01-general.png" />

2. Update the store name, logo, favicon, contact details, currency, time zone and language.
3. Set tax, order and stock options such as [tax rate, order number format, low stock threshold].

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel General Settings &amp; Configurations screenshot" label="Capture for General Settings &amp; Configurations" description="Capture: Tax and order options. Suggested filename: admin/settings/02-options.png" caption="admin/settings/02-options.png" />

4. Click **Save Changes**.
