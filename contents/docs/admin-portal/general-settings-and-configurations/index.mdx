## General

General settings hold the basic identity and behavior of the store.

- Set the store name, logo, contact phone and email.
- Choose the currency, time zone and default language.
- Save and check the header and footer on the storefront.

## Payments

Payment settings decide how customers can pay at checkout.

1. Open Payments and enable the methods you offer: Online Payment, bKash and Cash on Delivery.
2. Enter the credentials your payment provider gave you for each online method.
3. Save and place a small test order with every method.
4. Disable any method that is not working until it is fixed.

## Social Login

Social Login lets customers sign up and Sign in with an account they already have.

- Create the app with the provider and copy its client ID and secret.
- Paste them into the matching fields and enable the provider.
- Test the Sign In button on the storefront with a spare account.

## SEO

SEO settings help search engines show your store clearly.

1. Set a default title and description for the store.
2. Edit the title, description and web address of key products and categories.
3. Add an image for sharing links on social media.
4. Search for your store after a few days to see the changes.

## Media

The media library stores every image, video and file used across the store.

- Upload files and organize them into folders.
- Rename or delete files you no longer need, after confirming nothing still uses them.
- Keep images compressed so pages load quickly.
