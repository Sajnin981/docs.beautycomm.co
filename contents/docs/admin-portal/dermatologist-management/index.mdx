Manage the dermatologists who answer customer requests. To add one:

1. Go to **Dermatologists** and click **Add Dermatologist**.

2. Enter the name, photo, specialty, qualifications and availability.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Dermatologist Management screenshot" label="Capture for Dermatologist Management" description="Capture: Add dermatologist form. Suggested filename: admin/dermatologist/02-form.png" caption="admin/dermatologist/02-form.png" />

3. Click **Save**. They now appear on the storefront.
4. Review customer requests under **Dermatologist Requests** and assign them.
