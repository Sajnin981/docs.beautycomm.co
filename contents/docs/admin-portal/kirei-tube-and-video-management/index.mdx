Manage the video commerce section. To add a video:

1. Go to **Kirei Tube** and click **Add Video**.

2. Upload the video or paste its link, and add a title, description and thumbnail.

3. Tag the products featured in the video.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Kirei Tube &amp; Video Management screenshot" label="Capture for Kirei Tube &amp; Video Management" description="Capture: Product tagging. Suggested filename: admin/kirei-tube/03-tags.png" caption="admin/kirei-tube/03-tags.png" />

4. Set the status to **Published** and save.
