Give team members the right access. To add a staff member:

1. Go to **Staff** and click **Add Staff**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Staff &amp; Role Permissions screenshot" label="Capture for Staff &amp; Role Permissions" description="Capture: Staff list. Suggested filename: admin/staff/01-list.png" caption="admin/staff/01-list.png" />

2. Enter their name, email and password, and select a **Role**.
3. To create a role, go to **Roles**, click **Add Role** and tick the permissions it needs, for example Orders, Products or Reports.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Staff &amp; Role Permissions screenshot" label="Capture for Staff &amp; Role Permissions" description="Capture: Role permissions checklist. Suggested filename: admin/staff/02-roles.png" caption="admin/staff/02-roles.png" />

4. Click **Save**. Deactivate staff accounts when someone leaves.

Give each person only the access they need.
