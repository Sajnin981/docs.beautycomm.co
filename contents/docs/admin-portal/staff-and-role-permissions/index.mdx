## Admins

The Admins menu contains: **All Admins** and **Add New Admin**.

1. Open Admins > Add New Admin.
2. Enter the person’s name, email and a secure password.
3. Assign a role and save.
4. Sign in as that person once to confirm they see only what they should.

**All Admins** lists your team and lets you edit or remove a member.

![Admins submenu open in the sidebar](/images/admin/ss12-admins-submenu.png)

## Roles & Permissions

Create roles and choose which pages and actions each role may use, for example an order handler who can open Orders but not Admin Controls. Give each person only the access they need.
