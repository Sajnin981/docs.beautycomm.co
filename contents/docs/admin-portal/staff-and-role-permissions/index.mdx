## Admins

Admins lists the people who can log in to the Admin Panel.

1. Open Admins and select Add Admin.
2. Enter a name, email and password.
3. Assign a role so the person only sees areas needed for their job.
4. Deactivate or remove an admin who leaves the team.

## Roles & Permissions

Roles decide which pages and actions an admin can use.

- Create a role for a specific job, such as order handler or content editor.
- Tick the pages and actions that the role may use.
- Assign the role to an admin, then sign in as that user to verify the permissions.

Give each person only the access they need.
