Let customers log in with their social accounts. To set it up:

1. Go to **Settings > Social Login**.

2. Choose a provider such as Google or Facebook.
3. Create an app in the provider's developer console and copy its **Client ID** and **Client Secret** [blur both].

4. Add the callback URL shown on the page to the provider's app settings.
5. Enable the provider and save, then test by logging in on the storefront.
