Keep the community healthy. To moderate it:

1. Go to **Community**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Community Management screenshot" label="Capture for Community Management" description="Capture: Community posts list. Suggested filename: admin/community/01-list.png" caption="admin/community/01-list.png" />

2. Review new posts and reported content.
3. Click **Approve**, **Hide** or **Delete**, or **Ban User** for repeated violations.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Community Management screenshot" label="Capture for Community Management" description="Capture: Moderation actions. Suggested filename: admin/community/02-moderate.png" caption="admin/community/02-moderate.png" />

4. Create **Topics** or categories to organize discussions.
