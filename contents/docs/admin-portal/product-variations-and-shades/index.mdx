Variations let one product have several shades, sizes or types. To add them:

1. Open a product and go to the **Variations** section.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Variations &amp; Shades screenshot" label="Capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Capture: Product edit page, Variations section. Suggested filename: admin/variations/01-section.png" caption="admin/variations/01-section.png" />

2. Click **Add Variation** and choose the type, for example Shade or Size.
3. Enter each option, with its name and swatch color or image.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Variations &amp; Shades screenshot" label="Capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Capture: Adding shade options with swatch colors. Suggested filename: admin/variations/02-add-shades.png" caption="admin/variations/02-add-shades.png" />

4. For each option, set the price, SKU and stock, and upload an image if it differs.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Product Variations &amp; Shades screenshot" label="Capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Capture: Variation table with price, SKU and stock columns. Suggested filename: admin/variations/03-variation-table.png" caption="admin/variations/03-variation-table.png" />

5. Click **Save**.

Each variation has its own stock, so keep it updated.
