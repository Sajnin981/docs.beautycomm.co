See how much you are selling. To open a report:

1. Go to **Reports > Sales**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Sales Reports &amp; Analytics screenshot" label="Capture for Sales Reports &amp; Analytics" description="Capture: Sales report page. Suggested filename: admin/reports-sales/01-report.png" caption="admin/reports-sales/01-report.png" />

2. Choose the date range, and group by day, week or month.
3. Review total sales, orders, average order value and refunds.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Sales Reports &amp; Analytics screenshot" label="Capture for Sales Reports &amp; Analytics" description="Capture: Report charts and totals with callouts. Suggested filename: admin/reports-sales/02-charts.png" caption="admin/reports-sales/02-charts.png" />

4. Click **Export** to download the data.
