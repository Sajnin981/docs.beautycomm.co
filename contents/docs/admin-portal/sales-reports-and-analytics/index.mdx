## Sales Reports

Sales reports turn raw orders into numbers you can compare and share.

1. Open Reports and select **Sales Reports**.
2. Choose the date range and, if needed, a category or payment method.
3. Read the totals and trend chart.
4. Export the report as a file for finance or management.

## Product Reports

Product reports show which items sell, which sit idle and which are running low.

- Check best sellers and slow movers for the selected period.
- See current stock levels beside recent sales speed.
- Export the list to prepare reorders with your suppliers.
