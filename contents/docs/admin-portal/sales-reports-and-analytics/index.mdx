## Sales Reports

Sales reports turn your orders into numbers you can compare and share. They live on the Dashboard > Sales page, so there is no separate Reports menu.

1. Open **Dashboard** in the left sidebar and select **Sales**.
2. Choose the period with the **Today** menu or **Select Date Range**.
3. Read **Sales Overview** for Total Sales, Total Discounts, Net Revenue, Total Orders, Sales Growth Rate and Conversion Rate.
4. Check **Sales Trend** to compare This Year with Last Year, and **Sales by Channel** to see Website, Mobile App, Influencer Links and Others.
5. Scroll down to **Sales by Region** to see which regions sell the most.
6. Select **Export** to download the figures for finance or management.

## Product Reports

Product reports show which items sell, which sit idle and which are running low. They combine two existing pages.

1. On Dashboard > Sales, scroll to **Top Selling Products**. It lists units sold, sales in Tk, discount % and stock left. Use the search box or change the entries per page to find a product.
2. Open **Stock Management** and select **All Stocks** to see current Stock beside the 30d, 60d and 90d sales columns. Products with low sales and high stock are your slow movers, and products with high sales and low stock need restocking.
3. Filter with **Stock Filter**, sort with **Sort By**, or search for one product.
4. Select **Export Stocks** to download the list and prepare reorders with your suppliers.
