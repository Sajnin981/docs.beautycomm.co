Keep stock numbers accurate. To manage inventory:

1. Go to **Inventory**.

2. Find a product or variation and click **Adjust Stock**.
3. Enter the new quantity or the amount to add or remove, with a reason, and save.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Inventory &amp; Stock Management screenshot" label="Capture for Inventory &amp; Stock Management" description="Capture: Adjust stock dialog. Suggested filename: admin/inventory/02-adjust.png" caption="admin/inventory/02-adjust.png" />

4. Set a **low stock threshold** so the dashboard warns you.

5. Review the stock history to see every change.
