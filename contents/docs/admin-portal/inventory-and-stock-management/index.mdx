## Stock

Stock Management keeps the quantities in the store aligned with the shelves in your warehouse.

- Open Stock Management in the left sidebar to see the quantity available for each product.
- Update quantities after receiving goods, processing an order or counting stock.
- Set a low-stock level so the Dashboard can warn you before an item runs out.
- Review stock history to see what changed and when.

## Multi-Store Sync

If you sell from more than one store or branch, Multi-Store Sync keeps their stock aligned.

1. Open Multi-Store Sync and check that each store is listed.
2. Choose which stores share stock for a product.
3. Run a sync, or confirm that automatic sync is switched on.
4. Compare the quantities across stores to make sure they match.

## Barcodes

Barcodes make receiving, counting and selling faster than typing product names.

1. Open a product and add its barcode number.
2. Open Barcode Import or Barcodes and scan an item with a scanner or camera.
3. The matching product opens so you can check or update its stock.
4. Use the same scan during stock counts to avoid typing mistakes.
