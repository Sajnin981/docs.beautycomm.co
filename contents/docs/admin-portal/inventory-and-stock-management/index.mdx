## Stock Management

The Stock Management menu contains: **All Stocks**, **Supplier Stocks**, **All Requested Stocks** and **All Imported Stocks**.

- **All Stocks** shows each product’s Stock, Rsvd (reserved), Upc (upcoming), Req’d (requested), 30d / 60d / 90d sales, Unit Price, Cost Price and Odoo reference. Filter with Stock Filter, sort with Sort By, search, or download with **Export Stocks**.
- A negative Req’d value means requested quantity is higher than what you have, so check it before restocking.
- **Supplier Stocks** lists stock held by your suppliers and partners.
- **All Requested Stocks** shows stock requests raised from the panel or by customers.
- **All Imported Stocks** shows stock added through file imports.
