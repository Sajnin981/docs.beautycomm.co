Work with influencers who promote your products. To add one:

1. Go to **Influencers** and click **Add Influencer**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Influencer Management screenshot" label="Capture for Influencer Management" description="Capture: Influencers list. Suggested filename: admin/influencers/01-list.png" caption="admin/influencers/01-list.png" />

2. Enter their name, photo, bio and social links.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Influencer Management screenshot" label="Capture for Influencer Management" description="Capture: Add influencer form. Suggested filename: admin/influencers/02-form.png" caption="admin/influencers/02-form.png" />

3. Select the products they recommend and set any commission [if supported].
4. Review their sales and performance under their profile.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Influencer Management screenshot" label="Capture for Influencer Management" description="Capture: Influencer performance view. Suggested filename: admin/influencers/03-performance.png" caption="admin/influencers/03-performance.png" />
