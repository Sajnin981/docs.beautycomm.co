## Products

The Products menu contains: **Add New Product**, **All Products**, **Brand Category**, **Events** and **Barcode Import**.

### Add a product

1. Open Products > Add New Product.
2. Under **Basic Information**, enter the Product Name (required), Japanese Name, Barcode and Published Status.
3. Under **Pricing**, enter Unit Price (required), Purchase Price (required), Cost Price and Cost Price (JPY). Optionally set a Discount and a Discount Type (Percent by default).
4. Scroll down to add the description, images, category, brand and stock quantity.
5. Save, then open the product on the storefront to check how it looks.

![Add New Product page](/images/admin/ss03-add-new-product.png)

### All Products

- The tabs at the top are **All**, **Published**, **Unpublished**, **Approved** and **Price Mismatch**, each with a count.
- Filter by Product Status, Stock Filter or Published Status, or use the search box, then select **Filter**. **Clear** resets everything.
- Columns include ID, Barcode, Stock, Sale Price, Discount and the toggles **is private**, **is New**, **Published** and **Is Request Available**.
- Use the edit and delete icons in **Action** to change or remove a product.

![All Products list](/images/admin/ss04-all-products.png)

**Brand Category** groups products by brand and category. **Events** links products to promotional events and seasonal campaigns. **Barcode Import** adds barcodes to many products at once from a file.

## Verify Products

The Verify Products menu contains: **All Verify Products** and **Add Verify Product**.

Verify Products powers the authenticity check on the storefront.

1. Open Add Verify Product.
2. Enter the product details and the authenticity code or batch it belongs to.
3. Save, then test the code on the storefront to confirm it verifies.

The list has a search box and **Filter** / **Clear** buttons.

## Product Reviews

The Product Reviews menu contains: **Product Reviews** and **Add New Product Review**.

1. Open Product Reviews to see all reviews. The sidebar shows the total count.
2. Narrow the list with Bulk Action, Rating Order, the filters or **Filter by Products**.
3. Approve, hide or delete reviews that break your rules.
4. Use **Add Review** to enter a review manually.

## Product Authenticity

Holds the authenticity records linked to Verify Products. Use it to look up a code, see whether it has been checked by a customer, and flag a suspicious product.
