Add and manage everything you sell. To add a product:

1. Go to **Products** and click **Add Product**.

2. Enter the product name, description, ingredients and how-to-use text.
3. Choose the category and brand, and add any attributes.
4. Upload product images and choose the main image.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Products screenshot" label="Capture for Products" description="Capture: Add product form, general information and image upload. Suggested filename: admin/products/02-general.png" caption="admin/products/02-general.png" />

5. Set the price, discount, SKU and stock quantity.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Products screenshot" label="Capture for Products" description="Capture: Add product form, pricing and inventory section. Suggested filename: admin/products/03-pricing.png" caption="admin/products/03-pricing.png" />

6. Set the status to **Active** and click **Save**.
7. To edit, hide or delete a product, find it in the list and use the action buttons.

Use clear names and good images, because they directly affect sales and search.
