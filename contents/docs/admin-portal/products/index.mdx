## Manage Products

Products is the working catalogue where every item sold in the store is created and maintained.

1. Under Product Management in the left sidebar, open **Products**.
2. Select **Add New Product** to create an item.
3. Enter the product name, Japanese name, barcode, description, category, brand and photos.
4. Set the unit price, purchase price and cost price. Add a discount and choose its type if needed.
5. Set **Published Status** to Published when the item is ready for the storefront.
6. Complete the remaining fields, save the product and confirm it appears in **All Products**.
7. Use the edit icon to update an item, or the bin icon to delete it.

## Shades & Variations

Use variations when one product comes in different shades, sizes or packs.

- Open the product and go to its Variations section.
- Add each shade, size or pack as its own option with a name, price and stock count.
- Upload a photo for each variation so customers can identify the exact option.
- Save the product and confirm the variation selector appears on its storefront page.

## Authenticity

Authenticity management supports the Verify Product feature on the storefront.

1. Open **Verify Products** in the left sidebar.
2. Generate or import the authenticity codes that belong to a product batch.
3. Link each batch to its product so the code returns the right details.
4. Print or attach codes to packaging through your normal process.
5. Test one code on the storefront to confirm it verifies correctly.

## Suppliers

Keep a record of who supplies your products so reordering is quick.

- Add a supplier with its name, contact details and the products it provides.
- Link products to their supplier from the product or supplier page.
- Update contact details when they change, and remove suppliers you no longer use.
