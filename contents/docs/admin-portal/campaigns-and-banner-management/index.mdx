Control the banners and campaigns customers see. To add a banner:

1. Go to **Marketing > Banners** and click **Add Banner**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Campaigns &amp; Banner Management screenshot" label="Capture for Campaigns &amp; Banner Management" description="Capture: Banners list. Suggested filename: admin/banners/01-list.png" caption="admin/banners/01-list.png" />

2. Upload the banner image, with the right sizes for [desktop and mobile].
3. Set the link, position (home slider, promotional strip) and the start and end dates.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Campaigns &amp; Banner Management screenshot" label="Capture for Campaigns &amp; Banner Management" description="Capture: Banner form. Suggested filename: admin/banners/02-form.png" caption="admin/banners/02-form.png" />

4. Save, then reorder banners by dragging them in the list.
5. For campaigns, go to **Campaigns** to group banners, coupons and products under one name and schedule.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Campaigns &amp; Banner Management screenshot" label="Capture for Campaigns &amp; Banner Management" description="Capture: Campaign page. Suggested filename: admin/banners/03-campaign.png" caption="admin/banners/03-campaign.png" />
