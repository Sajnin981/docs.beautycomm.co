## Marketing & Promotions

### Coupons

The Coupons menu contains: **All Coupons**, **All Spinner Coupons** and **Add New Coupon**.

Coupons are discount codes customers enter at checkout. **All Coupons** lists the codes you have created. **All Spinner Coupons** lists the coupons that are given out as prizes on the storefront’s Spin & Win wheel.

#### Add a coupon

1. Open Coupons > Add New Coupon.
2. Choose the coupon **Type** (required) and, if the coupon is only for members, a **Membership**.
3. Enter the **Code** (required) customers will type at checkout.
4. Enter the **Discount** (required) amount and choose the **Discount Type** (required), for example fixed amount or percent.
5. Set **Minimum Buy** (required), **Minimum Order** (required) and **Maximum Customer** (required). These control how much a customer must buy or spend, and how many customers can use the code.
6. Choose a **Start Date** and **End Date**.
7. Add a **Description** if you want to note what the coupon is for.
8. Use the switches as needed:
   - **Is Unlimited Usage**: lets the code be used without a limit.
   - **Apply Hot Deals**: lets the coupon apply to products that are already on a hot deal.
   - **Is Spinner Coupon**: makes the coupon available as a Spin & Win prize.
9. Scroll down and save the coupon, then try the code on a test order.

A coupon only works on products in a category where **is Coupon Applicable** is switched on (Category > Categories). You can check how your codes perform under Dashboard > Coupon.
