Manage your newsletter audience. To use it:

1. Go to **Subscribers**.

2. Search, filter or export the list.
3. Remove a subscriber or mark them as unsubscribed on request.
4. Use the list as an audience when sending emails.

Only email people who agreed to receive your messages, and always honor unsubscribe requests.
