Manage your newsletter audience. To use it:

1. Go to **Subscribers**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Marketing &amp; Subscribers screenshot" label="Capture for Marketing &amp; Subscribers" description="Capture: Subscribers list. Suggested filename: admin/subscribers/01-list.png" caption="admin/subscribers/01-list.png" />

2. Search, filter or export the list.
3. Remove a subscriber or mark them as unsubscribed on request.
4. Use the list as an audience when sending emails.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Marketing &amp; Subscribers screenshot" label="Capture for Marketing &amp; Subscribers" description="Capture: Export and audience options. Suggested filename: admin/subscribers/02-export.png" caption="admin/subscribers/02-export.png" />

Only email people who agreed to receive your messages, and always honor unsubscribe requests.
