Send messages to customers. To send a notification:

1. Go to **Notifications** and click **Create**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Email &amp; Push Notification Management screenshot" label="Capture for Email &amp; Push Notification Management" description="Capture: Notifications list. Suggested filename: admin/notifications/01-list.png" caption="admin/notifications/01-list.png" />

2. Choose the channel: email, push notification or both.
3. Write the title and message, and add a link or image.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Email &amp; Push Notification Management screenshot" label="Capture for Email &amp; Push Notification Management" description="Capture: Compose form. Suggested filename: admin/notifications/02-compose.png" caption="admin/notifications/02-compose.png" />

4. Choose the audience, such as all customers or a segment.
5. Send now or schedule it.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Email &amp; Push Notification Management screenshot" label="Capture for Email &amp; Push Notification Management" description="Capture: Audience and schedule options. Suggested filename: admin/notifications/03-audience.png" caption="admin/notifications/03-audience.png" />

6. To edit automatic messages such as order confirmations, open **Templates**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm Admin Panel Email &amp; Push Notification Management screenshot" label="Capture for Email &amp; Push Notification Management" description="Capture: Email templates list. Suggested filename: admin/notifications/04-templates.png" caption="admin/notifications/04-templates.png" />

Avoid sending too many promotional messages, to prevent unsubscribes.
