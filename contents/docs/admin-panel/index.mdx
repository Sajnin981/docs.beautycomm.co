This section is intended for authorized business staff. Its proposed feature pages are listed directly beneath **Admin Panel** in the sidebar. Administrative product records, operations, configuration, and reporting are owned here rather than in customer-facing guides.

Individual workflows, access requirements, and integrations must be documented only after they are confirmed for the relevant release and business setup. A proposed page title does not confirm that a feature is currently available.

For personal account activity, see the [User Portal](/docs/user-portal). Public shopping guidance belongs in the [Storefront](/docs/storefront).
