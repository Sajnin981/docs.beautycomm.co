## Viewing Users

Review the user list to confirm who has access and whether an account matches the intended business context. Handle user information only as permitted by your role and policy.

## Creating or Inviting Users

Create or invite users only after access has been approved. Verify the identity and business context, assign the appropriate role, and communicate any required sign-in steps through approved channels.

## Editing User Information

Update only fields that are authorized and necessary. Confirm the selected user before saving, especially when a change could affect identity, notifications, or access.

## Managing Roles and Permissions

Choose the least-privileged role that allows the user to perform their work. Review access periodically and remove permissions that are no longer needed.

## Activating or Deactivating Accounts

Account status changes can immediately affect a user’s ability to work. Confirm the request and business impact before making changes, and follow the organization’s offboarding process when access is no longer required.

## Access Troubleshooting

When a user cannot access a feature, confirm their account status, business context, and assigned role. Do not resolve access problems by granting broader permissions than necessary.
