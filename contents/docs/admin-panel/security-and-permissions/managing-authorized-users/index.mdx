Learn about managing authorized users. This guide covers **Managing Authorized Users** in the Admin Panel.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to managing authorized users.

## Who can use it

Administrators and other users whose role includes administrative access.

## Where to find it

Open the Admin Panel and look for the area related to managing authorized users. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.
