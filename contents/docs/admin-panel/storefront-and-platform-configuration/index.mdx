## Business and Store Details

Confirm the correct business and store before changing shared information or preferences. Use approved business details and check how a setting may affect orders or customers.

## Shipping and Payment

Where configured, shipping settings may include delivery areas and charges. Payment settings determine which methods shoppers can choose at checkout. Confirm approved values with the responsible business owner, then test the checkout options.

## Homepage and Store Content

The store may provide homepage sections, banners, popups, and FAQs. Check text, images, links, dates, and display timing before publishing. Remove expired promotions and preview on mobile where possible.

## Connected Services

This may include social accounts, email delivery, notifications, and delivery services. Connect only approved accounts. Email and API credentials are secrets; do not put them in screenshots or support tickets. Check the integration status and verify the related workflow after setup.

## Check a Configuration Change

After saving, confirm the new value and test the affected customer or staff workflow. If the result is wrong, note the store, setting, time, and observed behavior before contacting support.
