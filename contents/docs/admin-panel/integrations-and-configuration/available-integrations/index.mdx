Integrations connect BeautyComm to other services.

## Purpose

Help you see which integrations are available to you.

## Who can use it

Administrators and other users whose role includes administrative access.

## Where to find it

Open the integrations area of the Admin Panel.

## Before you begin

- Administrator access.

## Steps

1. Open integrations.

<Screenshot step={1} alt="Integrations list" label="Integrations list" />

2. Review the list.
3. Open an integration for details.

## Expected result

You know which services are connected and available.

## Notes

- Available integrations vary by plan and version.
