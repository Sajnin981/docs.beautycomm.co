## Supplier Records and Orders

Keep supplier details and product associations accurate. Review supplier orders separately from customer orders; confirm the supplier, item, quantity, and status before acting.

## Stock Levels and Requests

Search for the product and confirm the correct variant and store. Supplier stock may appear separately from stock held by the business. A low-stock alert signals that a quantity needs review; confirm the actual count before ordering.

<Screenshot alt="BeautyComm stock list" label="Add a screenshot of stock quantities and low-stock alerts" />

## Stock Adjustments

Record receipts, returns, damage, or count corrections only from a verified source. Confirm the product and stock record, enter the approved adjustment, and check the saved quantity. Follow the business approval process for stock requests and corrections.

## Customer Order Fulfillment

Before preparing an order, check its items, quantities, payment state, address, and current status. Update the order only after the related step—such as packing or dispatch—has occurred. Add tracking details where available.

## Delivery Providers

Use provider tools only when configured for your store. The product manual describes Pathao and RedX delivery workflows. Check the order reference and latest provider status before updating a customer. If tracking does not match, record the displayed status and contact support.
