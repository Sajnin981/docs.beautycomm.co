## Business Profile Configuration

Keep business profile information accurate and aligned with the organization’s approved details. Confirm the correct business context before editing shared settings.

## Store Information

Store information can provide context used across product, order, and reporting workflows. Review details for accuracy and update them only when the change is authorized.

## Store Preferences

Preferences may affect how the workspace behaves for a store. Before changing a preference, understand who it affects and whether the change requires coordination with other users.

## Business-Specific Settings

Some settings are unique to an organization or deployment. Follow internal guidance for these options and avoid assuming that a setting has the same meaning across different businesses.

## Configuration Validation and Troubleshooting

After an approved configuration change, verify the relevant workflow in the correct environment. If behavior differs from expectation, document the setting changed, when it changed, and the observed result before escalating.
