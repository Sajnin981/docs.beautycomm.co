## Blog System

The blog system may include posts, categories, comments, menu configuration, and search metadata. A typical publishing workflow is:

1. Draft the article with an accurate title and useful body content.
2. Assign an appropriate category and author.
3. Add the available search title, description, or tags.
4. Preview the article and check links, formatting, and images.
5. Publish or schedule it according to the editorial process.
6. Review comments and moderate spam or inappropriate content.

Comment tools should be used to moderate genuine user submissions. Avoid adding fabricated reader comments.

## Beauty Blog

The beauty blog is a dedicated editorial area for beauty-related articles. It may have its own categories, comments, menu links, and featured-post selections.

Use clear categories so readers can find relevant content. Before featuring a post, confirm it is current, suitable for the audience, and approved for publication. Review comments against the same moderation policy used for the general blog.

## Community

Community tools may include user posts, images, discussions, and engagement such as likes or comments. Moderators can review reports and take action under the community rules.

When reviewing a report, assess the post and report context, record the moderation decision where the system allows, and escalate safety or privacy concerns through the organization’s established process. Do not expose a reporter’s personal details unnecessarily.

## Glowtube Video Content

Glowtube tools may provide playlists and user or staff video posts. Organize videos into relevant playlists, review post details, and use scheduling controls only when they are available and authorized.

Check video titles, descriptions, media, and intended audience before publication. Review engagement information as a signal for content performance, not as a substitute for editorial review.

## Uploaded Files

Uploaded files may include product imagery, documents, video, or other media used across the store. Before uploading, use the correct file and confirm that you have permission to publish it.

Keep files associated with the intended product or content, use clear names, and check references before deleting a file. Removing a file that is still in use can leave a broken image or document link. Avoid uploading confidential or personal information unless the feature and business policy explicitly permit it.
