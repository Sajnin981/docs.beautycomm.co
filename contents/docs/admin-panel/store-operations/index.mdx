## Dashboard and Navigation

Start from the dashboard to review available store summaries and alerts. Open the relevant section to make a change, and confirm you are working in the correct business or store before saving.

## Products and Catalog

Create or update product details using information approved by the business. Check the product name, description, category, images, price, and variants before publishing. Confirm that product availability matches the current stock record.

<Screenshot alt="BeautyComm product catalog" label="Add a screenshot of the product list or product editor with business data hidden" />

## Orders and Customers

Open an order to review its items, payment state, customer details, delivery address, and current status. Update the order only after the related business step is complete. Keep customer records accurate and access personal information only when needed for an authorized task.

If something does not match, verify the order reference and store before making a correction. Follow the business process for cancellations, returns, and customer requests.

<Screenshot alt="BeautyComm order management" label="Add a screenshot of an order detail page with customer data hidden" />

## Sales Channels

Products may be displayed through the business storefront or other channels enabled for the store. Review the channel’s product details and availability after changes. Channel options and workflows vary by business configuration.
