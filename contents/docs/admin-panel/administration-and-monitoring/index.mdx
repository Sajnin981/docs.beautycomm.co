## Administrative Activity

Activity records can help administrators understand what changes were made and when. Use them for authorized operational review and follow internal escalation procedures for unexpected activity.

## Audit Logs

Audit logs may provide a record of relevant user or administrative events. Access and retention vary by environment; handle log data according to organizational policy.

## System Status

Review available system status information when investigating a service issue. For details not exposed in the workspace, use your organization’s established support route.

## Background Jobs

Where background job information is available, use it to understand whether queued processing may affect an operational workflow. Avoid retrying or altering jobs unless authorized.

## Operational Monitoring

Use monitoring views to identify patterns or anomalies that may need follow-up. Confirm the affected service and time range before drawing conclusions.

## Maintenance Procedures

Follow approved maintenance instructions and change controls. Coordinate timing and communication with affected teams before maintenance that may impact business workflows.
