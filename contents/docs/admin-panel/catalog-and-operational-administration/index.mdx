## Product Administration

Product administration concerns the integrity and organization of catalog records. Confirm item identity and business scope before applying changes that could affect availability or customer-facing information.

## Category Administration

Categories support catalog navigation and organization. Use consistent naming and structure, and consider how changes affect product discovery and reporting.

## Inventory Administration

Inventory administration supports oversight and correction of stock data. Verify the source of a discrepancy and follow the business process before changing quantities.

## Order Administration

Order administration supports oversight of order records and workflow status. Confirm the order reference and context before making an authorized correction.

## Customer Administration

Customer administration involves personal and business data. Limit access to approved administrators and follow the organization’s privacy and retention requirements.
