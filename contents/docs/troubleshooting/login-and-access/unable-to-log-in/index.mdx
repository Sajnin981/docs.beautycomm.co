Try these checks when you cannot sign in.

## Purpose

Help you restore access.

## Who can use it

Any user.

## Where to find it

Use this guide from the sign-in page.

## Before you begin

- Your sign-in details.

## Steps

1. Check your email or username for typos.
2. Check caps lock.
3. Reset or retype your password.
4. Clear your browser cookies and try again.
5. Try another browser.
6. Ask your administrator to check that your account is active.

## Expected result

You can sign in.

## Notes

- Contact support if the problem continues. See the guide linked below.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Account locked | Wait and try again, or ask your administrator. |
