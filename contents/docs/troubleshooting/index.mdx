## Login and Access

For a business account, confirm the correct business environment, account status, and assigned role. For a shopper account, confirm that you are signing in to the correct store account and use that store’s password-recovery process.

## Product and Inventory Issues

Problems with product visibility or inventory often involve the item not being published, stock not updating, or the wrong business context being selected. Review the product or stock record before escalating.

## Order and Customer Issues

For business teams, check the order or customer record, filters, permissions, and store context. Shoppers should contact the store with the order reference if their order is missing or its status is unclear.

## Reports and Integrations

Report and integration issues typically involve filters, missing data, or delayed processing. Confirm the scope of the report and whether the underlying data source is current before escalating a problem.

## Mobile Troubleshooting

For the shopper mobile app, check your network, app version, and customer sign-in. For an order or payment problem, contact the store. Business teams should contact their BeautyComm support channel for a problem in the admin workspace.
