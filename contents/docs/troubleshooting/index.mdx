These troubleshooting steps are for BeautyComm business clients and staff. Shoppers should contact the store where they placed an order. If a suggested fix does not resolve the issue, contact support and include the relevant details from [Support and Feedback](/docs/support).

## Login and Access

**I cannot log in.**

- *Likely cause:* wrong email or password, or an inactive account.
- *What to do:* check the email spelling, then click **Forgot Password**. If the reset email does not arrive, check spam and ask your administrator to confirm your account is active.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Login and Access screenshot" label="Capture for Login and Access" description="Capture: Login error message. Suggested filename: troubleshooting/login/01-login-error.png" caption="troubleshooting/login/01-login-error.png" />

**The reset email or code did not arrive.**

- *Likely cause:* wrong email address on the account, or the message was filtered.
- *What to do:* check spam and junk folders, wait a few minutes, then ask your administrator to confirm your email address.

**I am logged in but cannot see a page or button.**

- *Likely cause:* your role does not include that permission.
- *What to do:* ask your administrator to review your role in **Staff & Role Permissions**. Log out and log in again after the change.

**I keep getting logged out.**

- *Likely cause:* session timeout for security, or browser settings blocking cookies.
- *What to do:* log in again, allow cookies for the Admin Panel, and avoid using the same account on many devices at the same time.

## Product and Inventory Issues

**A product does not appear on the storefront.**

- *Likely cause:* the status is not Active, there is no stock, the category is hidden, or the required fields are missing.
- *What to do:* open the product and check its status, category and stock. Save, then refresh the storefront.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Product and Inventory Issues screenshot" label="Capture for Product and Inventory Issues" description="Capture: Product status and visibility settings. Suggested filename: troubleshooting/products/01-visibility.png" caption="troubleshooting/products/01-visibility.png" />

**Stock numbers look wrong.**

- *Likely cause:* an order, return or manual change has not been recorded, or stock is set per shade or location.
- *What to do:* open **Inventory** and check the stock history for the product to see each change. Correct it with **Adjust Stock** and add a reason.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Product and Inventory Issues screenshot" label="Capture for Product and Inventory Issues" description="Capture: Stock history list. Suggested filename: troubleshooting/products/02-stock-history.png" caption="troubleshooting/products/02-stock-history.png" />

**A shade or variation cannot be selected.**

- *Likely cause:* that variation has zero stock or is inactive.
- *What to do:* open the product, go to **Variations**, and check the stock and status of each option.

**Product images do not show or look stretched.**

- *Likely cause:* the file is too large, the format is unsupported or the sizes are wrong.
- *What to do:* upload a compressed JPG, PNG or WebP at the recommended size [add size], and replace the old image.

**A price or discount is not showing correctly.**

- *Likely cause:* a flash sale, coupon or campaign is overriding the normal price, or it has expired.
- *What to do:* check **Flash Sales** and **Coupons** for rules that apply to the product, and check their dates.

## Order and Customer Issues

**An order is missing.**

- *Likely cause:* the payment was not completed, the order is under a different status filter, or the search used the wrong details.
- *What to do:* clear all filters, search by order number or phone number, and check failed or pending payments.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Order and Customer Issues screenshot" label="Capture for Order and Customer Issues" description="Capture: Orders list with filters cleared. Suggested filename: troubleshooting/orders/01-missing-order.png" caption="troubleshooting/orders/01-missing-order.png" />

**I cannot change an order's status.**

- *Likely cause:* the order is already cancelled or completed, or your role does not allow it.
- *What to do:* check the current status and your role. Ask an administrator if you need the permission.

**A customer says they did not get a notification or email.**

- *Likely cause:* wrong email, spam filter or notifications turned off.
- *What to do:* check the customer's email in their profile, ask them to check spam, and check **Email & Push Notification Management** and the templates.

**A customer cannot find their address or reward points.**

- *Likely cause:* they are logged in with a different account, or points have not been added yet.
- *What to do:* open the customer in **Customers**, check the details and points history, and adjust if needed with a reason.

**A refund has not reached the customer.**

- *Likely cause:* refunds take time depending on the payment method, or the refund has not been marked as processed.
- *What to do:* open the return in **Returns** and check its refund status and method. Tell the customer the expected timeline from your Returns & Refund Policy.

## Reports and Integrations

**Report numbers do not match what I expected.**

- *Likely cause:* a different date range, filter or time zone is selected.
- *What to do:* check the date range and filters, and confirm the store time zone in **Settings > General**. Refunded and cancelled orders may also be counted differently.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Reports and Integrations screenshot" label="Capture for Reports and Integrations" description="Capture: Report filters and date range. Suggested filename: troubleshooting/reports/01-filters.png" caption="troubleshooting/reports/01-filters.png" />

**An export does not download.**

- *Likely cause:* the browser blocked the download or the report is too large.
- *What to do:* allow downloads for the Admin Panel, narrow the date range and try again.

**Facebook Pixel or Google Tag Manager does not track events.**

- *Likely cause:* a wrong ID, the integration is turned off or an ad blocker is on.
- *What to do:* re-check the ID in the settings, save, then test with Facebook Pixel Helper or Tag Manager Preview mode in a browser without ad blockers.

**The courier or payment gateway does not connect.**

- *Likely cause:* wrong keys, Sandbox mode instead of Live mode, or an inactive account with the provider.
- *What to do:* re-enter the credentials from your provider, check the mode and run **Test Connection**. Never share keys in screenshots or messages.

**Social login does not work.**

- *Likely cause:* a wrong Client ID or Secret, or the callback URL is missing in the provider's app settings.
- *What to do:* copy the callback URL shown in **Social Login Configuration** into the provider's app, and re-enter the credentials.

## Mobile Troubleshooting

**The app will not install or update.**

- *Likely cause:* not enough storage, an old phone system version or a store problem.
- *What to do:* free up storage, check that your phone meets the minimum requirements [add versions], and try again from the Play Store or App Store.

**The app crashes or shows a blank screen.**

- *Likely cause:* an outdated app version or corrupted cache.
- *What to do:* update the app, close and reopen it, clear the app cache (Android), or reinstall it. Log in again after reinstalling.

**Push notifications do not arrive.**

- *Likely cause:* notifications are blocked in phone settings, battery saver is on, or they are turned off inside the app.
- *What to do:* allow notifications for BeautyComm in your phone's settings, turn off battery restrictions for the app, and check **Account > Settings > Notifications**.

   <Screenshot alt="BeautyComm support Mobile Troubleshooting screenshot" label="Capture for Mobile Troubleshooting" description="Capture: Phone settings, app notification screen. Suggested filename: troubleshooting/mobile/01-notifications.png" caption="troubleshooting/mobile/01-notifications.png" />

**The camera or barcode scanner does not work.**

- *Likely cause:* camera permission was denied.
- *What to do:* allow camera access for the app in your phone's settings and try again.

**Products or prices differ between the app and the website.**

- *Likely cause:* the app is showing cached data or an old version.
- *What to do:* pull down to refresh, update the app, and check that the change was saved in the Admin Panel.
