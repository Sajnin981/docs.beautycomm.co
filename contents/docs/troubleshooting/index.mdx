These troubleshooting steps are for BeautyComm business clients and staff. Shoppers should contact the store where they placed an order. If a suggested fix does not resolve the issue, contact support and include the relevant details from [Support and Feedback](/docs/support).

## Sign In and Access

**I cannot sign in.**

- Why this happens: wrong email or password, or an inactive account.
- How to fix it: check the email spelling, then click **Forgot Password**. If the reset email does not arrive, check spam and ask your administrator to confirm your account is active.

**The reset email or code did not arrive.**

- Why this happens: wrong email address on the account, or the message was filtered.
- How to fix it: check spam and junk folders, wait a few minutes, then ask your administrator to confirm your email address.

**I am logged in but cannot see a page or button.**

- Why this happens: your role does not include that permission.
- How to fix it: ask your administrator to review your role in **Staff & Role Permissions**. Log out and sign in again after the change.

**I keep getting logged out.**

- Why this happens: session timeout for security, or browser settings blocking cookies.
- How to fix it: sign in again, allow cookies for the Admin Panel, and avoid using the same account on many devices at the same time.

## Product and Inventory Issues

**A product does not appear on the storefront.**

- Why this happens: the status is not Active, there is no stock, the category is hidden, or the required fields are missing.
- How to fix it: open the product and check its status, category and stock. Save, then refresh the storefront.

**Stock numbers look wrong.**

- Why this happens: an order, return or manual change has not been recorded, or stock is set per shade or location.
- How to fix it: open **Inventory** and check the stock history for the product to see each change. Correct it with **Adjust Stock** and add a reason.

**A shade or variation cannot be selected.**

- Why this happens: that variation has zero stock or is inactive.
- How to fix it: open the product, go to **Variations**, and check the stock and status of each option.

**Product images do not show or look stretched.**

- Why this happens: the file is too large, the format is unsupported or the sizes are wrong.
- How to fix it: upload a compressed JPG, PNG or WebP sized for the storefront, and replace the old image.

**A price or discount is not showing correctly.**

- Why this happens: a flash sale, coupon or campaign is overriding the normal price, or it has expired.
- How to fix it: check **Flash Sales** and **Coupons** for rules that apply to the product, and check their dates.

## Order and Customer Issues

**An order is missing.**

- Why this happens: the payment was not completed, the order is under a different status filter, or the search used the wrong details.
- How to fix it: clear all filters, search by order number or phone number, and check failed or pending payments.

**I cannot change an order's status.**

- Why this happens: the order is already cancelled or completed, or your role does not allow it.
- How to fix it: check the current status and your role. Ask an administrator if you need the permission.

**A customer says they did not get a notification or email.**

- Why this happens: wrong email, spam filter or notifications turned off.
- How to fix it: check the customer's email in their profile, ask them to check spam, and check **Email & Push Notification Management** and the templates.

**A customer cannot find their address or reward points.**

- Why this happens: they are logged in with a different account, or points have not been added yet.
- How to fix it: open the customer in **Customers**, check the details and points history, and adjust if needed with a reason.

**A refund has not reached the customer.**

- Why this happens: refunds take time depending on the payment method, or the refund has not been marked as processed.
- How to fix it: open the return in **Returns** and check its refund status and method. Tell the customer the expected timeline from your Returns & Refund Policy.

## Reports and Integrations

**Report numbers do not match what I expected.**

- Why this happens: a different date range, filter or time zone is selected.
- How to fix it: check the date range and filters, and confirm the store time zone in **Settings > General**. Refunded and cancelled orders may also be counted differently.

**An export does not download.**

- Why this happens: the browser blocked the download or the report is too large.
- How to fix it: allow downloads for the Admin Panel, narrow the date range and try again.

**Facebook Pixel or Google Tag Manager does not track events.**

- Why this happens: a wrong ID, the integration is turned off or an ad blocker is on.
- How to fix it: re-check the ID in the settings, save, then test with Facebook Pixel Helper or Tag Manager Preview mode in a browser without ad blockers.

**The courier or payment gateway does not connect.**

- Why this happens: wrong keys, Sandbox mode instead of Live mode, or an inactive account with the provider.
- How to fix it: re-enter the credentials from your provider, check the mode and run **Test Connection**. Never share keys in messages.

**Social Login does not work.**

- Why this happens: a wrong Client ID or Secret, or the callback URL is missing in the provider's app settings.
- How to fix it: copy the callback URL shown in **Social Login Configuration** into the provider's app, and re-enter the credentials.

## Mobile Troubleshooting

**The app will not install or update.**

- Why this happens: not enough storage, an old phone system version or a store problem.
- How to fix it: free up storage, check that your phone meets the app requirements, and try again from the Play Store or App Store.

**The app crashes or shows a blank screen.**

- Why this happens: an outdated app version or corrupted cache.
- How to fix it: update the app, close and reopen it, clear the app cache (Android), or reinstall it. Sign in again after reinstalling.

**Push notifications do not arrive.**

- Why this happens: notifications are blocked in phone settings, battery saver is on, or they are turned off inside the app.
- How to fix it: allow notifications for BeautyComm in your phone's settings, turn off battery restrictions for the app, and check **Account > Settings > Notifications**.

**The camera or barcode scanner does not work.**

- Why this happens: camera permission was denied.
- How to fix it: allow camera access for the app in your phone's settings and try again.

**Products or prices differ between the app and the website.**

- Why this happens: the app is showing cached data or an old version.
- How to fix it: pull down to refresh, update the app, and check that the change was saved in the Admin Panel.
