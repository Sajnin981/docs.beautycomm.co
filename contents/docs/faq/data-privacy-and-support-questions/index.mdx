These questions are most relevant when you need to understand account data, review policy information, or escalate a problem to the proper support channel.

## Where do I get help?

Start with the most relevant troubleshooting guide or the support page in [Support and Feedback](/docs/support). If the issue affects a live workflow or access problem, ask the official support channel for your business.

## What information should I provide when reporting an issue?

Share the exact workflow, the date and time of the issue, the account role, and any screenshots or error messages. This helps support investigate quickly and avoid unnecessary back-and-forth.

## Is it safe to share my password or verification codes?

No. Never share your password, one-time verification codes, or other account security details with anyone, including support representatives, unless you are using the official authentication flow provided by your organization.

## Where can I find privacy or legal information?

Check the official privacy and legal links provided by your organization or BeautyComm team. See [Privacy and Legal Links](/docs/support#privacy-and-legal-links).
