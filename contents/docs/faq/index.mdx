## BeautyComm for Businesses

BeautyComm is e-commerce software used by businesses to manage their store, products, stock, customer orders, engagement, and reporting. Business clients and their authorized teams use the [Business Admin Panel](/docs/admin-panel).

## Business Accounts and Permissions

These answers are for business clients and staff using their BeautyComm account. Shoppers do not need business-admin permissions to browse or buy from a store.

## Products, Orders, and Store Operations

These topics cover business workflows such as product listing, stock management, fulfillment, and customer records. For shopper order or delivery questions, contact the store that accepted the order.

## Customer Shopping and Mobile App

These answers are for customers browsing, checking out, or managing a purchase on a store website or supported mobile app. The store sets its own availability, payment, delivery, and return options. See the [Shopper Portal](/docs/user-portal).

## Privacy and Getting Support

Shoppers should contact the store using its published support details. Business clients should use the BeautyComm support contact assigned to their business. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or full payment-card details in support messages.
