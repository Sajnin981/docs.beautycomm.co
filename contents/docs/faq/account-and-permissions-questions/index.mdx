These questions usually come up when a user cannot see a menu item, a workflow is missing, or an account seems to have limited access.

## Why can I not see a feature?

The most common cause is account permissions. A user may be signed in with a valid account but still not have access to a specific part of the platform because their role does not include it.

## How do I check whether I have the right access?

Start by confirming that you are signed into the correct business environment and that your account is active. Then review your role and the tasks you are expected to complete.

## Who should I contact if access is missing?

Speak with your administrator or the support team for your business. They can confirm whether the access level is expected for your role or if a configuration change is needed.

## Why do I see different menu items than another user?

BeautyComm often presents different parts of the system based on role and business context. If menus or workflows look different, it is usually because the current user role does not include that feature.
