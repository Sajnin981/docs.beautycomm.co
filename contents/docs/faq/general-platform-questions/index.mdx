These are the most common starting questions for new and returning BeautyComm users.

## What is BeautyComm?

BeautyComm is a digital workspace for beauty business operations, product and inventory work, and customer-facing or internal operational workflows. See [What Is BeautyComm?](/docs/getting-started#what-is-beautycomm).

## Where do I start?

Start with [Welcome to BeautyComm](/docs/getting-started) and then move into the most relevant task guides in [Common Tasks](/docs/common-tasks).

## Is there a mobile app?

The documentation includes a mobile app section if your business uses the mobile experience. See [Mobile App Guides](/docs/mobile-app).

## Where do I get help?

Start with the relevant troubleshooting guide or [Support and Feedback](/docs/support). For direct help, see [How to Contact BeautyComm Support](/docs/support/how-to-contact-beautycomm-support).

## Why do I not see some menu items?

Menu visibility depends on the account and role assigned to you. If a feature or section is missing, confirm your permissions and role context.
