These are the questions teams most often ask when working with products, stock, and orders in BeautyComm.

## Where do I find a product?

Use the product or catalog area to search, filter, and open the item you need. If the item is missing, check whether it has been published and whether your account has the right access level for that area.

## Why is a product not showing up?

A product may be hidden by filters, not yet published, or outside the current business context. Review the product record and confirm that the correct catalog or store view is selected.

## How do I update stock information?

Open the relevant inventory screen, review the item details, and update the available quantity or status based on your process. If the change does not save, review permissions and check whether the inventory record is locked or still syncing.

## How do I find an order?

Use the order list with filters such as status, date, or customer reference. If you cannot find the order, check whether the order belongs to a different business context or status bucket.

## Why is an order not updating?

This usually occurs when permissions are limited, the record is loading slowly, or the process is still in progress. Refresh the page, confirm you have access, and review the latest status in the order details.

## What should I do if customer information is missing?

Check that the customer record exists and that the correct business context is selected. If the record is incomplete, confirm whether the data was not entered yet or if permissions restrict visibility.

## Where should I go for support?

If the issue continues after checking access and record details, use the support guidance in [Support and Feedback](/docs/support) and include the exact workflow, item, and error you encountered.
