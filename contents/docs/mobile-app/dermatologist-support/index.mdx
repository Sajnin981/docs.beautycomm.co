Get guidance on skin concerns. To request support:

1. Tap **Dermatologist Support**.
2. Choose a dermatologist.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Dermatologist Support screen" label="Phone capture for Dermatologist Support" description="Capture: Dermatologist list. Suggested filename: mobile/dermatologist/01-list.png" caption="mobile/dermatologist/01-list.png" />

3. Tap **Request Consultation**, describe the concern and add a photo if needed.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Dermatologist Support screen" label="Phone capture for Dermatologist Support" description="Capture: Request form with photo upload. Suggested filename: mobile/dermatologist/02-form.png" caption="mobile/dermatologist/02-form.png" />

This service does not replace emergency medical care.
