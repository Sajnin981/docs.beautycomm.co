The app suggests products based on what you view and buy. To see them:

1. Look for **Recommended for You** on the home screen.

2. Swipe sideways to see more, and tap a product to open it.
3. On a product screen, scroll down to **You May Also Like**.

Log in to get the most relevant suggestions.
