Use coupons to save on your order. To use them:

1. Tap **Account**, then **Coupons**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Coupons &amp; Discounts screen" label="Phone capture for Coupons &amp; Discounts" description="Capture: Coupons screen. Suggested filename: mobile/coupons/01-list.png" caption="mobile/coupons/01-list.png" />

2. Tap **Collect** on a coupon to save it.
3. In the cart or at checkout, tap the coupon field, enter or select your code and tap **Apply**.
