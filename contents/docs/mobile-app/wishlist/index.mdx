Save products to buy later. To use the wishlist:

1. Tap the **heart icon** on a product card or product screen.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Wishlist screen" label="Phone capture for Wishlist" description="Capture: Product card with heart icon highlighted. Suggested filename: mobile/wishlist/01-heart.png" caption="mobile/wishlist/01-heart.png" />

2. To see saved items, tap **Account** and then **Wishlist**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Wishlist screen" label="Phone capture for Wishlist" description="Capture: Wishlist screen. Suggested filename: mobile/wishlist/02-wishlist.png" caption="mobile/wishlist/02-wishlist.png" />

3. Tap **Add to Cart** to buy an item, or tap the heart again to remove it.
