## Wishlist

Your Wishlist contains products saved for later. Use it to review saved items, compare available options, and add products to your cart when ready.

## Saved Products

The Wishlist keeps products you like together until you are ready to buy them.

- Tap the heart on any product to save it.
- Open the Wishlist to see the products saved on your account.
- Review availability, prices and variations before buying a saved item.

## Manage Wishlist

Manage the list whenever your preferences change.

- Tap **Add To Cart** to move an available item into your cart.
- Tap the heart again to remove a product from the list.
