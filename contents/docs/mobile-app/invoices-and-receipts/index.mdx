Download your invoice from the app. To get it:

1. Open **My Orders** and tap a completed order.
2. Tap **Download Invoice**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Invoices &amp; Receipts screen" label="Phone capture for Invoices &amp; Receipts" description="Capture: Order details with invoice button. Suggested filename: mobile/invoices/01-button.png" caption="mobile/invoices/01-button.png" />

3. The invoice opens as a PDF. Tap **Share** or **Save** to keep it on your phone.
