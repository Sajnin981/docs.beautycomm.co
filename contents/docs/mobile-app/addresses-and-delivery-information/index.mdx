Save delivery addresses for faster checkout. To manage them:

1. Tap **Account**, then **Addresses**.

2. Tap **Add New Address**, fill in the details and tap **Save**. You can use **Current Location** to fill in the address [if available].

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Addresses &amp; Delivery Information screen" label="Phone capture for Addresses &amp; Delivery Information" description="Capture: Add address form. Suggested filename: mobile/addresses/02-add.png" caption="mobile/addresses/02-add.png" />

3. Tap an address to edit or delete it, and choose **Set as Default**.
