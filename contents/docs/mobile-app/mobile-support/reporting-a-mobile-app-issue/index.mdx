Learn about reporting a mobile app issue. This guide covers **Reporting a Mobile App Issue** in the mobile app.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to reporting a mobile app issue.

## Who can use it

Authorized users signed in to the BeautyComm mobile app. Some features may be available only on the web application.

## Where to find it

Open the mobile app and look for the area related to reporting a mobile app issue. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.

## Steps

Detailed step-by-step instructions for this guide are being prepared and will be published once they are verified against the live product.

## Expected result

You can find the related area and understand what it offers.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot see this area | Your role may not include it. Ask your account administrator. |
| The page does not load | Refresh the page, then sign out and back in. If it continues, contact support. |
