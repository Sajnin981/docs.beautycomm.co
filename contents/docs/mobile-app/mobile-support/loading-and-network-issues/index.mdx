Learn about loading and network issues. This guide covers **Loading and Network Issues** in the mobile app.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to loading and network issues.

## Who can use it

Authorized users signed in to the BeautyComm mobile app. Some features may be available only on the web application.

## Where to find it

Open the mobile app and look for the area related to loading and network issues. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.
