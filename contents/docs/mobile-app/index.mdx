This section is for customers shopping from a supported BeautyComm-powered mobile app. Mobile App Guides organize customer help by task, from accessing the app to shopping and managing personal activity. Business owners and staff should use the [Admin Panel](/docs/admin-portal), not the shopper app, for store operations.

## What You Can Do

Depending on the store and app version, the mobile experience may include product browsing and search, product details, cart and checkout, order history and tracking, wishlists, profile settings, notifications, rewards, and support. The store decides which features are enabled.

## App Availability and Safety

Get the app from the store’s official website or an official app-store listing. If you are unsure whether an app is genuine, contact the store using the contact information on its official website. Do not install apps sent through an unexpected message, and never share your password or verification code with someone offering app support.
