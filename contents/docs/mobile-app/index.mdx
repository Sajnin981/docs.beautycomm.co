This section is for **customers shopping from a supported BeautyComm-powered mobile app**. Use it to find products, place and follow orders, and manage customer-account features. It is not the business admin app. Business owners and staff should use the [Business Admin Panel](/docs/admin-panel).

## What You Can Do

Depending on the store and app version, the mobile experience may include product browsing and search, product details, cart and checkout, order history and tracking, wishlist, profile, notifications, rewards, and support. The store decides which features are enabled.

For step-by-step shopping instructions, see [Shop and Place an Order](/docs/user-portal/storefront-and-shopping). For account information, see [Manage Your Customer Account](/docs/user-portal/customer-account).

## App Availability and Safety

Get the app from the store’s official website or an official app-store listing. If you are unsure whether an app is genuine, contact the store using the contact information on its official website. Do not install apps sent through an unexpected message, and never share your password or verification code with someone offering app support.
