Install the app from your device store.

## Purpose

Show how to install.

## Who can use it

Authorized users signed in to the BeautyComm mobile app. Some features may be available only on the web application.

## Where to find it

Your device app store.

## Before you begin

- An internet connection and free storage.

## Steps

1. Open your app store.
2. Search for BeautyComm.

<Screenshot step={2} alt="App store listing" label="App store listing" />

3. Install the app.
4. Open it and sign in.

## Expected result

The app opens and shows the sign-in screen.

## Notes

- Install only the official BeautyComm app.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| App not found | Your region or device may not be supported. |
