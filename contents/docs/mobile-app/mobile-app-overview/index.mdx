## What the Shopper App Is For

The app may let you browse products, use a cart and checkout, check orders, save wishlist items, review rewards, and manage customer details. Available features depend on the store and app version. Business operations such as stock editing or staff access belong in the business admin workspace, not the shopper app.

## Supported Devices

Check the store’s official app listing for supported devices and operating-system versions. Make sure your device can connect to the internet and has enough available storage.

## Install the Official App

Use the official app-store listing or the download link on the store’s official website. Confirm the app name and publisher before installing. Avoid third-party download sites or unsolicited links.

## Update the App

Install updates through the same official source used for the original app. Updates can improve compatibility and provide the latest supported shopping features.

## Sign In and Out

Sign in with the customer account you use for that store and complete any requested verification. Sign out when using a shared device. Do not share your password, reset link, or one-time verification code.
