Follow your order until it arrives. To track it:

1. Open **My Orders** and tap the order.
2. Tap **Track Order**.

3. Check the timeline to see the current stage, for example Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery.

Turn on notifications to get an alert when the status changes.
