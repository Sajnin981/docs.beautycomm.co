Check that your product is genuine. To verify it:

1. Tap **Verify Product** [from the home screen or Account menu].

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Authenticity screen" label="Phone capture for Product Authenticity" description="Capture: Verify Product entry point. Suggested filename: mobile/authenticity/01-entry.png" caption="mobile/authenticity/01-entry.png" />

2. Allow camera access when asked.
3. Point your camera at the QR code or barcode on the packaging, or enter the code manually.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Authenticity screen" label="Phone capture for Product Authenticity" description="Capture: Camera scan screen. Suggested filename: mobile/authenticity/02-scan.png" caption="mobile/authenticity/02-scan.png" />

4. The result screen shows whether the product is verified.
