## Product Page

The product page shows the photos, name, price, rating, description, size and usage information needed before purchase.

1. Open a product from the Home screen, search results or a category.
2. Check the photos, quantity, price and availability.
3. Review the description, ingredients and usage notes below the title.
4. Choose a quantity, then tap **Add To Cart** or the heart to save it.

## Shades & Variations

Some products have several shades, sizes or variants with different prices or stock.

1. Open the product and select the shade or variation near the price.
2. The photo, price and availability update for the selected option.
3. Unavailable options appear disabled; choose another or save the product to the wishlist.
4. Add the chosen variation to the cart and confirm its name in the cart.

## Verify Product

Verify Product lets you check that a purchased item is genuine.

1. Find the authenticity code or QR mark on the packaging.
2. Open Verify Product and enter the code, or scan the mark if scanning is available.
3. A genuine result shows the product details.
4. If the code is not recognized, contact Support before using the product.

## Reviews

Reviews help other shoppers choose products and help BeautyComm improve its catalogue.

1. Open a delivered order, or a product purchased previously.
2. Select a star rating and write a short description of your experience.
3. Submit the review.
4. It may appear after the store team performs a quick check.

## Sharing

Share a product you like with friends or family in one step.

1. Open the product page.
2. Tap the share option.
3. Choose an app or copy the product link.
4. Send the link. The recipient opens directly to the same product.
