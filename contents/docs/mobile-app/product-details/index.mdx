The product screen shows everything you need to decide. To read it:

1. Tap a product to open it.
2. Swipe the images left or right, and pinch to zoom.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Details screen" label="Phone capture for Product Details" description="Capture: Top of the product screen with callouts on images, price, rating, stock. Suggested filename: mobile/product/01-top.png" caption="mobile/product/01-top.png" />

3. Scroll down for the description, ingredients, how to use and reviews.

4. Tap **Add to Cart** or **Buy Now** at the bottom of the screen.
