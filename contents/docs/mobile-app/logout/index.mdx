Log out to protect your account, especially on a shared phone. To log out:

1. Tap **Account**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Logout**, then confirm.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Logout screen" label="Phone capture for Logout" description="Capture: Account screen with Logout highlighted. Suggested filename: mobile/logout/01-logout.png" caption="mobile/logout/01-logout.png" />

Your cart and wishlist stay saved in your account for your next login.
