Your dashboard shows your account at a glance. To open it:

1. Tap **Account** in the bottom bar.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Dashboard screen" label="Phone capture for Dashboard" description="Capture: Bottom bar with Account highlighted. Suggested filename: mobile/dashboard/01-account-tab.png" caption="mobile/dashboard/01-account-tab.png" />

2. Check the summary at the top: your name, reward points and membership level.
3. Tap a shortcut such as **My Orders**, **Wishlist**, **Coupons** or **Addresses** to go to that section.
