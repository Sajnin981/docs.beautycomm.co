## Place an Order

Finish your purchase in a few steps.

1. Under **Your Order**, review the items and quantities.
2. Check **Subtotal**, **Shipping Cost**, **Discount**, and **Total**.
3. Confirm your delivery details under **Deliver to**.
   ![Checkout order summary](/images/mobile/checkout-summary.jpg)
4. Add a note under **Notes about your order** if you need to.
5. Choose **Cash on Delivery**, **bKash**, or **Pay Online**.
6. Tick the box to agree to the Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy.
   ![Checkout payment options](/images/mobile/checkout-payment.jpg)
7. Tap **Place My Order** and wait for the confirmation screen.

## Addresses

Save your delivery address once and reuse it.

1. At checkout, tap **Change Delivery Address** to pick a saved address.
2. Or fill in the form: **Name**, **Phone**, **Email** (optional), **Address**, **City**, and **Zone**.
   ![Checkout address form](/images/mobile/checkout-address-form.jpg)
3. Fields marked with * are required.
4. Check your details before you place the order.

## Reward Points

Use the points you earned to pay less.

1. At checkout, find **Your Balance** to see your points.
2. Tap **REDEEM POINTS** to apply them.
   ![Your Balance with Redeem Points](/images/mobile/checkout-rewards.jpg)
3. Check that the **Discount** line updates before you place the order.
