## Place Order

Finish your purchase in a few steps.


1. Review the items and the Sub Total, Shipping and Total.
2. Confirm your delivery details.
3. Choose Online Payment, bKash or Cash on Delivery.
4. Tap **Place Order** and wait for the confirmation screen.

## Addresses

Save your delivery address once and reuse it.

- Tap Addresses to see the ones you have saved.
- Add a new one with your name, phone, city, zone, area and full address.
- Choose which address to use for the order and set one as default if available.

## Reward Points

Use the points you earned to pay less.

1. At checkout, look for your Reward Points balance.
2. Choose how many points to apply.
3. Check that the Discount line updates before you place the order.
