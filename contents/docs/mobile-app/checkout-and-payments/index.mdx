Place your order in a few taps. To check out:

1. From the cart, tap **Checkout**.
2. Select or add a delivery address.

3. Choose a delivery method.
4. Choose a payment method, such as [card, mobile banking, cash on delivery].

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Checkout &amp; Payments screen" label="Phone capture for Checkout &amp; Payments" description="Capture: Payment method list. Suggested filename: mobile/checkout/02-payment.png" caption="mobile/checkout/02-payment.png" />

5. Review the order summary and tap **Place Order**.

6. For online payment, complete the payment on the screen that opens, then return to the app. A confirmation screen shows your order number.
