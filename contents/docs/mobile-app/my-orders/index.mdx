See all your orders and their status. To open them:

1. Tap **Account**, then **My Orders**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile My Orders screen" label="Phone capture for My Orders" description="Capture: My Orders list with status labels. Suggested filename: mobile/orders/01-list.png" caption="mobile/orders/01-list.png" />

2. Use the tabs at the top to view Pending, Shipped, Delivered or Cancelled orders.
3. Tap an order to see items, address, payment and total.

4. To cancel an order that has not shipped, tap **Cancel Order** and choose a reason.
