## Order List

See all your purchases in one list.

- Open My Orders to see them newest first.
- Each row shows the order number, date, total and status.
- Tap an order to see its items and delivery details.

## Tracking

Follow your parcel from your phone.

1. Open the order and tap **Track Order**.
2. Read the current status and the steps still to come.
3. Contact Help & Support if it has not changed for longer than expected.

## Invoices

Get the invoice for an order.

- Open the order and tap **Invoice**.
- Check the items and totals.
- Save or share the invoice from the same screen.

## Returns

Request a return or refund without leaving the app.

1. Open the order and tap **Return**.
2. Pick the item and reason, and add a photo if it helps.
3. Submit the request.
4. Follow its status until the refund is completed.
