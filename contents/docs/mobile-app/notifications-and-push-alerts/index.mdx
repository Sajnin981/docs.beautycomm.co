Get alerts for orders, offers and updates. To manage them:

1. Tap the **bell icon** on the home screen to see your notifications.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Notifications &amp; Push Alerts screen" label="Phone capture for Notifications &amp; Push Alerts" description="Capture: Notification list. Suggested filename: mobile/notifications/01-list.png" caption="mobile/notifications/01-list.png" />

2. To choose which alerts you receive, tap **Account**, then **Settings**, then **Notifications**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Notifications &amp; Push Alerts screen" label="Phone capture for Notifications &amp; Push Alerts" description="Capture: Notification settings toggles. Suggested filename: mobile/notifications/02-settings.png" caption="mobile/notifications/02-settings.png" />

3. If you do not receive alerts, check that notifications are allowed for BeautyComm in your phone's settings.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Notifications &amp; Push Alerts screen" label="Phone capture for Notifications &amp; Push Alerts" description="Capture: Phone system settings, app notification screen.
