Keep your details up to date. To edit your profile:

1. Tap **Account**, then your **profile picture** or **Edit Profile**.

2. Update your name, email, phone or photo. To change the photo, choose camera or gallery.

3. Tap **Save**.
4. For password and other settings, tap **Settings**, then **Change Password**.
