Keep your details up to date. To edit your profile:

1. Tap **Account**, then your **profile picture** or **Edit Profile**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Profile &amp; Account Settings screen" label="Phone capture for Profile &amp; Account Settings" description="Capture: Profile screen. Suggested filename: mobile/profile/01-profile.png" caption="mobile/profile/01-profile.png" />

2. Update your name, email, phone or photo. To change the photo, choose camera or gallery.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Profile &amp; Account Settings screen" label="Phone capture for Profile &amp; Account Settings" description="Capture: Edit profile with photo options. Suggested filename: mobile/profile/02-edit.png" caption="mobile/profile/02-edit.png" />

3. Tap **Save**.
4. For password and other settings, tap **Settings**, then **Change Password**.
