Find products quickly with search and filters. To use them:

1. Tap the **search bar** at the top of the home screen.

2. Type a product, brand or concern, and tap a suggestion or the search key.
3. On the results screen, tap **Filter**.

4. Choose brand, price range, skin type, rating or other options, then tap **Apply**.

5. To clear filters, tap **Reset** or remove the filter tags above the results.
