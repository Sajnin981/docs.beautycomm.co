Learn about finding available features. This guide covers **Finding Available Features** in the mobile app.

## Purpose

Help you understand and complete tasks related to finding available features.

## Who can use it

Authorized users signed in to the BeautyComm mobile app. Some features may be available only on the web application.

## Where to find it

Open the mobile app and look for the area related to finding available features. Screens, labels and options can differ depending on your role, plan and BeautyComm version. If something looks different in your account, ask your account administrator.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your BeautyComm account.
- Confirm that your role gives you access to this area.
