## Browse Products
Browse the catalogue by swiping through categories and product lists.

1. Tap **Shop** in the bottom menu.
2. Scroll the product list, or tap the menu icon (☰) to pick a category.
3. Tap a product to open its page.
4. Tap **Add to cart** to buy it.
   ![Shop screen with Add to cart](/images/mobile/shop-browse.jpg)

## Search & Filters

Find exactly what you want in a few taps.

- Tap **Search for product** and type a product or brand name.
- Tap the **filter** icon next to the search bar to narrow the results.
  ![Search bar and filter icon](/images/mobile/shop-search-filter.jpg)
- Open the menu (☰) and choose a category such as K Beauty, J-Beauty, or Makeup.
  ![Menu with categories](/images/mobile/menu-categories.jpg)
- Clear the filters to return to the full list.

## Skincare Routine

Build a routine for your skin directly on your phone.

1. Open Skincare Routine and select your skin type and concerns.
2. Review the suggested morning and evening steps.
3. Tap a product for details, or add the routine to your cart.

## Consultation

Ask a beauty expert a question from the app.

- Tap **Consultation** and describe your concern.
- Add your skin type and the products you already use.
- Send the request, then check Notifications for the reply.

## Dermatologist

Request guidance from a dermatologist when a skin issue needs more than product advice.

1. Open Dermatologist and choose a specialist.
2. Describe the problem and attach a clear photo if you can.
3. Send it and wait for the reply in the app.
4. See a clinic in person if the problem is serious.
