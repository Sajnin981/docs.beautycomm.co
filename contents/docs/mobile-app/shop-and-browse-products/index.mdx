Browse the full BeautyComm range from your phone. To browse:

1. Tap **Categories** in the bottom bar, or tap a category on the home screen.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Shop &amp; Browse Products screen" label="Phone capture for Shop &amp; Browse Products" description="Capture: Categories screen. Suggested filename: mobile/shop/01-categories.png" caption="mobile/shop/01-categories.png" />

2. Tap a category to open its product list.
3. Scroll through the product grid. Each card shows the image, name, price and rating.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Shop &amp; Browse Products screen" label="Phone capture for Shop &amp; Browse Products" description="Capture: Product listing grid. Suggested filename: mobile/shop/02-listing.png" caption="mobile/shop/02-listing.png" />

4. Tap **Sort** to change the order, and tap a product to open it.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Shop &amp; Browse Products screen" label="Phone capture for Shop &amp; Browse Products" description="Capture: Sort options sheet. Suggested filename: mobile/shop/03-sort.png" caption="mobile/shop/03-sort.png" />
