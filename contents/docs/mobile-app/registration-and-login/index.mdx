## Sign Up

1. Open the app. It opens on the Home screen.
2. Tap the profile (account) icon at the top right.
   ![Profile icon at the top right of the Home screen](/images/mobile/home-icon.jpg)
3. The Login and Register screen opens. Tap the **Register** tab.
4. Enter your phone number and tap **Send OTP**.
   ![Register tab with phone number and Send OTP](/images/mobile/signup-register.jpg)
5. Enter the verification code (OTP) you receive.
6. When it is verified, your account is ready and you stay on your Dashboard.

## Sign in

1. Open the app and tap the profile (account) icon at the top right.
   ![Profile icon at the top right of the Home screen](/images/mobile/home-icon.jpg)
2. The Login and Register screen opens with the **Sign In** tab selected.
3. Enter your phone number and tap **Send OTP**, then enter the code you receive.
4. Or tap **Login with Password** to sign in with a password.
   ![Sign In tab with Send OTP and Sign In with Password](/images/mobile/login-screen.jpg)
5. If you don’t have an account, tap **Register**.
6. When you are logged in, you stay on your Dashboard. Log out on any shared device.
