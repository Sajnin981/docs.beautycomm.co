## Sign Up

1. Tap the profile icon, then choose the option to create an account.
2. Enter your details and submit.
3. You stay on your Dashboard.

## Log In

1. Tap the profile icon. The login screen opens.
2. Enter your phone number or email and your password, then tap Log In.
3. You stay on your Dashboard. Use the Home option to go back to the shop.
4. Tap the profile icon again at any time to return to your Dashboard. You don’t need to log in again.
