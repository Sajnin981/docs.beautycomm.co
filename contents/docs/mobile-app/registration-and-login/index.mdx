Create your account or log in to start shopping in the app. To get started:

1. Open the app and tap **Sign Up** on the welcome screen.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Registration &amp; Login screen" label="Phone capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Welcome screen with Sign Up and Login buttons. Suggested filename: mobile/auth/01-welcome.png" caption="mobile/auth/01-welcome.png" />

2. Enter your name, email or phone number and password, then tap **Create Account**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Registration &amp; Login screen" label="Phone capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Sign-up form. Suggested filename: mobile/auth/02-signup.png" caption="mobile/auth/02-signup.png" />

3. Enter the verification code (OTP) sent to you and tap **Verify**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Registration &amp; Login screen" label="Phone capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: OTP screen. Suggested filename: mobile/auth/03-otp.png" caption="mobile/auth/03-otp.png" />

4. To log in later, tap **Login**, enter your details, or choose a social login such as Google or Facebook.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Registration &amp; Login screen" label="Phone capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Login screen with social login buttons. Suggested filename: mobile/auth/04-login.png" caption="mobile/auth/04-login.png" />

5. If you forgot your password, tap **Forgot Password** and follow the code or link sent to you.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Registration &amp; Login screen" label="Phone capture for Registration &amp; Login" description="Capture: Forgot password screen. Suggested filename: mobile/auth/05-forgot-password.png" caption="mobile/auth/05-forgot-password.png" />

If your phone supports it, you may be able to log in with fingerprint or face unlock [confirm if available].
