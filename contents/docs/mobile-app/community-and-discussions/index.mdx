Join the conversation with other customers. To take part:

1. Tap **Community**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Community &amp; Discussions screen" label="Phone capture for Community &amp; Discussions" description="Capture: Community feed. Suggested filename: mobile/community/01-feed.png" caption="mobile/community/01-feed.png" />

2. Tap a post to read replies, and tap **Reply** or **Like**.
3. To start a post, tap the **+** button, add a title and message and tap **Post**.
