## Home Screen

The Home screen is the starting point of the app. Use it to see banners, new arrivals and the main shopping categories.

- Swipe the featured banners to view promotions.
- Tap **Shop** to open the product catalogue.
- Scroll through recommendations and new arrivals.
- Use the bottom navigation or menu to reach the rest of the app.

## Recommendations

BeautyComm suggests products based on what you browse, buy and save.

- Recommended products appear on the Home screen and below product details.
- Sign in for suggestions based on your account history.
- Use your consultation profile for products that fit your skin type and concerns.
- Tap a suggestion to view details, add it to the cart or save it to the wishlist.

## Blogs

The Blogs section shares beauty tips, ingredient guides and how-to articles from the BeautyComm team.

- Select **Blogs** in the menu to see the latest posts.
- Tap a post to read it in full.
- Open products mentioned in an article to view their details.

## Discussions

Discussions let customers ask questions and share experiences with one another.

1. Open **Discussions** and browse topics or search for a subject.
2. Tap a topic to read the conversation.
3. Sign in before posting a reply or creating a topic.
4. Keep posts respectful. The store team can remove inappropriate content.

## Kirei Tube

Kirei Tube is the video corner for tutorials, product demos and beauty routines.

- Open Kirei Tube and choose a video from the list.
- Tap Play and expand to full screen if needed.
- Open related products shown beside or below the video.

## Influencers

The Influencers page collects content from beauty creators who work with BeautyComm.

1. Open Influencers to see featured creators.
2. Tap a creator to view posts, picks and routines.
3. Open a recommended product and add it to the cart or wishlist.
