The home screen is the starting point of the app. To find your way around:

1. Open the app. The home screen shows a search bar, banners, categories and product sections.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Home Screen screen" label="Phone capture for Home Screen" description="Capture: Home screen with numbered callouts on the search bar, banner, categories and product sections. Suggested filename: mobile/home/01-home.png" caption="mobile/home/01-home.png" />

2. Swipe the banner left or right to see current offers, and tap one to open it.
3. Swipe down the page to see recommended products, new arrivals, flash sales and best sellers.
4. Use the **bottom navigation bar** to switch between Home, Categories, [Videos], Cart and Account.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Home Screen screen" label="Phone capture for Home Screen" description="Capture: Bottom navigation bar close-up with each icon labelled. Suggested filename: mobile/home/02-bottom-bar.png" caption="mobile/home/02-bottom-bar.png" />

5. Pull down from the top of the screen to refresh the content.
