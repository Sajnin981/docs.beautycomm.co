Share your opinion after receiving a product. To write a review:

1. Open **My Orders** and tap a delivered order.
2. Tap **Write a Review** next to the product.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Reviews &amp; Ratings screen" label="Phone capture for Product Reviews &amp; Ratings" description="Capture: Order details with the Write a Review button. Suggested filename: mobile/reviews/01-button.png" caption="mobile/reviews/01-button.png" />

3. Choose stars, write your comments and add photos from your camera or gallery.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Reviews &amp; Ratings screen" label="Phone capture for Product Reviews &amp; Ratings" description="Capture: Review form. Suggested filename: mobile/reviews/02-form.png" caption="mobile/reviews/02-form.png" />

4. Tap **Submit**.
