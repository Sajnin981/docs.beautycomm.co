Get help from the app. To contact support:

1. Tap **Account**, then **Help / Support**.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Customer Support screen" label="Phone capture for Customer Support" description="Capture: Support screen. Suggested filename: mobile/support/01-support.png" caption="mobile/support/01-support.png" />

2. Check the FAQs for a quick answer.
3. To reach the team, tap **Contact Us** or **Submit a Ticket**, describe the issue and attach screenshots if useful.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Customer Support screen" label="Phone capture for Customer Support" description="Capture: Ticket form. Suggested filename: mobile/support/02-ticket.png" caption="mobile/support/02-ticket.png" />

Include your order number to get help faster.
