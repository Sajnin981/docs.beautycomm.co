Get help from the app. To contact support:

1. Tap **Account**, then **Help / Support**.

2. Check the FAQs for a quick answer.
3. To reach the team, tap **Contact Us** or **Submit a Ticket**, describe the issue and attach screenshots if useful.

Include your order number to get help faster.
