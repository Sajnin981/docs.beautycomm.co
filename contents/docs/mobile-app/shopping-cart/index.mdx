Review everything before you check out. To use the cart:

1. Tap **Add to Cart** on a product, then tap **Cart** in the bottom bar.

2. Tap **+** or **-** to change quantity. Swipe left on an item, or tap **Remove**, to delete it.
3. Enter a coupon code in the coupon field and tap **Apply**.

4. Tap **Checkout**.
