## Manage Cart

Review and adjust your cart before you pay.

1. Tap the **cart icon** to open your Shopping Cart.
2. Use **+** and **−** to change quantities.
3. Tap the red **trash icon** to remove an item.
4. Check the **Subtotal**.
5. Tap **View Cart** to review, or **Checkout** to continue.
   ![Shopping cart](/images/mobile/cart-manage.jpg)

## Coupons

Apply a discount code while your cart is open.

1. At checkout, tap **Enter coupon code** and type or paste your code.
2. Tap **Apply Coupon**.
   ![Coupon box with Apply Coupon](/images/mobile/checkout-coupon.jpg)
3. Check that the **Discount** line changes.
4. If the code is rejected, check its expiry date and minimum order.
