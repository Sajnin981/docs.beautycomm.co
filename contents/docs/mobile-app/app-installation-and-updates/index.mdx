Install the app once and keep it updated to get the newest features and fixes.

1. Open the Google Play Store on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS.
2. Search for the BeautyComm app by name.
3. Select **Install** on Android or **Get** on iOS and wait for the download.
4. Open the app and allow notifications if you want order updates.
5. Allow camera access if you want to scan authenticity codes or QR marks.
6. When an update is available, return to the app store and select **Update**.

After installation, you can start browsing products, placing orders, tracking deliveries and managing your account.
