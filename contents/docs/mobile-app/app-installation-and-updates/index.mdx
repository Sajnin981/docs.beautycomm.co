Install the app once and keep it updated to get the newest features and fixes.

1. Open Google Play on your phone and search for **BeautyComm**.
2. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
3. Tap **Open** to start the app.
   ![BeautyComm on Google Play](/images/mobile/installation-play-store.jpg)
4. Allow notifications if you want order updates.
5. When an update is available, return to Google Play and tap **Update**.

After installation, you can start browsing products, placing orders, tracking deliveries and managing your account.
