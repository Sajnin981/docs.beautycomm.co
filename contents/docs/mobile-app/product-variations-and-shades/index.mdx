Choose the shade, size or type you want. To select a variation:

1. Open a product and find the **Shade** or **Size** options.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Variations &amp; Shades screen" label="Phone capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Capture: Shade swatches on the product screen. Suggested filename: mobile/variations/01-swatches.png" caption="mobile/variations/01-swatches.png" />

2. Tap an option. The image, price and stock status update.
3. If an option is greyed out or marked **Out of Stock**, choose another.

   <Screenshot width={300} alt="BeautyComm mobile Product Variations &amp; Shades screen" label="Phone capture for Product Variations &amp; Shades" description="Capture: Out-of-stock option shown as unavailable. Suggested filename: mobile/variations/02-out-of-stock.png" caption="mobile/variations/02-out-of-stock.png" />

4. Tap **Add to Cart** with your selection.
